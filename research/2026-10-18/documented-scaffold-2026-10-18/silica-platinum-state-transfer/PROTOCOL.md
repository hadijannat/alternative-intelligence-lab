## Eligibility questions

1. Exact parent/intermediate/recipient identities, retained material, feed, reduction/etching sequence, geometry, timing, specimen/replicate links and apparatus/organic dependencies.
2. Whether Au founders are quantitatively bounded after removal and in the functional Pt recipients. Microscopy non-detection and disappearance of a pre-growth signal are not automatically calibrated mass/function bounds.
3. Whether newly grown isolated Pt recipients carry a useful state/function associated with opposite parent variants under matched feed, with no-parent, empty-template, residual-founder and state-erased/cue controls.
4. Whether post-growth optical or other function can be explained by residual Au, Au-Pt interactions, silica/ligands or matrix artifacts. A linear founder-signal bound requires experimentally justified additivity/competence under the recipient assay; do not assume it.
5. Whether any recipient is used as a parent in a subsequent controlled functional transfer. Template casting and one parent-to-recipient transformation do not establish serial inheritance, variant selection or maintained integration.
