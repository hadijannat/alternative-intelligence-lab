# A documented inorganic optical-state transfer, with H still open

The newly acquired Luan2025 source package supports a component result: prepared opposite Au shapes can be carried through silica molds into Pt helicoids with opposite measured optical responses after the reported Au and silica removal steps. This is a useful addition to the state-carrier evidence. It does not close founder-bounded, serial functional inheritance or the full maintained-organization goal. Zero verified integrated complements are added.

The native article,36-page SI,37-sheet source workbook and two-page review file were verified against their acquisition receipt and archive members. Six relevant SI pages were rendered and visually read. `SOURCE_AUDIT.md` records the preparation sequence, controls, organic dependencies, simulation/experiment split and unresolved Pt/Pd caption discrepancy. These are literature observations, not a physical replication by this project.

## Eligibility decision

The source does not qualify a numerical founder-bound test under `PROTOCOL.md`. It lacks a calibrated residual-material/function bound for the optical recipients and a validated response law for the possible residual Au/Pt/ligand/matrix combination. Pt seeding begins before Au removal. Precursor amounts cannot identify the newly deposited fraction, and geometrical “purity” cannot substitute for chemical composition. Similar spectra of Au-retained and Au-removed products strengthen a shape interpretation but do not quantify each rival contribution.

No copying rate, functional amplification factor, founder-excess significance or model selection was fitted. Zero numerical response points were selected for analysis. Published optical magnitude/sign, morphology and visually inspected spectral controls were consumed; this is not a blinded validation. A simulation of ideal metal shapes cannot supply the missing impurity or lineage measurements.
