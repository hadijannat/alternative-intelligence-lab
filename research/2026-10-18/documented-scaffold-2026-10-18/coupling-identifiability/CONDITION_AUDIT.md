# GTM-4 preparation and measurement linkage audit

Date: 2026-10-18. Scope: the acquired primary article DOI [10.1021/jacs.5c12567](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.5c12567), its native main PDF/XML, and its single SI PDF. This is a condition audit, not a new fit or a global claim about unavailable data.

## Decision

Figure 1 and Figure S3 describe the same named catalyst and catalytic protocol sufficiently to compare their conversion-coordinate observables. The package does **not** provide an explicit aliquot/run/batch identifier joining those observations, a published conversion-to-clocktime map, or simultaneous Ge-state and product measurements. The structural experiments support the proposed chemistry qualitatively but use different preparation histories and media. They cannot currently identify an activation rate, water transport coefficient, or humidity-dependent loss of catalytic function for the liquid catalytic experiment. Those parameters remain unmeasured, not zero.

The strongest negative coupling result is that ambient-air XRD persistence is observed after 115 C butanol treatment, whereas the article reports catalytic inactivity following 120 C preactivation. These are distinct preparations, so this is a warning against equating structural persistence with catalytic competence, not a paired quantitative causal estimate.

## Public dataset linkage within this package

Direct XML inspection finds two supplementary-material entries referring to the same `ja5c12567_si_001.pdf`. The “Associated Data” section supplies supplementary material, with no separate raw-data accession. The downloaded supplementary archive has 19 members: that PDF plus 18 article figure GIF/JPG images. The main article identifies Diamond proposal SP38597-1, but supplies no raw XAS dataset accession or link tying beamline timestamps to the catalytic aliquots. There is no linked CSV, HPLC chromatogram set, raw FTIR/XAS series, measured water-uptake transient, or sample-linked structure/function time series in the inspected package. This does not rule out unpublished records or another repository entry.

## Consequence for the goal

Retain the source-supported qualitative sequence of butanol adsorption, altered Ge coordination, and higher-temperature dehydration/butoxide formation as mechanistic hypotheses with experimental structural support. A new coupled model may state this topology, but its activation, deactivation, adsorption/desorption and water-transport parameters cannot be treated as calibrated by these heterogeneous observations. Product-balance checks in conversion coordinates remain possible without assigning those rates.

Butanol is an active ligand/reactant/solvent dependency here, not merely a fabrication residue. That dependency must accompany the component claim. The original protocol requires noncarbon-based active organization; it does not impose a universal zero-carbon rule on all feeds, measurement apparatus or fabrication. This audit neither admits GTM/butanol as a fully noncarbon integrated complement nor proves that every architecture using it is impossible. PMo12 and Mo34WV were absent from these experiments.
