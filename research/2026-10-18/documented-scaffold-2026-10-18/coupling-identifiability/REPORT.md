## Why another rate fit would not resolve the missing evidence

The published catalytic observations use total conversion as their horizontal coordinate. For a kinetic system `dz/dt = k*f(z)`, replacing `k` by `lambda*k` and time by `t/lambda` leaves the trajectory `z` against conversion unchanged. More generally, a common scaling of reaction rates cannot be recovered without an observed time scale. Thus the earlier apparent ratio 3.93837 remains a dimensionless source descriptor; it is not a turnover rate, Ge activation rate, water permeability or repair rate.

Relative rate combinations could be estimated under an additional specified network, but that conditional fit would not establish clock-time transport or repair. Independent clock information from a different preparation does not break the scaling symmetry for the catalytic specimen. In addition, the primary XAS discussion explicitly declines reliable quantitative coordination-number fitting because responsive and unresponsive Ge environments contribute to an averaged signal. Assigning all average signal change to the fraction of active sites would create an unmeasured calibration. Product-family ee is also normalized and cannot alone establish absolute competent-material yield.
