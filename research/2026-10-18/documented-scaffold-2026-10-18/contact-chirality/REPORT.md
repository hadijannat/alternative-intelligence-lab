# Contact-triggered crystal-state transfer remains a proposal for inheritance

Niinomi et al. report [directed chirality transfer through contact-induced polymorphic transformation](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.cgd.0c00693) in sodium chlorate. This is a distinct possible state-writing mechanism: an initially separate recipient crystal can change state without being assembled entirely from the donor's material. It could guide a functional-inheritance experiment. The acquired evidence does not establish an isolated recipient's inherited function, serial transmission or an integrated complement to Mo34WV.

The [source audit](SOURCE_AUDIT.md) and [acquisition record](ACQUISITION.md) document a verified13-page SI plus native Movies SI7 and SI8. File sizes and deposited MD5 values agree, and SHA256 checks pass. Root also inspected the normal publisher browser page: the abstract and SI are accessible, while full main text explicitly requires access. No purchase or login was attempted. Methods and controls not present in the acquired SI therefore remain unverified, rather than assumed absent from the whole study.

The publisher describes plasmonically guided contact between a chiral crystal and an achiral metastable crystal on an engineered substrate. The native SI captions identify the two movies. One-frame-per-second contact sheets show distinguishable bodies approaching, connecting and changing visible appearance near the focal region. Root and an independent reviewer inspected the MovieSI8 sheet. This is not calibrated optical rotation or an independently measured handedness sign. Sampled frames also cannot prove that an unobserved event never occurred elsewhere in a movie.

The missing distinctions are causal and functional. The acquired observations do not verify reciprocal left/right donor controls under common conditions, a quantified donor contribution in the recipient, a donor-removed recipient assay, persistence of its optical function after separation, or recipient-to-recipient serial transfer. A transformation front or crystal resemblance does not supply a copying probability or establish useful heredity. No numerical inheritance fit is justified from selected movies.

NaClO3 and Au are inorganic, but laser positioning/heating, the fabricated substrate and evaporation are dependencies of this demonstration. External drive is permitted by the full-goal contract; it must still be counted and separated from autonomous material action. The complete fabrication/material inventory is not verified without main methods. SI thermal assumptions and stress/energy estimates are not validated or used to infer transfer kinetics here.

The prior2021 sonication and2019 initial-breeding negatives remain intact. This contact-transformation proposal does not replace their founder-bound results. The additional2023 Mueller-polarimetry lead has no acquired dataset; its data statement requires a request, and no author contact was made.

Disposition: retain **contact-directed recipient-state writing** as a component research proposal, with no function credited beyond the source's reported transformation. The next decisive test is recipient-resolved optical function before/after donor removal, opposite donor states and no-contact controls, followed by serial transfer under a shared drive/feed. Until then H/V/J remain unclosed. Zero verified integrated complements are added, and the full original objective remains unachieved.
