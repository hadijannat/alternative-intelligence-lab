# Competent-organization renewal and retained-site reactivation

Date: 2026-10-18. This eligibility protocol is written before extracting response points from a new source in this iteration. It does not alter the original I/E/T/B/R/H/V/S/M/A/J/C contract or any prior frozen protocol/hash.

## Question and prior exposure

The original R requirement asks whether new competent material restores measured function after a specified perturbation, with feed and founders accounted. It does not require every catalytic turnover to be assigned exclusively to an incoming atom. The prior `origin-function-followup/PROTOCOL.md` tests the stronger origin-resolved attribution route. Its sufficient measurements must not become the sole definition of component-level restoration of competent organization.

Those observations are not blinded holdouts. No new response points have been selected or fitted here.

## New-source audit

Seek a selective active-material removal/deactivation and precursor-addition rescue under matched post-assay conditions. Record exact preparation, perturbation, supplied species, retained material, structural/compositional measurements, input/effluent, function, specimen/replicate linkage, uncertainty and organic/apparatus dependencies. A source that only adds Fe to an undamaged film remains activation evidence.

For a damage x precursor factorial, a useful descriptive estimand is:

`(Y_damage,feed - Y_damage,vehicle) - (Y_sham,feed - Y_sham,vehicle)`

All four arms need matched wash, electrolyte, potential history and resource accounting. This interaction tests a damage-specific rescue; it does not on its own demonstrate new material or exclude deblocking. Do not turn an unmeasured arm into zero, infer missing uncertainty or fit a copying/repair rate from this expression.

To support component R, the evidence must additionally attribute recovery to reconstitution of the claimed competent assembly from supplied material, while accounting for retained founders. Suitable evidence can combine calibrated material depletion/reconstitution with functional add/remove/rescue controls; a validated alternative route is allowed. Isotope labels or isolated-new-atom turnover are not mandatory if a different quantitative causal/material bound resolves the rivals.

Explicit rivals are retained-site deblocking, redox reactivation, survival, solution/bubble changes, altered transport, and importing fresh externally prepared catalyst. A common-medium post-assay, matched no-precursor regeneration arm, bare-support+feed arm and fresh-catalyst comparator address different rivals; source missing controls remain missing. The original R requirement is not waived because a curve recovers.

## Decisions and checks

1. Verify native primary source identity, access/license and hashes; preserve failed routes.
2. Decide which contrasts and measured quantities actually exist before numerical response extraction.
3. If the source identifies a useful limited model, write a separate source-specific model/baseline/uncertainty/holdout protocol first. Otherwise leave parameters unidentified and record the exact failed claim.
4. Keep component-supported, proposed, rejected and unresolved conclusions separate. No repair result automatically closes H, V, J or C. Coupling to PMo12 or Mo34WV needs its own characterized joint operation, potentially including separately accounted compartments.

No numerical pass threshold or prospective physical validation is claimed by this eligibility protocol. The full goal remains unachieved; integrated complements added remain zero.
