# Primary evidence and identity

Selected scaffold: phosphomolybdic acid H3PMo12O40, with Keggin phosphomolybdate framework [PMo12O40]3-. The operational aqueous forms in the selected assay are primarily Ox = [H2PMo12O40]- and Red = [H4PMo12O40]- at pH about 0.3, with hydronium counterions. These are the source's operational assignments, not a complete protonation microstate calculation. Mo34WV is not assigned any of these properties.

[Symes & Cronin 2013, Nature Chemistry 5, 403–409](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchem.1621) provides the co-measured functions: externally driven electron/proton storage, later hydrogen evolution, membrane-mediated separation of stages, and electrode transport limitations. Main article p404, equations 3–4 and speciation discussion; Figures 4–5 and pp406–408 for electrode geometry, gas analysis, stability and efficiency. The source is experimental evidence; this branch's simulations are reanalysis and conditional scenarios.

## Why this alternative

Mo36 has documented assembly data ([Miras et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1921536117)), but repeated local data use and missing final-dose conversions prevent calling another split fresh physical validation. PMo12 assembly data in that separate work are too fast/under-specified for a reliable coupled renewal model; they are not combined with the acidic electrolysis assay.

Dawson Li6[P2W18O62] has documented charge storage ([Chen et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.1c10584)), with concentration/counterion effects and dilution-induced hydrogen release. Its capacity and kinetics are not substituted into PMo12. W72V30 stability spectra similarly do not identify this assay's active fraction or transport. Choosing PMo12 reduces the number of invented inputs; it does not establish a route to life.
