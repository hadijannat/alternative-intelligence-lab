Date: 2026-10-18. Scope: primary observations of inorganic new-material function with a quantitative bound on retained-founder contribution, preferably shared feed and serial functional variants. This is a bounded search, not evidence of impossibility. No candidate is admitted as a verified inheritance complement; no copying rate is fitted.

## 1. Timed opposing seed/cue experiment in luminescent terbium phosphate

Schwartz, Hananel and Markovich, *Circularly polarized and total luminescence as probes of nucleation and growth in chiral nanocrystals*, Chirality 35, 104-109 (2023), [DOI 10.1002/chir.23523](https://doi.org/10.1002/chir.23523). [Primary full text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10108007/fullTextXML) acquired as `sources/inheritance/PMC10108007.xml`; derived body text and byte receipt accompany it.

This is the strongest new inheritance-oriented lead in this search. It measures total and circularly polarized Eu luminescence over clock time during growth of Eu-doped TbPO4 nanocrystals. The experiment actually uses D2O to avoid H2O emission quenching; Tb:Eu is 95:5, pH approximately 2, temperature 50 C, and tartaric acid:lanthanide ratio 1:1. Purified L-prepared seeds, 20 microlitres out of 2 mL (nominally 1% synthesis quantity), are added to either L- or D-tartaric-acid feed. L/D labels denote preparation, not independently assigned absolute crystal handedness.

The source reports opposing-cue behavior: L seeds introduced into D feed at time zero first produce negative CPL, followed by movement toward the D direction after roughly 30 minutes. The final source estimate is about 20% D ee. Introducing the L seeds after 26 minutes instead gives about 92% D ee. Matched L seed/L cue gives a high L response. Figures 1-3 contain the timed measurements and identify normalization. Thus seed history and addition time affect a measured inorganic optical function even against a fresh opposing cue.

The authors interpret the early-seed effect as secondary nucleation preserving handedness, and argue that it cannot be entirely explained by seed growth. Preserve that positive inference, but also its limits: the acquired article supplies no independently measured maximum founder growth/emission contribution, no isolated-daughter functional assay, and no serial descendant transfer or alternative stable functional lineage under a common achiral feed. The nominal 1% seed quantity is not a 1% final photon-flux bound. Luminescence and CPL are useful observables, but the source does not demonstrate their coupling to maintained chemical work. Tartaric acid remains an active organic directing reagent.

The source explicitly reports uncertainty in later-time dissymmetry due to scattering and therefore presents subsequent CPL plots qualitatively. Do not convert normalized CPL curves directly to exact population ee or time-dependent inheritance rates. Numerical raw data are stated to be available from the authors upon request; no request was sent. The directly available dataset is published figure data plus source-reported endpoints, not an acquired raw spreadsheet.

**Decision:** retain as a new, useful counterseed/cue mechanism dataset; functional inheritance beyond retained founders remains unresolved. It could constrain a competition-between-seeding-and-new-cue model if figure extraction and observation calibration are declared, but cannot validate a copying kernel.

## 2. New catalytic aluminosilicate with sacrificed silicalite seeds

*Facile Morphology and Porosity Regulation of Zeolite ZSM-5 Mesocrystals with Synergistically Enhanced Catalytic Activity and Shape Selectivity*, Nanomaterials 12, 1601 (2022), [DOI 10.3390/nano12091601](https://doi.org/10.3390/nano12091601). [Primary full text XML](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9105084/fullTextXML) acquired as `sources/inheritance/PMC9105084.xml` with derived text and receipt.

The source uses approximately 250 nm silicalite-1 seeds, typically 7 wt% of total starting silica, in Na/K-mediated aluminosilicate growth. Its etching/TEM evidence supports different seed fates: K conditions dissolve/sacrifice seed structure, whereas Na conditions preserve a core. The unseeded controls remain partly amorphous after 72 h; seeded crystallization is faster. Actual product function is measured: o-xylene conversion/selectivity/deactivation at 673 K (Figure 6) and p/o-xylene adsorption at 298 K (Figure 7). These are stronger than a structure-only endpoint.

This is not transmission of an already measured parent functional variant: the feed changes composition to an aluminosilicate acid catalyst, Na/K changes the environment, and no serial variant-lineage comparison appears. The seed suspension is directly used without further treatment; product H-form preparation includes three NH4NO3 exchanges and 550 C calcination. A statement that no additional organic template is added to growth must not be expanded into a proven organic-free seed preparation. Morphological seed dissolution does not establish an isotope/material founder bound. This is synthesis of useful new catalyst, not damage-triggered autonomous repair.

**Decision:** retain as a fresh competent-material/transport-access comparator, not functional inheritance or integrated PMo12 repair. The article lists a supplementary ZIP, but this search did not acquire it or extract curve points. No new model is fitted from the publication-level screen.
