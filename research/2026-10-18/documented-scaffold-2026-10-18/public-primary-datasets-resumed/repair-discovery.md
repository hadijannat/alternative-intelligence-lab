### Methods and control linkage

Main Methods, “Self-healing electrode,” specifies 0.2 g Bi2O3 and 4.5 g Ga for cyclic regeneration at approximately 30 °C. The electrode is removed from electrolyte, mechanically stirred to redisperse deactivated surface material, rested for another 30 minutes, and subjected to triangle-potential scanning from -0.6 to -1.2 V versus RHE. **Electrolyte is renewed each cycle.** Consequently refresh, stirring/redispersion, rest, and reducing potential are bundled interventions.
