# New repair data tested; serial functional inheritance remains unresolved

The resumed public-data search found a usable native repair dataset and a new source linking seed-produced inorganic crystals to an actual adsorption function. The repair calculation confirms recorded recovery between source-defined stages alongside renewed within-cycle deterioration. It also exposed a mistake in the initial FE stage pairing, which is preserved and corrected explicitly below. No public dataset in this bounded search establishes founder-bounded serial functional inheritance. All twelve integrated requirements remain unclosed.

## Public data and material roles

[The dataset registry](<DATASET_REGISTRY.md>) records eight exact primary source/deposit records, access classes, licenses and unacquired files. The three discovery notes preserve repository-led searches: 18 repair queries, 25 solid-inheritance queries plus targeted source-chain follow-ups, and 19 dynamic-inheritance queries. They are a bounded search, not an exhaustive proof of literature absence. Root also checked the exact 2019 PNAS source chain and current native routes. Duplicate/historical Fe datasets were not recounted as fresh.

**Repair:** [Hou et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-35416-w), Bi-containing Ga liquid-metal electrode, supplies a 29.7 MB native source workbook, 21-page SI and captured public main. Seventeen integrity/identity checks pass; all 24 workbook sheets are inventoried without initial response selection. The source reports phase conversion from metallic Bi to Bi2O2CO3 during CO2 reduction, poorer function of the carbonate-phase comparator and regenerated Bi during externally driven redispersion/reduction. This is new source-level reconstruction/function evidence, not a new authorized replacement for PMo12 or an integrated member.

The main methods use 0.5 M KHCO3, approximately 30 °C, CO2 feed and external electrochemical drive. Electrode removal, mechanical stirring, rest, reducing TPS and electrolyte renewal are bundled. Initial mixing uses 0.2 g Bi2O3/4 g Ga; cyclic regeneration states 0.2 g/4.5 g. PLA support and Nafion separator remain recorded organic apparatus/structural dependencies. Complete Bi/Ga inventories, cycle-resolved losses and matched damaged refresh-only/stir-only controls are unavailable. Phase and functional panels do not expose per-specimen material/function identifiers or independent batch uncertainty. These gaps prevent isolated mechanistic or full R/J attribution.

**Inheritance:** [Idrees et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.4c16883), TbPO4·H2O nanocrystals, explicitly assays enantioselective adsorption on purified material from a seeded synthesis. Root acquired the exact main XML and [public Figshare registry](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/28823413). The main uses approximately 1% synthesis-volume seed aliquot for a new synthesis without added TA, then purification and adsorption. This is a genuine preparation-to-function link, stronger than merely calling a structure inherited.

However, the aliquot fraction is not an independently calibrated founder mass/surface/adsorption-capacity bound. The main estimates residual TA after purification below 10 µM; that organic-contaminant estimate does not bound surviving seed function. No isolated founder-only adsorption assay, controlled serial recipient-as-parent series or inherited-variant selection is established. Native SI was transiently read in scout memory; root's local attempts retained 500/202 error bodies, not a PDF. No numerical inheritance test is justified. Organic adsorbates/feed are not automatically disqualifying; residual/director and structural dependence must be measured, not assumed absent.

Other concrete deposits are weaker for the requested gap: Dryad's serial-transfer LCMS archive concerns an organic chemical ecosystem and composition; Zenodo 14736840 has restricted files; Zenodo 10560157 is simulation code. McGlothin's time-lapse includes actual offspring generations but no measured serial inherited functional variants/founder exclusion. The registry keeps these distinctions rather than turning every deposit into a usable inheritance test.

## New native-data calculation and the FE pairing mistake

The [frozen test](<TEST_PROTOCOL.md>) and preexecution clarification preceded numeric current/FE extraction. Its mapping and limits were independently approved. Fifteen synthetic checks pass. Every attempted record remains: three FE label pairs and eleven current traces, no exclusion or fitted rate. Published qualitative outcomes and SI curves were already seen; this is retrospective, with no blinded or held-out validation.

The literal native same-index labels yielded ratios 0.84855, 0.84481, 0.84521. Root initially interpreted these as healed/original recovery comparisons. After execution, the actual publisher Figure 5e was acquired and independently viewed: the high bars denote cycle starts, low bars denote deactivation after 10 h, and the recovery arrows connect each low bar to the next cycle start. Thus the frozen ratios measure within-cycle FE retention; interpreting them as repair failure was wrong. `results/hou-test.json`, its false `nominal_recovery` flags and original hashes remain unchanged. [The source-stage amendment](<STAGE_MAPPING_AMENDMENT.md>) saves the correction and consumed-outcome timing.
