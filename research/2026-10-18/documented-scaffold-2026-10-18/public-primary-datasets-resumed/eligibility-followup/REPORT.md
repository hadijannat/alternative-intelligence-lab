## Inheritance: inventory is not functional capacity

Three distinctions decide eligibility:

- PDF page 9 uses acid-only titration references to quantify depletion from solution. Those references do not measure the adsorption capacity of the surviving seeds alone.
- PDF page 13 states the ideal Langmuir assumptions. Page 10 describes continuing uptake without demonstrated saturation over its tested range and possible intermolecular stabilization. The main article states that the extended competitive Langmuir treatment fails for racemate adsorption. Fitting the descendant-batch response therefore does not independently calibrate founder capacity.
