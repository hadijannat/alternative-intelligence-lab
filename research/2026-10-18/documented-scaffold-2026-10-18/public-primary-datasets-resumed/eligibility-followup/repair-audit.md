## Treatment, material accounting and full-goal implications

The Methods self-healing procedure removes the electrode, physically redistributes the deactivated surface material into Ga, rests it, applies reducing TPS and changes electrolyte each cycle. The paper describes renewed Bi segregation, not an explicit addition of ready-made fresh Bi catalyst at each cycle. Do not convert the term “fresh” in that description into an undocumented external-catalyst addition. Conversely, there is no matched fresh-catalyst replacement arm or quantified cycle-resolved inventory demonstrating what fraction of competent organization came from damaged material versus the retained reservoir. Initial preparation and cyclic-regeneration recipes specify different Ga amounts (4 g versus 4.5 g); they cannot be silently pooled.

The decisive missing observations are a linked damaged-electrode reconstruction versus matched refresh/handling/redox comparator, a common-medium product assay, and accounted material/organization reconstitution with a selective discriminator of the restored assembly. Suitable causal intervention designs need not assign every catalytic turnover to newly incoming atoms; retained atoms can participate in genuinely rebuilt organization. Here the issue is the unavailable control/linkage/accounting information, not an atom-by-atom exclusivity requirement.

No numerical analysis of the unexamined phase or impedance curves can supply those absent interventions. A future descriptive phase-presence audit is possible after a separate frozen protocol, but would refine an already consumed structural observation rather than resolve the causal R gap. No new numerical R/J test is recommended from this package alone.

The Bi/Ga system also remains separate from the PMo12 target and its operating conditions. CO2/bicarbonate feed, electrical/mechanical handling, thermal conditions, PLA support and Nafion separator are dependencies requiring accounting. These records contain no same-system PMo12 integration, maintained boundary renewal, founder-bounded serial descendants, inherited state selection or causal adaptive behavior. J and the other integrated requirements remain unclosed even if a future stronger source-level R demonstration becomes available.
