# Native extraction and model execution

The source inventory is `file_manifest.json`. `analyze.py` verifies the SHA256 of every OER, regeneration and processed file it uses. All numerical parsing uses native `T` seconds, `Vf` V versus the instrument reference, and `Im` A. Other native columns and their unit labels remain in the result metadata; a literal `...........` status field is not a numerical measurement. The misnamed WNi79T `.xls` is parsed as Gamry text. Native row widths, table row counts, finite selected values and nondecreasing times are checked. No RHE conversion, resistance subtraction, iron-dose assignment or transfer from Han is made.

There are420 main summaries:140 training (14 per specimen) and280 reserved. Each is the median native potential in the final300s of a complete6000s OER interval. Loop records are6000s each; continuous traces are divided into complete intervals, retaining incomplete tail duration in metadata. Model evaluation uses the median sample time, while training/reserved allocation uses interval-end OER exposure. The0.5–1h anchor median is represented at its median time0.75h, with competence1 at that reference. This is an effective conditional observation law, not an atom-inventory calibration.

Five loop specimens include native regeneration records. Nonnegative native pulse durations are inserted at their OER exposure locations; negative pre-step samples are excluded. Terminal pulses are retained as metadata but affect no earlier OER point. The WNi78T last pulse has only6s recorded. Wall gaps are separately recorded and omitted from the chemical evolution, since their phases are unknown. Total unassigned gaps between OER blocks are1285s for WNi176T-A andWNi99T,676s for WNi198T-A,1193s for WNi225T-A and694s for WNi78T. No missing block is reconstructed.

OER current medians are about0.057A for eight specimens and0.076A for the two Fig6b stress specimens. After the initial native time-zero sample, the maximum deviation from each record's median is below0.015%. Time-zero current values are preserved separately because the instrument begins from its previous state. Native header AREA=1 does not enter analysis. Models are fitted separately to each specimen; different current/geometry cases never share fitted rates.

The WNi78T `final`24h record follows an approximately3h unknown gap, an incomplete regeneration pulse and a separate EIS measurement. The metadata-only amendment therefore treats it as a separate followup:14 full summaries and2400s partial tail, no fit or reserved validation. It is never silently concatenated into the43-loop history.

Processed/native provenance uses acquisition-row pairing rather than interpolating potentials or assigning feeds. Overlapping voltage scales are999.965–1000.044 processed mV per raw V, with fitted offsets−0.057 to+0.033mV and maximum affine residual≤0.516mV. The five processed clocks differ from native cumulative OER times by up to0.00810h; fitted clock scales and residuals are retained. WNi225T-A has8040 acquired native rows versus8643 processed rows:603 unsupported tail rows beginning66.674h. Twelve processed tail rows contain a time and missing voltage, at original lines8239–8242,8440–8443,8641–8644. These are preserved as missing; all overlapping paired rows are finite. Processed tails supply no model observations.

The fixed grids were executed for M0/M1 and all three slopes. Both models pass4/10 reserved screens at each slope; the flat last-training-value baseline passes7/10. At primary b=.040V, WNi99T M1 improves reserved RMSE from16.45 to12.37mV relative to M0, while the flat baseline has12.12mV RMSE. Many effective parameters lie at boundaries or have large near-optimal sets. Positive regeneration rates selected at nearly zero activity gain cannot establish physical restoration. All60 fitted results, interval residuals, screens and parameter ambiguity diagnostics remain in `results/analysis.json`.

The origin witness uses identical initial inventories: one founder unit active, ten new units in liquid. Retained-founder activation exchanges active/buried founder material. Replacement exchanges active/liquid material and proportionally redeposits available old/new liquid material. Both conserve each origin's elemental inventory and produce the same total competence. After12 synthetic cycles the retained case has0.741368 active founder units; the replacement case has0.087546 founder plus0.653822 new active units. Voltage differs by at most1.74e−17V. This is a structural counterexample, not measured repair, a physical Fe inventory, or evidence that replacement occurs in these specimens.

Selfchecks cover native assumptions, a known synthetic OER/pulse aggregation, exact phase updates, no-loss/no-restoration limits, positivity and competence conservation, exact synthetic grid-parameter recovery, M0 nesting, nonpulse equality, and origin-specific elemental conservation. Only training arrays are passed to the fitting function; reserved values enter metrics after parameter selection. Parser and serialization failures are retained in `execution_failures.txt` with their corrections. They caused no changes to model ranges, data splits, screen thresholds or scientific negative results.
