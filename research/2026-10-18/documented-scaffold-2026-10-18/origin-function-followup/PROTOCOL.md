## Candidate test

Record the exact material, preparation, medium, drive/reference, loading, support, geometry, time, specimen/replicate linkage, measured state, independently measured function, and apparatus/organic dependencies. Mark missing information explicitly. A source may support a component without qualifying for a model or the integrated objective.

An R dataset qualifies for origin-resolved functional attribution only if it supplies:

1. A material-origin measurement distinguishing founders from incoming material, with uncertainty and an explicit sampling/normalization model.
2. Function on linked specimens under declared matched conditions, rather than a current-derived site count treated as independent evidence of current.
3. Regional/site competence or a quantitative maximum founder contribution, or a direct isolated-new-material functional assay. Bulk ancestry and total loading alone do not identify regional functional origin in general.
4. A perturbation and relevant survival/reactivation, fresh-supply and input controls sufficient to test the particular repair claim. Material exchange alone is not restored organization.

An H dataset additionally needs new recipient function beyond a quantitative founder/cross-talk bound, controlled shared feed, state-erased/unseeded rivals and serial transmission of useful variants. Structural copying or isolated optical/catalytic function alone remains component evidence.

## Analysis decision

Before extracting response numbers, write a claim-specific decision against these observations. If parameters remain unidentified, do not fit chemical rates, add free loss terms, digitize an easy performance curve or count an integrated complement. Record the failed claim and the next discriminator.

If a dataset qualifies, freeze a minimal chemistry-grounded observation model, conventional matched baselines, specimen/entire-condition/time holdout, uncertainty propagation and failure criteria in a separate protocol before extraction. Disclose already seen published outcomes. No consumed holdout may be moved or reused as new validation. The present eligibility protocol does not preregister an unspecified model or success threshold.

No numerical model is required to conclude that the acquired evidence cannot identify the requested quantity. Such a conclusion is limited to the audited sources; it does not prove that no relevant evidence exists anywhere.
