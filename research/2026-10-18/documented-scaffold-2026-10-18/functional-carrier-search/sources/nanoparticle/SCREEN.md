# Inorganic new-material function screen

Date: 2026-10-18. Read-only research subtask. Two primary experiments were screened. No candidate qualifies as demonstrated functional inheritance; no kinetic parameters were fitted or invented.

## Strongest distinct negative: AuPd photocatalysts produce Au products

Sanchez-Iglesias et al., Nanoscale 13 (2021), 15188-15192. DOI: [10.1039/D1NR04400A](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2021/nr/d1nr04400a).

The primary article couples AuPd-mediated NADH regeneration to gold nucleation in three light/dark cycles. It reports estimated products per founder rod of 100, 133 and 117. New gold spheres lack the Pd component needed for the efficient light reaction. The authors explicitly identify the missing positive feedback from product particles to subsequent photocatalysis. These are repeated uses of the retained parent bed, not three daughter generations. The system uses NAD, agarose and formate, so it also fails a strictly carbon-free implementation criterion. This is useful evidence that production of abundant new inorganic material does not establish inheritance of its parent's measured function.

## Supporting-information control and data boundary

[Primary SI, S16-S17](https://www.rsc.org/suppdata/d1/nr/d1nr04400a/d1nr04400a1.pdf), DOI above. Web PDF open returned 17 pages and extracted lines 0-250.

S16 Figure S13 reports approximately 2% NADH formation with bare Au nanorods, without Pd. This is a different geometry from the small product spheres and a different operating condition from the three-cycle experiment: 90-minute residence, 200 mW/cm2, versus 30 minutes and 300 mW/cm2. Therefore neither exactly zero daughter activity nor a quantitative daughter activity fraction is established.

S16-S17 estimates founders as 6e12 rods; product counts are 6e14, 8e14 and 7e14. Atomic gold concentrations are 0.00010, 0.00007 and 0.00003 M; corresponding TEM diameters are 2.5 +/- 0.5, 2.1 +/- 0.5 and 1.8 +/- 0.4 nm. Turnovers assume complete founder immobilization and no leakage. These are model-derived particle counts, not direct counts. The source supports a missing-feedback finding; it does not supply a measured multigeneration inheritance kernel.

## Growth-associated photocatalysis: anatase TiO2

Liu et al., JACS 137 (2015), 11327-11339. DOI: [10.1021/jacs.5b04676](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/jacs.5b04676).

The paper grows nanorod antennas on shaped anatase seeds and measures RhB photocatalytic degradation. Figure 10 compares original plates/TOB seeds, grown brushes/brooms, rods and commercial P25. Grown structures improve activity; feed rate also changes performance. Figure 11 compares surface area and apparent rate constants. These are useful growth/function controls. However, the core remains attached, no detached offspring are functionally assayed, no founder-contribution bound is reported in the inspected contents, and serial transfer of parent-dependent functional variants is absent. Repeated catalytic-use tests are not reproduction generations. Thus this source is a growth-to-function lead, not qualifying inheritance evidence.
