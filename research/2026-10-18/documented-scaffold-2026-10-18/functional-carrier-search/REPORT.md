The closest new functional-state candidate is **GTM-3**, a chiral germanosilicate framework with measured enantioselective catalysis after its organic directing agent is removed. This is a stronger function-carrier example than Beta's bulk composition. The acquired evidence still does not establish transmission of that useful state into new material. Two further candidates clarify why material proliferation and physical repair cannot substitute for functional inheritance.

## GTM-3: functional variants and a missing experiment

[Primary study, DOI10.1021/jacs.2c01874](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.2c01874). Complete primary XML and its24-page supplement were acquired through EuropePMC, with exact DOI and XML-linked ZIP-member checks. Source table/cell text is retained rather than reconstructed from an image.

Opposite chiral EMPS preparations produce frameworks with opposite catalytic biases. For trans-stilbene oxide ring opening with1-hexanol at30C, Al-free SS-GTM-3 gives inversion-product ee of−28.8% at50.7% conversion and−28.5% at99.4%. RR-GTM-3 gives+28.8% at60.2% conversion and+30.3% at99.5%. These are source observations, not new simulations. Catalysts are calcined at550C for12h; as-made template-containing material and catalyst-free controls lack activity in the reported assay. Thus the source supports a useful function of the prepared inorganic framework, beyond a growth or shape descriptor.

Absolute framework enantiopurity was not resolved because of beam sensitivity. The source infers enantioenrichment through several observations, including crossed seeding experiments: SS/RR seeds combined with SS/RR directing agent, at5% and10% loading, show greater crystallization progress when seed and directing agent match. This preserves genuine seed recognition. It does not provide catalytic ee for each crossed offspring batch or isolate new material from founders. Ge content and preparation also differ between the growth and catalyst assays.

The executed **identifiability test** enumerates eight source growth conditions. Two candidate explanations agree with the observed categories:

| Explanation | Growth response | Functional state of newly grown material |
|---|---|---|
| Parent priority | Matching seed/cue aids crystallization | Follows parent when a seed is present |
| Cue priority | Matching seed/cue aids crystallization | Follows the fresh chiral directing agent |

They disagree in the four mismatched conditions. **Zero identified offspring-function measurements distinguish those predictions.** Separately prepared catalyst signs are consistent with both. Functional sign here means the sign of **inversion-product ee**; retention-product signs are opposite, and SS/RR preparation labels do not assign absolute framework configuration. These are counterfactual witnesses to missing information, not fitted chemical mechanisms or validated chirality predictions. No copying probability, new-material rate or positive inheritance outcome was invented.

The seed percentage is relative to feed oxide, not final catalytic product flux. Even a measured offspring ee needs a founder bound: `ee_observed=phi_founder*ee_founder+(1-phi_founder)*ee_new`, with phi the fraction of catalytic product flux attributable to founders. Seed mass cannot determine phi without activity and material accounting. Neither phi nor a functional transmission matrix is measured here.

Decision: **component-supported useful structural state; inheritance unresolved**. This system uses an organic directing agent during synthesis, requires calcination and inert handling to avoid ambient humidity, and has no demonstrated shared operating window with acidic aqueous PMo12. It is not an integrated living subsystem.

## AuPd produces many particles without demonstrated functional feedback

[Primary DOI10.1039/D1NR04400A](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2021/nr/d1nr04400a). The original AuPd photocatalyst bed regenerates NADH that drives formation of new Au particles. Estimated outputs are100,133 and117 particles per founder rod over three cycles. Those are repeated founder-bed cycles, not descendant generations.

The authors explicitly identify the missing positive-feedback link: the new Au particles lack Pd needed for efficient cofactor regeneration. The supporting information's small bare-Au-rod response is a different geometry/condition, so daughter-sphere activity is not set to exactly zero. No quantitative offspring reproduction kernel follows from the counts. Organic cofactor/matrix dependencies remain disclosed. Detailed primary-access limitations and source controls are retained in [the screen](<sources/nanoparticle/SCREEN.md>).

Decision: **new material production supported; inherited catalytic feedback not demonstrated**. A future proposal must recreate the functional complement in the product and measure isolated-product activity; simply supplying a guessed daughter rate would hide the failure.

## Real functional repair, incompatible and incompletely documented

[Kazakli et al., DOI10.1016/j.memsci.2020.117855](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.memsci.2020.117855) reports porous alumina/molten-carbonate membranes recovering CO2 selectivity after induced damage. This is actual function recovery, not merely a healed-looking surface. It occurs in a700C gas-separation apparatus through redistribution of existing melt. It does not show newly synthesized competent POM or active-material rebuilding, and no aqueous PMo12/target integration is demonstrated. Carbonate contains carbon; its inorganic classification is not a literal absence-of-carbon claim.

The linked [numeric deposit](https://doi.org/10.25405/data.ncl.9610601.v1) was acquired and repository MD5 checksums match. However, the README names a different paper and drilled-channel geometry. Figure labels and the accepted paper differ. The strict paired-channel inventory finds:

## Consequence for the full goal

These results identify three distinct bottlenecks: a fresh cue can determine a functional state unless inheritance is separately tested; a product can omit the parent's indispensable catalytic complement; and a repaired transport barrier can redistribute a finite reserve without synthesizing new active organization. None closes the original R/H/J requirements.

The next decisive evidence is catalytic function on the mismatched-seed GTM-3 products, or on cue-controlled/achiral-feed descendants, after quantitative founder exclusion. The acquired source does not supply it. No compatible validated kinetic parameters exist for simulating that missing observation.
