## Why the calculation is defensible, and its exact limit

The three dimensionless FE ratios and eleven within-record late/early current-magnitude ratios address a useful narrow question: whether the source records exhibit improvement under the complete treatment while subsequent recorded current still changes. Using every mapped record, fixed windows and a persistence reference prevents selective favorable-panel reporting. The time-average integral handles irregular time spacing. Unknown multiplicative current scaling cancels within a trace; it does not authorize cross-trace absolute amplitudes. Missing, malformed or uncomputable comparisons must remain explicit failures, as specified.

This adds a new retrospective numerical description of native data. It does not independently discover the qualitative pattern: published prose and SI curves already expose it. It is useful progress toward identifying the repair evidence gap because recovery and durability are different questions. It cannot substitute for the requested repair mechanism, accounting or serial-inheritance experiments.

Execution and reporting must retain these interpretation limits already consistent with the frozen scope:

1. Call FE ratio greater than one a nominal increase in that labeled efficiency comparison. An `original` label alone does not establish a quantified pristine-performance baseline or full recovery fraction.
2. Call retention below one a nominal decline in recorded current magnitude over the selected windows. Current includes other electrochemical contributions; it is not by itself proof of catalyst-specific damage, formate-production decline, or removal of a material durability defect. The ban on FE-times-current reconstruction is appropriate.
3. The 10–20% window excludes the first 10% of recording. It does not establish that all physical transients have ended. These are fixed descriptive windows, not a validated steady-state criterion. No significance or minimum physically meaningful change follows from crossing one.
4. Source captions use replicate terminology, but unknown specimen identity prohibits treating the three pairs or eight shorter traces as independent experiments. Keep the two duration families separate and do not infer a specimen-linked FE/current/phase trajectory.
5. The treatment bundles electrolyte renewal, mechanical redistribution, resting and electrical reduction. Source phase comparators support a structural interpretation but do not isolate any treatment component or supply cycle-resolved loss accounting. R remains unresolved without demanding exclusive atom-by-atom turnover assignment.
6. All twelve I/E/T/B/R/H/V/S/M/A/J/C integrated requirements remain open. No PMo12 integration, maintained boundary renewal, founder-bounded new descendants, serial functional inheritance, heritable selection, adaptive action or intelligence is tested here. Carbon-containing apparatus must not be silently recast as a demonstrated carbon-free maintained organization.
