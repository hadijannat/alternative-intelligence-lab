## Concrete unconsumed action

[Zhao et al., Nature Communications 16, 5601 (2025), DOI 10.1038/s41467-025-60728-y](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-60728-y) reports NiFe-LDH in single-cell, AEM and CEM configurations, with activity, surface/bulk composition and counter-electrode Fe observations. Its AEM condition accumulates surface Fe while activity declines. The publisher lists source XLSX, SI and supplementary ZIP. The [primary full text at PMC12216224](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12216224/) was readable in this audit; later find calls encountered a browser challenge. An [author-repository PDF](https://dr.ntu.edu.sg/server/api/core/bitstreams/f877e8c1-c825-4abe-9e2d-530c6addc97f/content) was also identified. None of those attachments was downloaded here.

Exact workspace search finds this DOI only in `discovery-loop/sources/iron-resupply-functional-recovery-discovery-v1/search-receipt.json`; no local acquired package or analysis was found. This is a rediscovered lead, not a new unique-compound credit. It is unconsumed at the data-analysis level.

Recommended bounded execution:

1. Acquire the native source workbook/SI and bind each activity, composition and counter-electrode assay to the actual loading, cell geometry, electrolyte, time and replicate. Reject apparent matchedness if different specimens/protocols are pooled.
2. Audit the membrane intervention and actual Fe speciation. At alkaline conditions, a schematic free-Fe3+ flux cannot simply become the calibrated transported species. Check ion selectivity, local pH, geometry, counter sink, and whether the reported composition signals are independent observations.
3. If mappings qualify, freeze a minimal finite-inventory model with competent surface sites, inactive segregated Fe, solution and counter sink. Test whether a total-retention model is contradicted by crossed activity/composition changes. An entire cell configuration, not adjacent points from its trace, is the appropriate retrospective reserved intervention. Admit no origin-function repair claim from this test.
4. If mappings do not qualify, preserve that specific negative and stop fitting. Identify the missing observation rather than add a free mechanism until it fits.

This intervention changes an integration-relevant design choice: a boundary that retains metal can suppress competence if it prevents removal of inactive material. It may therefore change whether a membrane/retention complement is suitable for the proposed maintained architecture. It does not by itself close full-system J, which requires the actual chosen chemistry operating together.

A secondary unconsumed lead is [Wei et al., JACS 147, 13502–13511 (2025), DOI 10.1021/jacs.5c00438](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/jacs.5c00438). Primary abstract and SI description report spatial edge/plane function and potential-driven Fe incorporation. Only those portions were checked; native SI and raw availability remain unverified. Oxygen isotope labeling in these leads must not be relabeled as Fe founder-origin labeling. This secondary lead should not become an open-ended performance-paper search.

## External observations still needed if these leads fail

For R: same-preparation damaged founders, matched metal supply/withdrawal, state/speciation and total inventories, simultaneous function, plus selective new-material function or an independently justified upper bound on all surviving-founder function. Survival, reactivation and incoming-metal presence must remain admissible rivals until excluded.

For H/V: two distinguishable useful parent states under common feed, state-erased/unseeded controls, an independent founder bound in offspring, isolated offspring function and serial transfer. Structural replication or pooled composition alone cannot fill these observations.

For J and the exact starting target: species-resolved identity/persistence in the operating medium, then common-system add/remove/rescue tests that connect energy and transport to state renewal, inheritance and adaptive action. Separate alkaline NiFe and acidic POM literature does not establish this. C additionally requires actual bounded adaptive tasks and causal material mechanisms; no current assay establishes it.

No simulation can create these absent measurements. No recommendation to mark the goal blocked is made while the specified intervention dataset remains a concrete safe action. Root owns the status decision.
