# Why the finite transport models amplify background

The finite models retain and repeatedly expose substrate as well as catalyst.
Their existing bulk chemistry then converts Hyper-A into fully competent F through
`vh = kh A² [B/(KB+B)]²`, even when the initial seed is zero. Generated F drives
further F formation and reporter ligation. Most late reporter production is
therefore catalytic without being evidence for inheritance of the initial seed.

This diagnosis uses fixed parameters from the preceding failed comparison. It
adds no fit, model selection or held-out validation result. All 36 predetermined
paths, including duplicate controls and unfavorable outcomes, are preserved in
[diagnostics.csv](diagnostics.csv); the [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) precedes execution.

In the bath, 91.59% of new intracellular reporter in generation 7 is generated by
`vr`, and 56.00% of newly formed intracellular F is generated by `va`. Because this
path began with zero F, all its F ultimately requires the background `vh` channel.
These are reaction-channel counts, not experimentally measured molecular ancestry.
The diagnostic does not label individual descendants of a background event.

The intracellular background exposure integral, `∫Σw A²s² dt`, rises from
15.209 µM² min in legacy to 47.808 in bath and 48.749 in patch. End-of-generation
retained A, including outside, rises from 1.875 to 4.711 and 4.694 µM. The bath
therefore has 3.14 times the intracellular background F formation and 7.14 times
the intracellular catalytic reporter formation of legacy at generation 7.

The net exchange of A, B, F and reporter is still outward in the reference paths.
For bath at generation 7 the intracellular changes during transport are −1.3585,
−2.0274, −0.007863 and −0.016683 µM, respectively. Thus the result must not be
described as net inward uptake. A finite exterior permits return flows and reduces
permanent loss, while the present diagnostic records their net effect. It does
not estimate separate gross inward and outward fluxes.

Freezing exterior chemistry only modestly reduces background. Removing the active-F
initiating channel has the largest effect on seed-0 activity; removing direct
reporter background does not. Yet even deleting both background channels leaves
seed contrast far below the previously observed 9.8283 pp. Background suppression
alone does not fix the failure to retain seed dependence. The observed contrast is
historical context, not an independent target used in this diagnostic.

Inert exterior follows the prior definition: it also suspends competence decay.
Discarding exterior is a hypothetical whole-culture operation. The source's PAGE
aliquot wash does not establish that this operation occurs in the continuing
culture. Controls remain conditional interpretations of the old model.

## Where chemistry is being extrapolated

The published S13 assay starts with 5 µM Cy5-A and 7.5 µM B; the retained fit uses
Hyper-A doses through 2.5 µM and initial F through 1 µM. The GUV substrate cohorts
instead start with 10 µM Hyper-A plus 2.5 µM reporter, or 15 µM B in a separate
population. These differences are verified in the [primary article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-36940-z)
and its retained supplementary methods, section S13.

The actual warm-reaction states, after exchange, are still different. Maximum
intracellular Hyper-A at G7 is 5.112 µM for legacy, 5.341 for bath and 5.351 for
patch. All generations retain cohorts above the 2.5 µM nominal S13 A limit. Bath
G7 local B spans 0.298–8.002 µM, and F reaches 0.1264 µM. Its exterior A reaches
1.1219 µM and B 1.6627 µM. The concern is the joint mixture and occupancy law, not
merely whether one component lies within a nominal initial-dose interval. These
bounds are not a reconstructed calibration trajectory envelope.

The quadratic Hyper-A channel, instantaneous competence of every new F, and
application of the same sucrose-derived effective rates to the glucose exterior
are model assumptions. Source evidence supports residual Hyper-A activity; it does
not establish this rate law across all local mixtures, or that every ligated F
is immediately active. Local catalyst–substrate association, competition, bound
products and activity recovery would require independently constrained kinetics.

A rate-of-means shortcut would make the problem worse: bath G7 `vh` computed from
the intracellular mean trajectory would be 0.09500 µM, versus the correct weighted
cohort extent 0.032685. Legacy gives 0.06617 versus 0.010398. The existing model
already uses cohort rates, so this diagnosis finds no such implementation bug.
