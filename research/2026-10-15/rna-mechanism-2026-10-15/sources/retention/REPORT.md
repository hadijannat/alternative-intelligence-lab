# Additional retention datasets

Two new public numerical datasets were acquired and normalized into **100 rows**. Neither measures the probability that a descendant compartment inherits newly produced, catalytically active R3C. They constrain separate mechanisms and expose important observer limitations. Search cutoff: client date **2026-10-15**; five primary candidates reviewed. This is a targeted search, not an exhaustive literature review.

## Most useful new constraints

**Ghosh et al. (2025)** supplies the strongest new joint *partition and function* dataset: a 39-nt hammerhead ribozyme with seven peptide coacervates. `ghosh_pellet_cleavage.csv` contains 77 representative time-series points, each traceable to source line and independently checked against the substrate/product gel intensities. `ghosh_HH_phase_concentrations.csv` preserves seven phase compositions in **micromolar charge equivalents**, as the SI explicitly requires. For example, P-3's representative cleavage reaches 80.5859% at 360 minutes; P-7 remains near background at 2.4631%, despite strong RNA sequestration. The series is a representative replicate, not the paper's three-replicate mean. Whole-dispersion replicate data are also retained raw. [Article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63656-z), [CC BY 4.0 numerical repository](https://edmond.mpg.de/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=doi:10.17617/3.G6KSU2).

Interpretation safeguards: the pellet contains dilute fluid as well as coacervate; dense-phase composition uses QPI and tie-line inference. Fluorescent HH partition is an RNA-species measurement, not an active-fraction assay. Partial FRAP describes internal mobility. `ghosh_partition_coefficients.xlsx` contains **model estimates for substrate/product**, including an assumed twofold difference, rather than measured catalyst escape rates. `ghosh_supernatant_fit.xlsx` is fitted from an assumed zero-time point and one measured 360-minute endpoint. Neither file should be promoted into an independently measured rate constant. The source text has one P-2 aggregation that treats a missing replicate as zero; the normalized phase workbook's corresponding nonmissing mean is retained. The workbook's average total compositions are not the nominal kinetic recipe, so we do not derive retained mass by subtracting concentrations across those preparations.

**Le Vay et al. (2023)** supplies 16 rows of active/inactive R3C ladder systems, two polylysine mixtures and four observation times (0.5, 1, 2, 24 hours). At 24 hours the long-polylysine population correlation is −0.456875 for active systems and +0.566333333 for inactive systems, consistent with slower population mixing when substrate concatenation occurs. The normalized table includes population and within-droplet Pearson coefficients with source SEMs. **The fluorescent label follows substrate/concatemer, not catalytic RNA.** Fusion and diffusion both contribute, so these coefficients cannot be used as leakage fractions. No offspring compartments were assayed. The short-polylysine figure caption has a peptide-name inconsistency; the figure title and numeric workbook agree on `(Lys)5-24`. [Primary article and source data](https://elifesciences.org/articles/83543).

## Closest alternate membrane routes

**Peter et al. (2023)** is the closest R3C/vesicle extension: reversible polyU/spermine organelles aid freeze-thaw content exchange. Table 1 gives fluorescence partition coefficients 286 for Cy5-F (66 nt), 104 for substrate A and 44 for B. However, the modified reaction produces a **catalytically inactive ligated product**. Partial FRAP is not an efflux experiment. Raw data are available from authors on request; direct publisher downloads were blocked with HTTP 403, though the main text/tables were accessible in the web reader. Exact buffer/pH/Mg conditions were not independently recovered from SI and remain unverified here. [Primary article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/anie.202218507).

**DasGupta et al. (2023)** measures the actual 70-nt ligase species retained in fatty-acid vesicles, with separate activity measurements: after three hours at 3 mM Mg, ribozyme retention was 72%, versus 32% for the 16-nt substrate; ribose increased these to 92% and 74%. The FAM-labelled ligase has lower activity than its unlabelled counterpart. Critically, retention uses 250 mM Tris while ligation captions specify 100 mM Tris: do not treat them as an exact common-condition fit. SI download was blocked; only published numerical endpoints were read, not a new machine-readable replicate series. No lineage/serial-transfer assay. [Primary article](https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/chem.202301376).

The original Salibi (2023) dataset remains the relevant R3C/POPC serial-transfer source, already retained in the preceding laboratory. This search did not find a public same-system time series separately measuring *new active F1*, *founder active F1*, and *inactive reporter* inside identified descendants.

## How these data should change the model

Keep catalytic state separate from RNA amount and fluorescent label. Add chemically distinct observation operators for active catalyst, reporter product and phase enrichment. Model compartment loss and horizontal transfer separately from division. Use the new data to challenge structural assumptions (e.g. “better retention always raises activity”), not to insert numerical permeability or reaction constants into the incompatible POPC/R3C chemistry. A direct experiment must recover inside/outside full-length F1, measure catalytic turnover after recovery, and distinguish founder from newly ligated active F1 across a transfer or division.

## Reproduction and evidence

`python3 rna-mechanism-2026-10-15/sources/retention/acquire.py` refreshes primary downloads; HTTP failures are explicit. URLs, sizes and SHA256 digests are in `retrieval_receipts.json`. `sources.json` records conditions, observer classes, licenses and exclusions for every candidate. Archive originals are unchanged.

`python3 rna-mechanism-2026-10-15/sources/retention/extract.py` verifies 12 source hashes, reconstructs 77 cleavage percentages to an absolute tolerance of 0.000001 percentage points, checks exact workbook headers/cells and 16 exchange rows, and verifies the SI unit declaration. Its binary result is `PASS` in `.omo/evidence/verification.json`; normalized counts are 16 exchange + 77 cleavage + 7 phase composition rows. No fitting or cross-chemistry calibration was performed.

Skipped: author requests, inaccessible Wiley SI, and large raw microscopy archives. Risk: none of these observations identifies functional inheritance of regenerated active catalyst in the original R3C/POPC system.
