# Fixed-parameter seed-memory diagnosis

Declared before diagnostic execution, 2026-10-15. This is an explanatory audit of an already observed failure, not a held-out candidate comparison. It performs no parameter fitting or model selection. Earlier laboratories remain unchanged.

Reuse the immutable `rna-turnover-tests-2026-10-15/model.py` and saved baseline/release full-chemistry parameters plus their independently calibrated fractional/uniform mixing rates. For seeds 0, 1, 2 µM, preserve all seven generations and each feed, exchange, and reaction stage. Record all twelve weighted species concentrations, PAGE yield, total competent catalyst, conditional competent founder, and source/removed/retained moieties. Verify inventory conservation to 1e-7 µM and the reference against the prior model to 1e-7 percentage points.

The twelve fixed interventions are reference; zero competence loss; no material loss; both zero competence/material loss; no serial transfer and no fresh feed; no Hyper-A background pathways; no unseeded active initiation alone; no direct background reporter ligation alone; no regeneration; replacing fresh substrate with empty volume; complete mixing; and no further mixing after G1. Every intervention retains unchanged remaining parameters. No-transfer necessarily also removes replenishment in a fixed-volume population, so this is a coupled counterfactual. Empty-volume replenishment preserves the transfer schedule while removing fresh chemical input. Zero competence loss, perfect material retention and full mixing are limiting cases, not measured alternatives. Disabling regeneration converts newly ligated active products into inactive material using the prior model's stoichiometric switch.

Do not choose a new transport model or fitted bounds by these scores. Independently derive the passive founder contraction and the covariance contraction of fractional mixing.
