# Non-carbon chemistry: compound hypothesis and evidence audit

**No novel compound or non-carbon living, intelligent system was established.** The useful result is a specific, falsifiable composition hypothesis, a reproducible inventory of 1,000 existing inorganic structure records, and a comparison of the functions that have actually been demonstrated. Negative results and unknowns remain part of the record.

The requested 1,000-agent run was **not performed**. This pass used **24 subagents: 15 direct specialists and nine child audits**, plus the coordinator. The tool interface reported 51 concurrent slots including the coordinator. That limits simultaneous execution; it does not prove that 1,000 sequential agents are impossible. Names and contributions are recorded in [the participation ledger](verification/agents.json). Database records are not counted as agents. Raising an application setting was not independently verified as changing this tool limit.

This investigation is separate from the preceding [RNA chemistry checkpoint](../rna-coupled-chemistry-2026-10-15/WORKPLAN.md), which was stopped at the user's change of direction. No new RNA model or held-out RNA fit was completed after that pivot.

## A concrete compound hypothesis

The exploratory target is the nominal anion

\[
\boxed{[\mathrm{Mo}^{VI}_{34}\mathrm{W}^{VI}\mathrm{V}^{V}\mathrm{O}_{112}(\mathrm{H_2O})_{16}]^{9-}}
\]

It replaces two metal positions in the established Mo36 composition with one W and one V. Its charge is consistent: `34×6 + 6 + 5 − 112×2 = −9`. A formal V(IV) counterpart would be `10−` if the composition and protonation stayed fixed. Water is neutral in this accounting. [Machine-readable hypothesis](candidate.json) · [independent charge checks](verification/verification.json).

The research question is whether substitution can preserve assembly while allowing a distinguishable, reversible chemical state. **Neither improved stability nor useful memory is predicted here.** The substitution sites, atomic geometry, actual hydration/protonation, counterions, solution lifetime and electron localization are unresolved. No synthesis, new DFT calculation, measured spectrum or derivative kinetic fit was performed. A balanced formula can still describe a species that cannot be isolated or that immediately rearranges.

Parent Mo36 has measured seed-dependent assembly in acidic water. That evidence does not transfer to this derivative. Strong reduction destroys the parent in one reported regime; it neither proves nor rules out survival of a hypothetical single-electron change. [Miras et al., primary study](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1921536117).

The bounded novelty search found no indexed exact-formula match, but did find relevant positive prior art: Mo36-related heterometal assignments, mixed Mo/W/V Keggin families and doped tungsten oxides. Consequently, **novelty remains unestablished**. The target is not identified as a discovery or given an invented molecular structure. [Novelty audit and search log](research/novelty/REPORT.md) · [mixed-metal primary manuscript](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_1849723/component/file_1854326/content).

The expanded structure search acquired two parent-salt CIFs. The potassium record is a traceable heavy-atom starting reference, with missing hydrogen positions and unresolved hydration/protonation choices. The sodium record contains a suspicious 1.3352 Å Mo–O separation reproduced from its coordinates; its original bytes and the anomaly are retained. Neither file was silently converted into a supposed derivative. Before symmetry reduction, choosing distinguishable W and V positions on 36 sites permits 1,260 ordered assignments, illustrating why a composition alone does not specify a molecule. [Structure audit and acquired coordinates](research/structure/REPORT.md) · [primary potassium structure](https://doi.org/10.1107/S0567740882007018).

## More interpretable reference systems

The more defensible first functional comparison uses known materials. Mo36 supplies an assembly reference; hydrated tungsten oxide supplies a separate proton/electron-state reference. Their coupled behavior remains unmeasured. A common medium, explicit electron pathway and preserved species identity would have to be established before claiming that they support one another. Known monovanadium Keggins, including `H4[PVMo11O40]` and `Na4[PVW11O40]`, provide additional redox controls. [POM dossier](research/pom/REPORT.md).

| Candidate family | Supported observation | Main missing connection or failure |
|---|---|---|
| Parent Mo36 | Seed-dependent assembly | New material carrying a parent-specific functional state through repeated renewal |
| Hypothetical Mo34WV | Formal composition and charge only | Species identity, stability, redox response and assembly all unverified |
| Hydrated WO3 / HxWO3 | Proton insertion and state-dependent electrochemistry | Catalytic hydrogen evolution can discharge the stored state; aqueous inheritance unmeasured |
| NaClO3 crystals | Seed-correlated handedness after removal of the mounted parent | Microscopic founder carryover is incompletely bounded; catalytic or maintenance-function inheritance is unmeasured |
| Na/K aluminosilicate CHA | Successive seeded growth preserves topology | Composition and size drift; feed can overwrite a candidate inherited state |
| Fe(1−x)NixS | Nickel uptake and retained composition | Adsorption, substitution and phase change are not evidence of copied catalytic function |
| Iron hydroxide/sulfide boundaries | Driven mineral growth and measured electrical output | Selectivity maintained by useful internal chemistry and inherited by descendants is unmeasured |
| VO2, NiO and engineered inorganic memory stacks | Material history dependence, plasticity or programmed memory | No demonstrated combination with autonomous renewal and functional inheritance |

The distinctions matter in both directions. Structural inheritance and material learning are real, bounded observations when measured. External energy or stimuli do not invalidate them. They also do not establish a living entity: the role of an external controller, the material's own state update, renewal and transmission must be assessed separately. An additional audit found experimentally demonstrated local analog learning and chemical plasticity, while distinguishing manufactured devices from the proposed aqueous chemistry. [Independent functional gates](research/critical/REPORT.md) · [information and learning audit](research/information/REPORT.md).

Examples of incompatibility already constrain designs. Acidic POM chemistry cannot inherit transport constants from alkaline precipitation gardens. Intact active species must be distinguished from retained total metal. More ionic/redox exchange can erase state differences. Several familiar devices use organic binders or electrolytes, although genuinely inorganic active stacks also exist. Carbon in the active material, supporting components, preparation and apparatus is reported separately. [Memory review](research/memory/REPORT.md) · [compartment review](research/compartments/REPORT.md).

Hydrated WO3 is particularly instructive: its insertion chemistry and hydrogen-evolution activity are experimentally coupled, so storing reducing equivalents may also open a discharge pathway. This supplies a failure mechanism for a future model, not a memory lifetime to import into a new environment. [Spencer et al., primary study](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4SC00102H). Aluminosilicate seeded growth likewise supplies a useful positive structural control while preserving compositional drift as a negative result. [CHA primary study](https://doi.org/10.1039/C9RA10825D).

The [common-medium audit](research/redox_compatibility/REPORT.md) records ten condition rows and six primary sources. The relevant hydrate study uses sulfuric acid, continuous hydrogen purging and controlled electrode potentials; the parent cluster assembly has different medium and redox boundaries. No potential conversion or shared kinetic constants were invented. Favorable evidence is retained too: unchanged hydrate morphology after one reported cycling check prevents a blanket claim of inevitable dissolution. Joint compatibility remains unmeasured.

The additional [replication audit](research/replication/REPORT.md) independently reacquired an already catalogued 13-trial NaClO3 supplement. Four printed aggregate cells disagree with the listed trials beyond the declared rounding tolerance. For example, stirring similarity is printed as 99.26%, whereas the four listed values average 99.4425%. Both versions are preserved. The observations are matching **mass fractions**, not counts of independent offspring. They have already been inspected and are not an untouched holdout. Mounted-seed removal also leaves microscopic founder mass unresolved. [Primary study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultsonch.2021.105763).

## The 1,000-record computational screen

The retained official NIST JARVIS snapshot contains 75,993 structure records. Composition exclusions leave 71,739 eligible carbon-free, multielement records. Deterministic composition strata and salted source-ID hashes select **1,000 structures representing 923 reduced compositions**. This is a deliberately stratified inventory, not a representative sample of all chemistry. It uses the registry-pinned 2022-12-12 archive; a newer archive exists, and no latest-release claim is made. [Official NIST JARVIS](https://pages.nist.gov/jarvis/) · [source attribution and license](database/SOURCES.md).

| Recorded property | Result | Interpretation limit |
|---|---:|---|
| Formation energy ≤ 0 eV/atom | 912 records | Relative to the database's elemental references |
| Formation energy > 0 eV/atom | 88 records | Retained as unfavorable outcomes |
| Energy above hull ≤ 0.1 eV/atom | 680 records | Broad declared solid-state screen; does not establish solution stability |
| Energy above hull > 0.1 eV/atom | 320 records | All retained; the cutoff is not a universal existence criterion |
| MBJ bandgap unavailable | 751 records | Missing remains missing |
| Maintenance, replication, functional inheritance, learning, intelligence | Unmeasured for every record | No life score or inferred probability |

These categories overlap. No unfavorable row was swapped for a better one. Existing structure entries are not novel compounds, and database membership alone does not prove experimental synthesis. The proposed molecular Mo34WV target was **not selected or validated by these periodic-solid calculations**. [Full inventory and methods](database/REPORT.md) · [all 1,000 outcomes](database/results-v1/screened_1000.csv).

Eight screening tests passed. Six scientific artifacts reproduced byte-for-byte. An independent implementation reconstructed all selected IDs from the raw archive and verified all 4,000 numerical cells, missing values and category totals: 22 checks passed. Property-independent selection is inspectable; an independently timestamped pre-property code hash is missing. The audit therefore does not certify the historical exact-code freeze. This limitation remains explicit. [Independent audit](verification/REPORT.md).

## Earlier failures remain evidence

The [prior-repository audit](research/prior_repo_audit/REPORT.md) checked 19 unchanged source hashes and retained several results that a new candidate proposal must respect:

- In an earlier hypothetical oxide simulation, improved growth survived compositional reset. Higher yield therefore did not identify inheritance of a parent-specific function.
- Prior WO3 accounting gives a finite accepting capacity. A reservoir already fully charged under that model has no remaining capacity to accept the proposed repair electrons. Complete counterion transport also changes the energy accounting; a proton-only electrical term is insufficient.
- Executed B3N4H7 calculations failed numerical qualification: derivative convergence failed, a subsequent SCF attempt stopped early, and no molecular minimum was established. These are computational failures rather than a proof that B–N chemistry is impossible.
- An earlier fibrous calcium-silicate candidate depended on unmeasured physical copying and reading assumptions. Its abstract-model learning results cannot be assigned to a new substance.

These negatives are preserved in their original laboratories. No successful documentation check overrides a failed physical or numerical claim.

## Decisive tests before a stronger claim

1. Establish the actual species and its function in one declared operating medium, including after redox cycling and contact with the proposed boundary.
2. Separate total retention, active retention, irreversible deactivation and production of new active material. A metal balance alone does not measure competence.
3. Test whether distinct parent states cause distinct functions in **newly made descendants** under the same feed. Quantify founder carryover; include no-seed, inactive-seed, composition-matched and state-erased controls.
4. Interrupt the proposed energy-to-maintenance connection while matching material supply. Determine whether it supports measured renewal or selectivity.
5. Freeze a model and complete-condition holdouts before fitting. Retain failed synthesis, mixed species, background amplification, erased seed differences and unsuccessful forecasts.
6. Test adaptive use of state against matched controls only after a causal state-to-action link exists. A successful learning device need not be alive; a persistent chemical state need not learn.

No examined candidate currently establishes all these functions together. The most useful next evidence is a species-resolved, same-medium retention/renewal dataset with distinguishable founders and newly formed product. Assigning invented rates to the hypothetical formula would not close that gap.

Skipped physical synthesis, new DFT/MD, exhaustive patent/structure clearance and a 1,000-agent execution. The principal risk is that the proposed species does not exist as intended, or that coupling useful components destroys their individual functions.
