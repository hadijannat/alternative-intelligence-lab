# Preliminary novelty and composition audit

Date: 2026-10-15. Scope: public primary literature, institutional manuscripts, public patent descriptions and selected database-indexed records. This is a bounded scientific prior-art screen, not an exhaustive structure search or a patentability opinion.

**Outcome: no novel compound for non-carbon life has been identified.** Known inorganic compounds supply individual functions, including autocatalysis, redox storage and switching. Combining those functions is a testable system hypothesis. Neither the combination nor a previously unseen formula establishes life, intelligence, molecular existence or novelty.

## The proposed Mo34WV composition

The proposed anion `[Mo(VI)34 W(VI) V(V) O112(H2O)16]9−` passes formal charge and atom-count checks: `34×6 + 6 + 5 − 112×2 = −9`; neutral water contributes zero. A one-electron reduction assigned specifically to V gives a formal 10− state. Attribution of that electron to V, rather than Mo/W or a changed structure, remains an experimental question. The 36 metal positions match the parent nuclearity, but arithmetic supplies no atomic geometry.

The all-Mo parent `[Mo36O112(H2O)16]8−` is established. Eda and colleagues characterized related condensed frameworks with changed bound-water counts and carbon-containing linker cations. Their work illustrates that the framework, ligation and complete salt must be specified separately; an inorganic anion does not make its counterion-containing material carbon-free. [Primary manuscript](https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/kernel/90000962/90000962.pdf)

There is closer substitution precedent than a generic Keggin analogy: a 2012 hydrothermal study assigns solution species to `{Mo36−xNbxO112}` and its SI discusses Raman shifts that *might* indicate partial V substitution. This is tentative spectroscopic assignment in a mixed precursor system; it does not characterize the proposed Mo34WV molecule. It nevertheless defeats a claim that heterometal modification of Mo36 is an entirely new idea. [Sanchez Sanchez et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201200746), [retained author manuscript and SI](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_1849723/component/file_1854326/content)

Known variations also include `K10[Mo36O110(OH)6(H2O)12]·38H2O`, whose formal anion charge is −10. Thus retention of exactly O112 and sixteen bound waters after substitution is an additional hypothesis. [Baizig et al.](https://journals.tubitak.gov.tr/chem/vol42/iss2/10/)

Exact-string queries for `Mo34WVO112`, `Mo35W` with `O112`, and `Mo35V` with `O112` returned no indexed hits. Broader searches returned parent/related families and irrelevant atom labels. These absences are preserved in `search_log.json`; they are **not proof that the compounds have never been reported**. No target CIF, isolated salt, measured spectrum, positional isomer assignment or formation energy was recovered.

Disposition: retain Mo34WV only as a **hypothetical formula target, insufficient structural evidence**. A peripheral-octahedral substitution proposal is less disruptive than assuming replacement of a pentagonal core, but neither site preference nor retention of parent autocatalysis is established. Do not rank it above known comparators merely because its exact formula was absent from search results.

## Recommended falsifiable lead

Prefer a **known-compound architecture** as the first functional test: established Mo36 chemistry coupled to an independently characterized hydrated-WO3 charge reservoir through a declared inorganic interface. This recommendation is an inference from separate component evidence, not evidence that the coupled assembly works. The bounded `Mo36`/`WO3` searches did not identify an exact coupled device, but absence of a match cannot establish architectural novelty either.

The discriminating question is whether changing the WO3 state changes subsequent Mo36 formation/retention under identical imposed feed and energy, while the material state is itself renewed. Compare the physically coupled system with each component alone, an uncoupled mixture, a state-erased control and matched total metal/redox inventory. Resolve phase/species identity before attributing kinetic differences to molecular replication. A charge reservoir that simply discharges, a seed that survives without renewal, or nonspecific accelerated background assembly is a negative outcome.

For the hypothetical Mo34WV target, first establish an isolated/solution-resolved species and actual site occupancy; test its stability and reversible redox response before assigning catalytic inheritance. Parent Mo36 and independently known POM redox compounds are necessary comparators. There is currently insufficient evidence to predict that the derivative improves seed preservation or to designate it a life compound.

## Evidence boundaries and checks

`acquisition.json` records successful raw-file downloads and failed URLs. `search_log.json` records bounded queries and outcomes; `sources.json` contains the primary references. `verify.py` checks exact formal-charge arithmetic, carbon absence for the proposed anions, retained-file hashes and explicit unresolved novelty dispositions. A passing verification is a documentation/accounting result.

Skipped synthesis, quantum calculations and exhaustive structural/patent searches. Molecular existence, functional coupling, autonomous renewal, inheritance and intelligence remain unverified.
