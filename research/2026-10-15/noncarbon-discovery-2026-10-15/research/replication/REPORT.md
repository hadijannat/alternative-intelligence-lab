# Carbon-free replication: bounded counterexample search

Audit date: 2026-10-15. This is an independent primary-source check of the strongest alternatives to Mo36, with five decisive papers. No new compound or living system was discovered. The sodium-chlorate dataset was already present in the repository; this pass independently reacquired and checked it.

**The conclusion must distinguish inherited physical state from inherited organizational function.** Sodium chlorate is an established carbon-free solid with two chiral lattice states. Seed-associated handedness can persist in recovered crystals after a mounted seed is removed. That is substantial evidence for structural transmission, and optical rotation is a measurable physical property. A blanket assertion that inorganic matter cannot transmit any information would be false. These observations do not yet establish active-catalyst renewal, inherited maintenance, adaptive evolution, or intelligence.

## Independent check of the existing chlorate dataset

The 2021 supplement contains 4 stirring trials, 5 at 98 kHz, and 4 at 200 kHz. The extraction preserves every individual value and every printed aggregate; no outliers are removed. **Similarity is mass-based, not an offspring count.**

| Condition | Printed matching mean (%) | Mean of listed trials (%) | Printed total-mass mean (g) | Mean of listed masses (g) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Stirring | 99.26 | 99.4425 | 8.332 | 8.06475 |
| 98 kHz ultrasound | 85.75 | 84.428 | 8.906 | 9.4868 |
| 200 kHz ultrasound | 91.40 | 91.4025 | 8.994 | 8.994 |

Four of twelve printed mean/standard-deviation cells differ from recalculation beyond the declared 0.015 comparison tolerance; the listed standard deviations agree within that tolerance. The abstract additionally reports 92.4% at 200 kHz, inconsistent with the table and listed observations. The cause is unresolved. This discrepancy was already retained by the repository's earlier source audit; the present independent extraction agrees with that warning.

## What seed removal does not establish

The existing [founder-mass analysis](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/chlorate-founder-mass-bound-v1/report.md) supplies the relevant restricted rival. If recovered founder mass F entirely matches the seed and all new material is exactly racemic, observed recovered mass M and matching fraction s can be explained when `F = M(2s−1)`. Excluding that rival requires an independently measured founder-mass bound below this value. The dataset provides no such bound. Removing the visible mounted seed does not establish the mass of founder fragments in the recovered crop.

Even excluding that particular rival would leave stochastic nucleation, fragment-mediated amplification and environmentally biased growth to test. No isotope-resolved founder accounting, functional descendants or serial adaptive lineages are inferred here. The 13 observations have already been inspected; they cannot now be represented as a fresh untouched validation set.

## Additional rejected shortcuts

The child source audit identified [Dateo et al., 1982](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja00366a021), a chlorite–iodide autocatalytic oscillator, but its reported methods include either organic acetate or inorganic sulfate buffer. No figure-to-buffer mapping was verified, so this pass does not credit the whole study as carbon-free. A newer [chlorite–tetrathionate front study](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3CP00217A) uses organic color indicators; it is a useful dynamical comparator with a carbon-containing experimental mixture. Neither access-limited lead is promoted to inherited-function evidence.

For the proposed Mo/W/V cluster, these sources provide assay controls and failure mechanisms, not transferable rate constants. Stronger stirring, sonication or turnover can increase competing state production instead of preserving seed information. A usable next dataset must combine species/state identity, quantified founder contribution, new-material function, and repeated transfer under a common environment. Until that exists, a coupled life-like simulation would depend on unmeasured assumptions.

Verification and retrieval failures are retained in [checks.json](checks.json), [evidence ledger](evidence_ledger.json), and acquisition records. No previous laboratory files were changed.

Skipped: new fitting, synthesis and exhaustive novelty searching. Main risk: interpreting a seed-associated crystal state or oscillatory response as independently attributed functional heredity.
