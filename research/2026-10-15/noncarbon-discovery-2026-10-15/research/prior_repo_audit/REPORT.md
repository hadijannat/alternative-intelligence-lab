# Prior repository evidence audit

15 October 2026. Read-only audit of selected existing `noncarbon-route`, `metal-oxide` and `discovery-loop` artifacts. This audit adds no chemical experiment, simulation or independent literature replication. The earlier numerical failures and evidence boundaries remain unchanged. Current bytes of 19 source artifacts are pinned in [sources.json](sources.json); direct saved-summary checks are recorded in [checks.json](checks.json).

**The new proposal has no demonstrated route from composition to functional inheritance or intelligence.** The prior work already contains useful negative controls, a competing structural-transmission lead, and executed B–N calculations that should remain visible beside the new chemistry dossiers. Eight findings follow.

1. **No earlier exact Mo34WV proposal was located in the bounded local search.** Searches for `Mo34`, `Mo35W`, `Mo35V`, `WVI1`, and `Mo.?W.?V` returned no matches in Markdown under the three named prior-work directories, excluding source downloads, checkpoints, frozen artifacts and replay copies. An additional JSON/CSV search excluded bulk results and verification directories and also returned no matches. This is a local negative search, not proof of external novelty, exhaustive local absence or an established compound. In particular, an invented formula does not acquire measured properties from the old Mo36 data.

2. **The prior strongest structural comparator was NaClO3; its attribution gap is still open.** The [discovery-loop summary](../../../discovery-loop/report.md) identifies cooled aqueous sodium-chlorate crystals as a structural-inheritance lead. The [source audit](../../../discovery-loop/sources/chlorate2021/audit.md) preserves 13 vessels, both parent handednesses, five-minute parent exposure, mounted-parent removal and later recovered matching-handedness mass. These are mass fractions, not independent copying probabilities; removal does not bound microscopic founder carryover. The later [founder-mass calculation](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/chlorate-founder-mass-bound-v1/report.md) derives `T = M(2s−1)` for the restricted rival of matching founder matter plus exactly racemic new mass. Required founder thresholds range from 3.0253512 to 10.52424 g, but no measured upper bound on recovered founder matter exists. The functional-inheritance gate remains unresolved. This provides a more concrete structural comparator than a guessed information-carrying Mo/W/V molecule, while supplying no functional copying or intelligence. Separately, the older [CL-B1-v1 fibrous calcium chain-silicate candidate](../../../claude-run/noncarbon-route/MATERIAL_SEARCH_B175.md) remains unvalidated: breakage is described, but no source images the transmitted internal sequence or continuation in new growth. That older literature sweep was predominantly abstract-level evidence.

3. **The existing lattice's learning labels are explicitly conditional, and its integration failed.** The [thesis status](../../../claude-run/noncarbon-route/STATUS_FOR_THESIS.md) reports abstract response, memory and learning separately; the integrated W-c design did not meet its criterion and G4 stays unresolved. The first reactor's learning was falsified within its specified scope. A faster-flushed reactor supported a model-level learning result, but the growth reader, bit, flip rule, sequence-copying errors and transmission at breakage were supplied rules. No physical material was mapped to those assumptions. Therefore no candidate formula can inherit those cognitive results. The prior record also shows how reading inflow instead of local solution, or allowing a late growth observation, can supply information that a chemical sensor would not have.

4. **Improved growth survived removal of direct compositional inheritance.** The [metal-oxide inheritance report](../../../metal-oxide/report.md) and [saved summary](../../../metal-oxide/results/evolution/summary.json) record growth-factor changes of +0.8711166 for intact inheritance and +0.9667095 under pooled-composition reset. Ancestor resemblance H3 changes from 0.2541272 to 0.00008961. These were dimensionless simulations of six abstract components, supplied catalytic pairs, fission and culling, not identified molybdenum chemistry. All 20 equal-clock controls were censored at a size cap and have no endpoint inference. The useful negative control is that faster production alone does not identify transmission of parent-specific function.

5. **Even newly produced material did not resolve a retained organization advantage.** The later [frontier report](../../../metal-oxide/frontier/report.md) and [saved chemical summary](../../../metal-oxide/frontier/chemistry/results/summary.json) retain final intact-minus-reset contrasts of 0.0067982 and 0.0157217 for assumed catalytic strengths 2 and 20. Their graph-level intervals include zero. All fueled final material came from assay feed, yet seed-free and composition-reset arms also maintained products. This is an unresolved contrast in a limited hypothetical reaction family, not an impossibility result. The same report separately preserves a computation failure: an externally trained abstract reservoir's binding accuracy fell from 81.5% nominal to 50.3% at longer delays. Retained state, nonlinear response, useful task behavior and chemical inheritance therefore require separate tests; none can be inferred from oxide hysteresis or output abundance.

6. **The Mo36 curves constrain descriptive prediction only.** The [published-data audit](../../../metal-oxide/docs/published-model.md) extracts 480 measured observations in six seeded conditions and excludes source prediction columns. The [calibration report](../../../metal-oxide/published/calibration/report.md) tests chronological holdouts, with double-relaxation mean absolute errors 1.3080×10⁻⁶ and 1.6246×10⁻⁶ native signal units for the two cutoffs. These are previously available data and retrospective within-condition forecasts. Units, seed/mixing conversion, observation calibration and replicate identifiers remain unresolved; fast-shape parameters hit bounds in 8/12 fits. A good tail forecast does not identify elementary rates, two molecular pathways, active retention, derivative kinetics or hereditary generation times. The original Julia network was inspected, not reproduced.

7. **Prior interface accounting already rejects shortcuts in the proposed integration.** In the [Mn/Mo36 counterion analysis](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/manganese-mo36-counterion-transport-v1/report.md), electrical terms cancel for complete neutral H+/Cl− transport or H+/Na+ exchange. Under its stipulated pH 7–9 to 1.7 gradient, equal-counterion scenarios retain 30.18–41.57 kJ/mol H+ increments; these are conditional transfer terms, not measured pump work or intrinsic Mo36 synthesis costs. The [WO3 capacity calculation](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/wo3-mn-ce-redox-capacity-v1/report.md) gives `2*rMn + rCe <= W_empty`: a fully charged assumed reservoir has no remaining forward electron-accepting capacity, and reversing the formal reaction undoes the oxidation being called repair. Neither accessible bronze capacity nor a joint regeneration cycle was measured. The [Ag2O/H2 compatibility report](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/ag-oxide-hydrogen-mo-compatibility-v1/report.md) concerns metallic Mo oxidation and must not be used as direct evidence of Mo36 degradation. The [Mo36 dilution calculation](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/mo36-dilution-detectability-v1/results/report.md) uses an assumed 1% threshold and scenario-dependent signals; a numerical PASS does not establish experimental detectability. These constraints support keeping reservoir occupancy, counterions, background, speciation and active function explicit.

8. **B–N already has executed adverse quantum-calculation results.** The [E841 finer-grid report](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/borazine-amino-finer-grid-v1/report.md) concerns an isolated B3N4H7 monoamino-borazine configuration, with RKS PBE0/def2SVP and no solvent or operating cycle. Primary full-vector derivative convergence failed and the center did not qualify as a molecular minimum. The [E843 tighter-SCF attempt](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/borazine-amino-tight-scf-v1/report.md) stopped after point 3/10: two nuclear gradients completed, the third SCF failed admission, and seven points were unattempted. The [E844 diagnostic](../../../discovery-loop/experiments/borazine-amino-scf-terminal-diagnostic-v1/report.md) explains the failed termination conjunction but preserves `minimum_qualified=false` and an independent metadata-audit finding (504/505, not all-pass). These are numerical qualification failures, not evidence that B–N chemistry is impossible; neither the successful software checks nor tighter orbital residuals supply physical persistence or life.

The highest-value additions to the new dossier are the NaClO3 structural comparator, the two inheritance controls, the B–N failure chain, and the counterion/finite-capacity constraints. No earlier report supports a life probability, a unique winning compound, an autonomous chemical reader, or transferring known component properties to a new mixed-metal formula.

Skipped: exhaustive recursive history, fresh simulation, external source re-acquisition and physical measurements. Risk: a catalogue of individually plausible functions can conceal incompatible operating conditions and missing causal transmission into new material.
