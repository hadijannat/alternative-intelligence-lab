# Carbon-free alternative scaffolds: bounded primary-source screen

Search date: 2026-10-15. Scope: Si–O, B–N, P–N, sulfur/S–N, mineral and ionic structural encoding. This is a materials shortlist, not a discovery of life or a probability ranking. **No reviewed source demonstrates renewal, transmissible functional alternatives, and autonomous organization in one carbon-free system.** Known substances below are not novel compounds. Proposed directions have unverified novelty.

## Three useful directions

| Priority for investigation | Specific family and supported property | Proposed direction, explicitly untested | First failure criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1: directly test seed dependence | Na/K aluminosilicate frameworks: CHA, with LTA and MER controls; nominal framework `(Na,K)x[AlxSiyO2(x+y)]·nH2O` | Compare two chemically characterized seed populations under an identical inorganic feed. Ask whether framework/cation differences alter ion selectivity and survive substantial replacement of founder material. Cation ratio is a measurable coordinate, not a new molecule. | Newly deposited material converges to the same feed-selected composition or function, while only old seed cores retain differences. |
| 2: structural-memory negative/positive controls | Elemental sulfur, ring-rich S8 versus amorphous polymeric-chain sulfur Am-SP | Map whether existing chain fraction changes incorporation of fresh sulfur under the same imposed history; distinguish seed effects from compression-rate history and founder carryover. | Chain differences disappear during the operating cycle, or new sulfur acquires the same state regardless of the seed. |
| 3: narrowly exploratory Si–O chemistry | Carbon-free idealized hydrogen silsesquioxane `(HSiO1.5)n`, allowing measured silanol and network defects | Test whether cage/network topology biases subsequent deposition from a carbon-free precursor mixture while preserving a reproducible material function. This is conditional on verifying the full preparation and carbon budget. | Water-mediated alteration/condensation erases topology, or apparent function requires organic passivation. |

The first direction has the clearest published renewal-from-feed evidence. The second has stronger measured persistence but a severe pressure envelope. The third has controllable preparation-dependent structure, but weaker evidence for carbon-independent processing and no demonstrated copying. These ranks concern the next falsifiable measurement, not likelihood of life or intelligence.

## Evidence that limits the interpretation

**Aluminosilicates.** A primary LTA study uses Na+ as inorganic directing ions and directly images growth; transient Al-rich material with Si/Al about 0.7 evolves toward a surface value about 1.1. Thus, compositional contrast can disappear during crystallization. [Kumar et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-04296-4). A separate CHA study reuses products as seeds for two successive growth rounds. CHA topology persists, while particle size, Si/Al and Na content increase and K content decreases. That is valuable serial-growth evidence and simultaneously a warning against calling topology retention faithful compositional inheritance. Its feed contains inorganic reagents, but the FAU starter's preparation is delegated to an external synthesis reference; polymer vessels and external NMR standards are apparatus, not demonstrated functional components. High alkalinity can dissolve CHA seeds and yield MER. [Kadja et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1039/C9RA10825D). Neither paper supplies matched functional phenotypes transmitted into newly formed daughter material.

**Sulfur.** Pure-chain sulfur glass persists across 5–573 K at 5 GPa in the reported measurements. On pressure release, approximately 57% chains remain at room temperature versus 75% after release at 173 K; these are structural fractions, not lifetime estimates. Its synthesis requires a particular pressure history, and diamond apparatus does not imply carbon in the material. Source data and structural supplements are linked by the paper. [Shi et al., published 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55028-w). Conversely, dissolved inorganic polysulfide chain lengths equilibrate on an approximately 10-second timescale after the reported pH jump. Aqueous S4/S5 speciation is therefore a useful memory-erasure control, not a durable alphabet without further stabilization. [Kamyshny et al., 2003](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:AQUA.0000029023.07252.c3).

**Hydrogen silsesquioxane.** A 2025 primary study reports 15 precursor formulations and preparation-dependent cage/network composition, including trichlorosilane/water mixtures without methanol. Its eventual optical endpoint uses alkyl-passivated silicon nanocrystals, which fails a strict carbon-free functional-system criterion. The parent synthesis and the optical endpoint must remain separate evidence classes. Full SI retrieval failed in this screen, so precise impurity and washing details are unresolved. [Jabrayilov et al., 2025](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5DT01479D). A different HSQ study observes gas evolution and Si–H cleavage when its gel is washed with water or dilute HCl, but that preparation uses organic templates. This is a mechanism warning, not a universal measured lifetime for carbon-free HSQ. [2015 primary study](https://doi.org/10.1039/C4NJ01762E).

## Routes not promoted to the shortlist

| Route | Primary evidence and exclusion from a near-term inheritance claim |
|---|---|
| B–N “carbonless DNA” | Quantum-chemical designs and selected higher-level method checks exist. Synthesis, hydrolytic lifetime, copying and sequence-dependent catalysis were not established. An optimized structure is insufficient evidence for a viable chemical alphabet. [Skurski and Brzeski, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4CP04410J). |
| Carbon-free polyborazylene | Borazine-derived material is real; the authors' experimental synthesis compendium reports rapid hydrolysis in solution and continued crosslinking during storage. Organic substituents used to improve processing invalidate a strict carbon-free claim when essential. Dry B/N/H topology could be an exploratory variable, but compatible replenishment and fidelity remain unmeasured. [Primary 1995 paper](https://doi.org/10.1021/cm00058a028), [author experimental procedure, chapter 39](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/jbuss/wp-content/uploads/sites/811/2020/08/inorganic-synthesis32.pdf). |
| P–N halophosphazenes `(NPF2)n`, `(NPCl2)n` | Carbon-free backbones and halogen substituents exist. Primary work on `(NPF2)n` reports chain cleavage during amine substitution and implicates HF attack and residual P–F hydrolysis. The stable substituted examples there use organic groups; their stability cannot be assigned to a carbon-free analog. Mixed-halogen sequence copying is an untested hypothesis. [Allcock et al., 1979, author institution abstract](https://pure.psu.edu/en/publications/synthesis-of-open-chain-polydifluorophosphazene-and-its-reactions). |
| Polythiazyl `(SN)x` | A carbon-free conducting polymer is established. Its classic preparation includes organic processing; conductivity and persistence do not demonstrate growth coupled to copied function. It remains a control for the distinction between an electronic material and a renewing lineage. [Mikulski et al., 1975](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja00855a012). |
| Inorganic liquid-crystal orientation | Vanadium-oxide gels have measured orientational organization, but this screen did not acquire primary evidence that the orientation regenerates into new material after serial transfer under identical fields. Do not count alignment imposed by a field as inherited information. [Primary synthesis study identified](https://doi.org/10.1021/la9914155); full text not acquired. |
| Organic-counterion silicate cages | The experimentally studied cubic silicate `[Si8O20]8−` is paired with tetramethylammonium. The paper attributes protection of siloxane bridges to the counterion arrangement. It cannot support a strict carbon-free stability claim without an inorganic-counterion control. [2019 primary study](https://doi.org/10.1039/C8CP05682J). |

## What would count as a better result

Use identical feed, energy history and exchange conditions for competing seed states; measure the composition and function of **new material**, using an inorganic isotopic label or independent founder-mass accounting. Hold out whole feed compositions, cycle histories and independent batches, rather than individual measurements from a shared trajectory. Compare intact seeds with dissolved seeds, composition-matched unstructured material, no-seed controls and pooled-reset controls. Track cumulative founder dilution. A material can preserve a shape while losing the causal feature that controls function.

This screen provides no kinetic fit: the papers measure different chemistries and envelopes. Combining their constants into a single “non-carbon organism” model would create an unsupported hybrid. There is no justified life/intelligence score, no claim of molecular novelty, and no performed synthesis.

## Local artifacts and limits

`evidence.json` is the structured ledger. `raw/` contains full-text XML and extracted text for the LTA, CHA and sulfur-glass papers, plus retrieval metadata where acquired. `manifest.json` hashes those retained files. Publisher access failures are retained in the ledger; successful metadata access is not presented as full-text inspection.

Validation: JSON parsing, unique IDs, required fields and SHA-256 checks are recorded in `checks.json`. Skipped: exhaustive novelty/patent searching, new simulations and physical synthesis. Main risk: treating stable structure, repeated seeded crystallization or conductivity as inheritance of function.
