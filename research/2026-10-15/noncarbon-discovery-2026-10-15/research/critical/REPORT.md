# Critical chemistry and claims review

Review date: 2026-10-15. Scope: independent assessment of the proposed Mo–W–V composition, compatibility of the investigated inorganic subsystems, and the inference from database properties to life or intelligence. This review does not report a synthesis or a new physical simulation.

**Conclusion:** the proposed `[Mo(VI)34 W(VI) V(V) O112(H2O)16]9−` is a formally consistent composition hypothesis. There is presently no demonstrated structure, solution identity, autocatalysis, inheritance, or intelligence for that composition. Its formal one-electron counterpart can be written with V(IV) and charge 10−; the electron's actual location is unestablished. No integrated candidate clears the necessary evidence gates below. This is an insufficient-evidence result, not an impossibility theorem.

## What the formula does and does not specify

The proposed oxidized charge is `34×6 + 6 + 5 − 112×2 = −9`; changing V(V) to V(IV) gives −10. Neutral aqua ligands do not affect this arithmetic. Protonation, deprotonation, different hydration, counterion association, or loss of an oxide ligand would change the actual solution formula. Formal charge consistency does not establish a stable molecular geometry.

The composition replaces two Mo centers of the parent Mo36 formula. It leaves both substitution sites, their coordination environments, the structural isomer, and possible positional disorder unspecified. The solution may instead contain separate molybdate, tungstate and vanadate species, another cluster topology, or a distribution of products. A bulk Mo:W:V ratio of 34:1:1 would not resolve these alternatives. Likewise, a crystal structure alone would not establish that the active species remains intact in the operating solution.

Mixed-addenda POM redox behavior is already established in other structures. A primary DFT study of Keggin and Dawson anions shows that metal identity and orbital distribution affect reduction and proton affinity. Its results support investigating electronic structure; they do not transfer a particular redox localization or stability to this proposed Mo36 derivative. [López, Bo and Poblet, 2002](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja020407z).

Primary Mo–Nb–V–Te work additionally reports Raman changes *consistent with possible* V substitution in Mo36−xNbx solution anions. That tentative assignment is relevant prior art for mixed-metal Mo36 chemistry, not proof of the present Mo34WV structure. A search without an exact-formula hit cannot establish novelty, and “first substituted Mo36” would be an unsupported claim. [Primary author manuscript and SI](https://pure.mpg.de/pubman/item/item_1849723_5/component/file_1854326/Movte_Raman_Angewandte_and_SI.pdf).

## Redox is a possible failure channel, not a guaranteed memory mechanism

The parent Mo36 study reports seed-dependent formation and a strongly reduced condition in which Mo36 forms and decomposes in less than a second. The reducing condition affects more than 60% of Mo centers. It therefore demonstrates a destructive redox regime, not that a single added electron necessarily destroys Mo34WV. Conversely, the parent's autocatalysis cannot be assumed to survive substitution, redox cycling, a different pH, or confinement. [Miras et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1921536117).

A reversible current or color change can arise from interconversion, adsorption, precipitation, slow equilibration or an electrode artifact. Calling the redox state “memory” requires a measured retained state and readout under specified conditions. Calling it adaptive behavior additionally requires causal use of that state to improve a prespecified task under an unseen challenge. Neither follows from a hysteresis loop. A single ingredient may contribute to a driven network; a compound formula cannot specify the energy source, reaction organization, boundary, feedback or inherited function of an entity.

## Falsifiable gates, without a weighted “life score”

1. **Identity and compatibility.** Establish species-resolved identity and oxidation state in the exact common medium, before and after the proposed transport/energy cycle. Resolve free, wall-associated and precipitated fractions. Reject the fixed-species model if the intended active cluster is not present or does not survive its required cycle. A mass spectrum or elemental ratio alone is insufficient.
2. **Active retention and renewal.** Measure total metal balance together with a standardized function assay on each fraction. Separately estimate loss, irreversible deactivation and regeneration. Compare with a passive adsorption/precipitation model. Reject “retained active catalyst” if retained metal lacks activity or activity comes only from fresh external replacement.
3. **New functional descendants.** Use compositionally distinguishable founders or a validated label and distinguish founder carryover from newly assembled product. At matched feed, test whether different parent states predict the identity and specific function of *new* products. Include no-seed, inactive-seed and composition-matched controls. Persistence of the original seed, a higher total yield, or faster formation of the same equilibrium product is not functional inheritance.
4. **Energy coupled to organization.** Account for reagent and electrical work, and interrupt the claimed coupling while matching material supply. Test whether that interruption removes maintained selectivity, renewal or another defined organizational function. Separate contemporaneous voltage/current measurements under load from isolated maxima. An externally driven gradient is a resource, not evidence that the candidate uses it for maintenance.
5. **Retained seed dependence.** Freeze the observation model and complete-cycle predictions before withholding entire seed/medium/transfer conditions. Track new active production and parent-conditioned differences separately from accumulated reporter or retained mass. Compare with carryover-only and seed-independent assembly baselines. Failure to exceed the propagated uncertainty of those baselines is a retained negative result.
6. **Adaptive use of state.** Only after a functional state is measured, prespecify an input–action task and an unseen change in the input relation. Compare history-dependent performance with matched-energy, matched-state and open-loop controls; interrupt the proposed memory-to-action connection. Hysteresis, morphology, repeated switching or a fitted readout alone does not pass this gate. No source reviewed here establishes it for an autonomous non-carbon entity.

Numerical acceptance limits must be tied to assay resolution and a frozen hypothesis before collecting or fitting the decisive data. Inventing broad rate priors and then obtaining a successful simulated organism would not constrain the proposed chemistry.

## Smallest useful computational work with present data

The existing Mo36 observations can constrain parent-system assembly curves and test complete-condition prediction, subject to their source metadata and lack of a replicate error model. They cannot estimate a W/V substitution effect, a mutation rate or a hereditary generation time. The [existing measured-kinetics report](../../../metal-oxide/docs/published-model.md) already preserves these boundaries; reusing its audited extraction is preferable to fabricating a new candidate trajectory.

The 1,000-entry JARVIS screen can reproducibly check composition exclusions, source IDs, units, missing values, duplicate structures and declared property filters. It cannot validate a life or intelligence endpoint because those labels and measurements are absent. Negative formation energy, a small bandgap or a polar structure must not be converted into a probability of life. Nor do database records count as independent research agents.

Before an atomistic simulation of Mo34WV, a parent structure with explicit atom sites, candidate substitution isomers, charge/protonation alternatives and an appropriate solvent treatment would be needed. An unconstrained formula-to-property prediction would hide the central uncertainty. No such calculation was performed in this review.

The most informative new dataset is therefore a same-medium, species-resolved retention/renewal and parent-to-new-product experiment. The existing literature supplies useful controls and exclusion conditions, but not the parameters needed to simulate that experiment as an established life-like network.
