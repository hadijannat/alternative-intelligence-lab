# Inorganic material memory: evidence, candidates and exclusions

Review date: 2026-10-15. This is a primary-literature review, not an experimental discovery or a fitted material model. No reviewed compound has demonstrated autonomous non-carbon life, functional heredity, or general intelligence.

**Recommendation:** prioritize the known **protonated tungsten oxide/hydrate family, HxWO3·nH2O**, for a chemistry–transport memory module. Treat aqueous **phosphomolybdate or silicotungstate** as a separate, soluble proton/electron reservoir candidate. Their combination is a testable system hypothesis; neither component, their generic combination, nor simple Mo/V/Nb doping can be claimed as a novel compound from this review. The competing tendency to discharge stored reducing equivalents into catalysis is central to the model, not a nuisance to omit.

## What the evidence establishes

| Family | Primary evidence and conditions | What transfers; what does not |
|---|---|---|
| WO3 / HxWO3 | A 2020 three-terminal device tuned conductance across seven orders of magnitude, with 1,000 programmed states and >20,000 programming pulses. Its 50 nm oxide channel, Au contacts and PdHx reservoir used **Nafion**, a carbon-containing polymer. Retention depended on interrupting the electron return path; oxygen ingress and proton loss were recognized failure modes. [Yao et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-16866-6) | Proton occupancy and W reduction provide a physically grounded state variable. The full device is not carbon-free, and its retention is not a property that can be assigned to freely suspended WO3 particles. |
| WO3 with inorganic phosphosilicate glass (PSG) | The 2022 all-inorganic active stack used Pd/PdHx, PSG, WO3 and metal contacts. It reached 5 ns programming with roughly +10/−8.5 V pulses and fields near 1 V/nm. Nanofabrication, imposed voltages and a protected electronic pathway were essential. The paper also reports a pulse-duration-dependent breakdown regime. [Onen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abp8064) | Establishes inorganic programmable memory, not aqueous autonomous learning. Neither nanosecond speed nor retention constants are transferable to a mineral suspension. |
| WO3·nH2O, n = 0, 1, 2 | Aqueous experiments in 0.5 M H2SO4 link proton occupancy, hydration and hydrogen-evolution activity. The order was monohydrate > anhydrous > dihydrate. Bulk inserted-proton participation in hydrogen evolution is proposed using electrochemistry, diffraction and DFT. The study uses carbon-containing electrode construction, so only the active oxide is carbon-free. [Spencer et al.](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4SC00102H) | Directly relevant coupled transport/reaction evidence. A strong proton store can become a catalytic discharge pathway. It is not a demonstrated nonvolatile aqueous memory device. |
| Mo-, V- and Nb-doped WO3 | A 2026 study already explores these substitutions. Mo6+ and V5+ shift and broaden proton-insertion responses; Nb5+ suppresses insertion without the same peak broadening. Defect compensation, hydrogen binding and structural transitions matter. [Holzapfel et al.](https://doi.org/10.1039/D5SC08564K) | Useful measured perturbations and negative results. Doping is not automatically beneficial; arbitrary dopant fractions must not be labeled novel or stable without composition and phase evidence. |
| Ag2S/Ag | Ag-ion redistribution and silver filament formation give stimulus-dependent conductance. A later primary study uses 200 × 200 nm devices with Ag/Pt contacts and electrical programming; thin filaments relax, thicker ones persist. Its motion-learning result is a system-level simulation built from measured device behavior. [Ohno et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nmat3054); [La Barbera et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep39216) | Genuine ionic/material memory, useful as a comparator. Device geometry, electrode reservoirs and field-driven redox must be reproduced; no evidence here supports inheritable memory in freely moving sulfide particles. |
| VO2 | Electrically accessed structural history was tracked beyond 10,000 s; the reported devices extended down to 50 nm channels. [Samizadeh Nikoo et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41928-022-00812-z) Pt inclusions separately increased a switching-history timescale by 33.3-fold in a device study. [Jo et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32081-x) | Strong solid-state memory controls. Metallic transitions and the fabrication environment differ from aqueous redox self-assembly. Neither paper establishes a solution-compatible replicating material. |
| NiO | Pt-contacted oxide films showed habituation and sensitization under imposed reducing/oxidizing gas stimuli, with principal gas-cycle measurements at 200 °C; room-temperature UV effects were also reported. The gas protocol and film defect state govern the response. An associated unsupervised-learning example is an algorithmic simulation inspired by the material. [Zhang et al.](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2017239118) | Important chemical-stimulus comparator, because the response is not merely voltage storage. Hot H2/ozone cycling is incompatible with simply reusing an aqueous POM compartment model. Chemical adaptation is not self-maintenance, reproduction or cognition. |
| NbO2 | A 2024 robot demonstration uses Pt/NbO2/Ti threshold devices, resistors, capacitors, DC supplies, LiDAR and designed control circuitry. [Yang et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-48399-7) | A real engineered sensorimotor system; it does not show that NbO2 itself is an autonomous or intelligent organism. Volatile threshold response is not hereditary state. |
| Fe3O4 | Room-temperature potentiation/depression was demonstrated in Pt/Fe3O4/FTO devices under imposed voltage sweeps and pulses. [Fe3O4 device study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.matlet.2023.134431) | A mineral-compatible research lead with a much weaker bridge to the proposed aqueous memory module. Mixed-valence iron alone is insufficient: phase, interface, oxidation and dissolution must be measured. Device data cannot establish aqueous-state retention. |

“Memory” in these papers has a specific observable: conductance, switching latency, proton content, defect population, or a response to a later stimulus. These are useful ingredients. Consciousness, biological intelligence, self-reproduction and evolutionary heredity are different hypotheses and have not been measured here.

## Two nominated chemistry families

1. **HxWO3·nH2O, n = 0, 1, 2.** Track insertion level x, hydration n, structure, electronic connectivity and surface reaction rates. Begin with known undoped phases; use already published Mo/V/Nb substitutions only as measured comparators. This is the strongest bridge between aqueous ionic transport, persistent material state, and a readout. It remains a candidate module, not a new compound or an organism. The reversible insertion reaction is `WO3 + x H+ + x e− ⇌ HxWO3`; water uptake/loss and phase conversion require their own accounting when they occur.

2. **Carbon-free Keggin proton/electron buffers**, particularly phosphomolybdate `[PMo12O40]3−` (acid form H3PMo12O40) and silicotungstate `[SiW12O40]4−`. The first was directly demonstrated as an electron-coupled-proton buffer for temporally separated water-splitting reactions. [Symes and Cronin](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchem.1621) A later primary study shows that changing the POM changes useful electron-storage and release behavior. [POM buffer study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6851869/) These are chemically grounded reservoirs; their redox history is not yet functional memory. A freely diffusing mediator may equalize compartments and erase state just as efficiently as it supplies energy.

An oxide plus dissolved POM should be represented as two chemical phases with interfacial electron/proton exchange. Calling an unspecified mixture a newly discovered compound would conceal the missing structure, stoichiometry, phase and novelty evidence. Previously demonstrated POM electronic memories also exist, including 2014 MOS-integrated clusters and 2019 redox-based switching; neither proves unassisted aqueous information inheritance. [Busche et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13951), [RSC primary study](https://doi.org/10.1039/C8NH00366A)

## Compatibility constraints and model implications

These are deductions to test, rather than measured constants to import:

- **pH and speciation:** the most relevant tungsten data use strong aqueous acid. Match the POM's verified pH/speciation window before combining systems. Silicate precipitation, alkaline mineral growth and acidic insertion data are not interchangeable operating environments.
- **Separate transport paths:** H+ crossing an aqueous gap does not by itself insert a neutral hydrogen equivalent. Charge balance requires an electron path or a chemically explicit redox shuttle. Do not model free electrons as an aqueous diffusing reagent without a justified mechanism.
- **Retention versus function:** the same mobile reduced species or inserted hydrogen that stores state can react, leak or equilibrate. Increasing exchange can amplify unseeded background and destroy compartment contrasts. A model must include these routes rather than treating all exported charge as active, permanently retained information.
- **Counterions and boundaries:** an anionic POM, proton, metal cation and neutral product generally have different partitioning and mobility. A single species-independent leak factor lacks support. A mineral barrier must have measured integrity and selectivity in the chosen acid/redox conditions.
- **Readout can change state:** conductance measurement and electrochemical probing supply current or alter potential. Separate passive optical/structural observation from a programming pulse, and account for charge passed during readout.
- **Carbon-free accounting:** report active inorganic phase, electrode/binder/electrolyte, and whole apparatus separately. Carbon-free WO3 does not make a Nafion/carbon-black electrode carbon-free. Conversely, carbon used in fabrication is not automatically an essential retained active component; establish its removal rather than guessing.

A minimal coupled model would have an internal inserted-equivalent inventory `q`, exterior mediator oxidation states, finite proton/ion reservoirs, and a transport boundary. Its balance is `dq/dt = insertion − extraction − catalytic discharge − leakage`, with each term charge-balanced and tied to the actual electrochemical potential. A separately fitted `G(q, phase, hydration)` maps state to readout. It should not infer a reaction rate from a different paper's electrical switching time. If the energy supply stops, the model must predict relaxation or a documented metastable state rather than free maintenance.

For inheritance, also track newly formed material and its state after transfer. Carryover of a precharged founder particle is persistence; a descendant that recreates a seed-dependent functional state in fresh material is a substantially stronger test. Neither is established by a large hysteresis loop.

## Validation consequence

No material-specific held-out test has been run in this review. A valid next test must choose one architecture and dataset, freeze the state equations and extraction rules, fit only the training voltage/time/composition conditions, and evaluate complete held-out trajectories or compositions. Splitting adjacent points from the same programming trace would overstate generalization. Report charge conservation, zero-input relaxation, seed/background contrast, and failure to transfer between hydration states. Published aggregate performance is not independent validation of a new compound.

The minimal negative controls are an unprogrammed material, an inert/non-inserting control, a thermal-history control, a disconnected electron path, and a well-mixed transport limit. Demonstrating storage without self-maintenance, or state persistence without copying, remains a negative result for the stronger life hypothesis.

No synthesis, device fabrication, patent search, wet experiments or new numerical fit was performed. The major unresolved risk is that stronger chemical coupling accelerates discharge and equilibration, erasing the very state it is intended to preserve.
