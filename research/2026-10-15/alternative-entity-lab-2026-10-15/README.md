# Alternative entity laboratory

Start with [REPORT.md](REPORT.md): a completed bounded investigation of a fueled, adaptive compartment informed by the alternative-intelligence-lab archive.

Continuation: [measured chemistry coupling](../measured-coupling-2026-10-15/REPORT.md) adds two source-grounded kinetic/transfer laboratories. It preserves the failed RNA prediction and the unidentifiability of functional inheritance; it does not change this suite's accepted results.

- [Maintenance](maintenance/results-v1/report.md): finite material and work accounting, 48 trajectories.
- [Functional transmission](heredity/results-v1/REPORT.md): conservative partition and finite paid synthesis, 432 trajectories.
- [Integrated adaptation](integrated/PROTOCOL.md): one maintained lineage, observed resource feedback, cue reversal, actual descendant partition and controls, 360 accepted trajectories. [Accepted results](integrated/results-v1.2/summary.json).
- [Measured component data](research/REPORT.md): ten primary studies reviewed, chemical and physical observations acquired and analyzed.
- [Verification](checks.md), [independent review](review.md), and [resume state](progress.md).

These are computational laboratories and measured-data reanalyses. No living organism, general intelligence or compatible physical integration has been demonstrated. The model supplies learning, copying and division rules.

From this directory:

```sh
/USER_HOME/.pyenv/versions/3.12.3/bin/python3 run_suite.py --output verification-new
```

Use a new directory. The command is offline, tests all modules in separate processes, reruns all three simulations and data analysis, and compares results with accepted outputs. It refuses to overwrite an existing run. Test environment: Python 3.12.3, NumPy 2.4.1, matplotlib 3.10.8, pytest 8.4.2. Figures can be rebuilt with `python build_figures.py` in that environment.

The public source baseline is commit `73d0d656a1f0f51ebbb59ba4c9cc4c7f8ea480d4`. The publication checkout and historical experiments were not modified. Initial integrated v1 and v1.1 are retained as documented in [the correction record](integrated/AMENDMENT-v1.1.md); use v1.2 for conclusions. Replayed or corrected calculations are not additional independent evidence.
