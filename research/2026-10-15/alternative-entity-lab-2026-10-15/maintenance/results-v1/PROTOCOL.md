# Maintenance v1: finite supplied fuel and labelled renewal

This preregistration is frozen with config.json, model.py, test_model.py and run_lab.py before the official run. Twelve new seeds (2026101500 through 2026101511) are paired across four arms. No search, selection, or tuning on their outcomes is allowed. The seeds were absent in prior claude-run, discovery-loop, cco, metal-oxide and noncarbon JSON, Markdown and Python files when checked before launch. Other laboratories may intentionally reuse these seeds for paired integrated comparisons; novelty means unused in prior campaigns, not statistical independence between laboratories.

The hypothesis is conditional: a supplied catalyst/renewal rule with finite activated substrate can replace decaying founder material while paying a specified work demand in this abstract model. No identified chemical reaction, measured rate, standard free energy, autonomous origin, actual organism, replication, cognition, or physical feasibility is tested.

Material units are fungible conserved tokens. Stored work is a separate arbitrary unit. All quantities and rates in config.json are analyst-selected assumptions. A surviving structural token supplies catalytic capacity. Over one step: founder and newly created structure decay exponentially into waste; stored work leaks exponentially into dissipation; a bounded amount of supplied fuel is converted; that conversion releases a fixed assumed work yield; up to the configured material yield becomes new structure if its explicit work cost can be paid; remaining converted material becomes waste; requested task work is capped by the remaining store. Renewal costs enter dissipation and external task payments enter spent. Waste is retained in the accounting boundary and cannot be recycled.

The exact numeric invariants are fuel + waste + structure = initial_material + fed_total, and energy + spent + dissipated = initial_energy + energy_generated. Each converted fuel token releases energy_yield work units, so no work arises without initial stored work or consumed fuel. The external reservoir is independently debited for every accepted influx; reservoir + fed_total = initial reservoir. The source's chemical preparation energy is not modelled. `structure = founder_structure + new_structure` prevents survival of supplied initial stock from being called regeneration. `new_structure` means current surviving product; repeated renewal does not count destroyed product twice.

The normal arm receives noisy feed with identical precomputed schedules across arms. The no_feed arm receives zero, keeping its external reservoir unused. The no_renewal arm converts fuel for work but sends every converted material token to waste. The extra_leak arm has three times the structural decay rate and identical feed/work. Initial inventories, step count, work demand, and seed-specific feed schedule are otherwise matched. A different flux or altered kinetic rule requires a separately labelled version.

Each run lasts 600 steps of 0.1 arbitrary time units, with an external reservoir of 30 tokens. At the endpoint every seed must meet all config.json criteria: normal structure at least 0.9, new structure at least 0.75, founder fraction at most 0.25, and at least 95 percent of requested work paid; no_feed and no_renewal final structures no more than half paired normal; extra_leak no more than 80 percent paired normal; every material/energy/reservoir residual at most 1e-9. All 12 seeds must pass for `SUPPORTED_WITHIN_PHENOMENOLOGICAL_MODEL`; any failed endpoint gives `UNSUPPORTED_WITHIN_THIS_PROTOCOL`. Invalid ledgers or missing runs give `INVALID_RUN`, never a scientific pass.

Retain every step including initial state, feed schedule, payments, labels and ledgers in CSV, per-seed endpoints and decision in JSON, a report, input copies, source hashes, environment and runtime. The CLI refuses an existing output directory. Run unit tests before freezing and launching. Maximum expected runtime is under two minutes. A deterministic replay establishes implementation repeatability, not independent experimental replication.

The model uses an explicit sequential time split. Exponential decay is exact in isolation; coupled conversion/renewal is a first-order discretisation. A separate deterministic dt/2 sensitivity run, outside official replicate decisions, is required for reported numeric stability. This is numerical verification, not chemical calibration.

Prior code inspected: discovery-loop/experiments/liquid-metal-fuel-budget-v1/model.py (atom/electron accounting) and discovery-loop/experiments/sulfur-nitrogen-budget-v1/model.py (finite replacement reserve). Their invariants motivate separate material/work accounting here. This model is a different phenomenological integration interface; it does not supersede their chemical bounds.
