## Completion checks

1. **Audit prior evidence**: exact repository revision, prior failure mechanisms and reused methodological ideas recorded. Complete.
2. **Develop laboratories**: finite-resource maintenance, stochastic functional transmission, and integrated resource-paid adaptation/descendant transfer; each has a frozen protocol/configuration, matched controls, raw outputs and meaningful tests. Complete.
3. **Review and analyze online data**: primary-source review, acquired chemical and physical numerical datasets, license/provenance/hash, reproducible analysis and explicit calibration limits. Complete.
4. **Execute and verify**: fixed seeds, no outcome-based tuning, bad-input checks, 33 tests, ten fresh-process replay comparisons and independent review. Complete; implementation corrections and prior outputs retained.
5. **Deliver findings**: REPORT.md, three inspected figures, reproduction command, measurement decisions, checks.md and progress.md. Complete.

## Evidence and resource boundaries

- This is a local predeclared computational construction, not independent scientific preregistration.
- Model parameters are investigator-selected unless tied explicitly to a measured observation and units.
- Supplied feedback, catalytic rules, partitioning and division do not demonstrate natural emergence.
- Model persistence, functional transmission, adaptive behavior, Darwinian evolution and intelligence receive separate verdicts.
- Preserve all frozen primary outcomes, including failures. A correction requires a recorded amendment and retained previous results.
- Bounded CPU runs use existing Python 3.12.3, NumPy/SciPy/matplotlib/pytest; no paid services, publication, deployment or physical experiments.
- Completion means a reproducible bounded investigation, not creation of a physical organism or proof of intelligence.

## Reusable procedure

Read the publication and relevant negative results; freeze a measurable construction with controls; test conservation and information access; execute once; replay; audit claims; choose the next physical discriminating measurement rather than tune away a negative result.
