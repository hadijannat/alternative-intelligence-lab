Our inference for the recursive search: a room-temperature acidic parent comparator has stronger support than a heated one. A proton activity alone does not specify an operating medium: total metal, acid dilution, Na/K/NH4 counterions, nitrate/chloride, redox state and precipitation must also match. The earlier Dawson persistence evidence in phosphate at nominal pH3/4 supplies no observation in these Mo36 preparations. It remains a proposed persistence comparator, not a verified energy or inheritance complement.

The next chemical discriminator is a species-resolved sequence under one fully recorded preparation: characterized parent alone; parent with W alone; parent with V alone; then the nominal Mo:W:V34:1:1 feed comparison, including precursor-only and seed-erased controls. Ratios describe feed hypotheses, not proven substitution. Only after identifying an intact active target should Dawson compatibility be tested against its separate-component controls. An observed new mass, retained total Mo/W/V or favorable isolated electronic energy cannot by itself admit the requested structure or inherited function. These are proposed measurements; no outcome is filled in.

## Candidate-role 18: niobium precursor for structural persistence

[Sanchez et al., 2012](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201200746), supporting information sections1.2,4.1 and4.2 (combined PDF pages6-7,11-12), uses NH4[NbO(C2O4)2]·9.7H2O with ammonium heptamolybdate. The reported Mo concentration is0.33M, Nb/Mo0.12, temperature448K and pressure9.5-10bar, with105minutes before adding vanadyl sulfate. A930cm-1 Raman band is assigned to dissolved {Mo36-xNbxO112}; an812cm-1 band accompanies nanocrystalline oxide formation. Adding V/Mo0.25 and holding another105minutes shifts both bands; the authors give tentative substitution interpretations. The [combined author manuscript and SI](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_1849723/component/file_1854326/content) was downloaded and its relevant text checked locally.

This is an **unverified structural-persistence comparator**, rejected for admission as a demonstrated complement to the unchanged Mo34WV target. Raman assignments in a reacting suspension do not identify an isolated molecular composition. Nb substitution would change the requested core, while concurrent precipitation can maintain a solution signal through phase equilibrium. The different temperature behavior from Noack2014 does not isolate a causal effect of Nb: preparations, ligands, concentrations and phases differ. Oxalate contains carbon; its ongoing necessity and residual presence would also need measurement. No measured target-compatible stabilization, energy coupling or inheritance follows.

It also reports0.33M. Final solution volume is unspecified, so actual concentration cannot be reconciled by assumption. No kinetic fit or cross-study stability ranking is justified by these entries.
