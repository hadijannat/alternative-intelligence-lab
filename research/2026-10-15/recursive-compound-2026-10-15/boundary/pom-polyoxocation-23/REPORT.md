# Candidate-role 23: a reported precipitation boundary, with finite growth

Points and colleagues demonstrate an all-inorganic chemical garden made from a cationic POM seed and an anionic POM solution. This supports a proposed boundary role and a negative comparator for sustained renewal. It supplies **zero verified integrated-complement admissions** for nominal Mo34WV: that target mixture, intact target retention and activity were not tested. This source assessment awaits independent root review. [Primary article, DOI 10.1039/C5CC09536K](https://doi.org/10.1039/C5CC09536K).

The solid cation is printed as **[epsilon-PMo12O36(OH)4{La(H2O)2.5}4]5+**. The seed salt is also printed as **[epsilon-PMo12O36(OH)4{La(H2O)2.5Cl1.25}4]**, without a net charge; five chloride equivalents are consistent with neutralizing the cation. The aqueous partner is **K8[gamma-SiW10O36]**, containing the 8- anion. The explanatory prose calls the caps {La(H2O)4}3+, whereas the displayed seed formula uses 2.5 waters per La. Preserve both notations; this assessment does not reconcile hydration or solution speciation. Page 3 also prints (OH4) where page 2 uses (OH)4. Species identities here follow page 2. **Locator:** PDF page 2 / journal page 1912, opening line and left-column seed paragraph; page 3 / 1913, La-loss paragraph.

The declared solution-concentration survey is **0.5-30.0 mM**. The text describes tubes with 1-20 mM solutions, clouds below 1 mM, and six morphology regimes: clouds, membranes, slugs, tubes, jetting and budding. Figure 3 labels the cloud column <=1 mM and its adjacent membrane column 1 mM, so a sharp boundary at 1 mM is not imposed here. Crucially, the text reports **no membrane formation or tube growth at 30 mM or above**. The maximum tested value above 30 mM is not specified; the wording does not establish an unlimited experimentally surveyed upper range. **Locator:** page 2 / 1912, concentration-survey paragraphs; Figure 3 on page 3.

Figure 2 shows a 20 mM case at 10, 40, 70 and 220 seconds. The authors describe an approximately **20-minute** growth timescale and explicitly state that growth stops while seed material remains. Remaining material is not evidence of continued renewal. Optical measurements also support the authors' reported relationship between projected crystal and tube areas, and a growth-rate trend with concentration. Figure 4 is a trend guideline; no curve was digitized or fitted here. **Locator:** page 2, Figure 2 and last paragraph; page 3 opening and Figure 4; page 4, Figure 5 and conclusion.

The proposed mechanism is precipitation followed by POM exclusion, osmotic water entry, rupture and continued tube-wall formation. The article's explanation is not a measured precursor-flux or intact-target-retention result. Its termination explanation invokes La loss and earlier NMR/X-ray work cited as reference 19; those earlier measurements and SI were not independently inspected here. **Locator:** page 1, Figure 1B; page 2, mechanism paragraph; page 3, La-loss discussion.

That discussion contains an unresolved interpretation: it reports a higher-than-expected **supernatant La:Mo ratio**, then associates it with greater La incorporation into the precipitate. Without numerical ratios, a defined comparison and a mass balance, the text does not make that inference clear. Neither the compartment nor the ratio direction has been silently changed. Elemental ratios also cannot establish intact active target recovery. **Locator:** page 3 / 1913, left-column paragraph beginning with synthesis of compound 1.

For the page 2 cation only, assuming P(V), La(III), oxide O(-II), four OH(-I) groups and neutral water gives

`5 + sum(Mo) + 4(3) - 36(2) - 4 = 5`, hence `sum(Mo) = 64` and a mean formal Mo state of `16/3`.

This is charge accounting, not a measured distribution of Mo oxidation states or a prediction about target electron transfer.

The printed La-loss sequence also needs a qualification: four La caps and charge 5+ become three caps and 2+, then no La and 5-. Removing four La(III) cations from 5+ while holding core composition, protonation and electrons fixed instead gives **7-**. The printed endpoint therefore has a formal **+2 charge difference** from that restricted model. An unmeasured redox, protonation or composition change, or a notation issue, could account for it. This assessment neither selects a correction nor disproves the reported species, and does not extend the initial-cation Mo calculation to the endpoint. **Locator:** page 3 / 1913, La-loss paragraph.

The assessment leaves pH, precursor/water flux, intact target activity, recovery fraction, its denominator and uncertainty, compatibility, and sustained functional renewal **null**. The source is useful for identifying a different boundary candidate and its failure behavior; it does not meet the protocol's joint target requirements.
