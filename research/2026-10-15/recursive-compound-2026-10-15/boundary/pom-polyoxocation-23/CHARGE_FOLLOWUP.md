## What the charge calculation establishes

Using P(V), La(III), oxide O(-II), four hydroxide groups and neutral aqua ligands, the initial cation requires `sum(Mo) = 64`. If only Mo(V)/Mo(VI) are allowed, that corresponds formally to eight Mo(V) and four Mo(VI); this is an assumption-based count, not a measured site assignment.

Removing four La(III) ions while holding that core's electrons and protonation fixed gives -7. Points prints -5. Neutral water counts of 4 versus 2.5 per La cannot remove this +2 discrepancy.

For comparison only, **if** the associated species were the unprotonated two-electron-reduced `[alpha-PMo12O40]5-` anion, it would require `sum(Mo) = 70`. Comparing that assumed formula with the initial La-free protonated core changes the Mo oxidation sum by +6 and the core proton count by -4, yielding a net +2 charge change. The acquired source does not establish that complete endpoint formula or those transformations. No six-electron oxidation, four-proton release, redox partner or reaction rate is admitted as observed.
