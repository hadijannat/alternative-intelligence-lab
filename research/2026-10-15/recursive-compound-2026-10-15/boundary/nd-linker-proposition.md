# Candidate-role 19: neodymium linkage of the target

**Decision: advance for structural investigation; no verified complement added.** This follows the unverified niobium persistence comparator. The missing function is retention of an intact, active cluster while feed and waste exchange continue.

Izarova et al., [DOI10.1002/ejic.200500484](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejic.200500484), report compound15, (H3O)2[{Nd(H2O)5}2{Mo36O112(H2O)16}]·40H2O, as a two-dimensional coordination polymer from molybdate, hydroxylamine and Nd3+. The publisher-deposited abstract and [SI TableS15, PDFpage9](https://application.wiley-vch.de/contents/jc_2005/2005/i200500484_s.pdf) distinguish its ordinary Mo36 unit from the nitrosyl compounds1-14. TableS15 gives Nd-O2.47(2)-2.66(3)A and Mo-O-Nd151.7(12)-160.9(12)degrees. Exact compound15 feed salt, pH, preparation and CIF were not recovered. The accessible records supply no active-retention, permeability, leaching or renewal result.

Our proposed derivative is the **unverified stoichiometric hypothesis** (H3O)3[{Nd(H2O)5}2{Mo34WVO112(H2O)16}]·nH2O. It keeps the requested target formula and borrows only the observed parent linkage motif. Formal target charge-9 plus two Nd3+ leaves-3, requiring three monovalent positive charges. This accounting does not establish proton locations, Nd coordination, water count, crystal topology, existence or novelty. The lattice-water number n is unknown. Hydronium is a bookkeeping choice based on the parent salt; target protonation could instead change the molecular charge.

This proposal improves on an unspecified adsorbent by identifying a measured linkage to the same parent family. It still has no target coordinates, binding energy, exchange rate or functional validation. A layer is not a closed compartment. Hydroxylamine in the reported parent preparation may alter the proposed target's oxidation states; copying that preparation is not justified by this evidence.

The next discriminator is a structure-resolved target/Nd comparison with the already-characterized target alone and Nd-only controls in the same measured medium. Admit the retention role only if intact target identity and activity persist with quantified target escape, feed entry and waste exit. Challenge dilution and chemical turnover; distinguish persistent founders from newly formed competent material. A precipitate, total-metal uptake or parent crystal alone cannot pass. Conditions and thresholds must be frozen from measured target lifetime/activity and assay uncertainty before any decisive run; none is invented here.

Counterion caution: [Long et al., DOI10.1007/s10876-006-0058-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10876-006-0058-9), author PDF printedpp261-263, reports14 aqua ligands rather than16 because TEAH hydroxyl groups occupy two sites, with sodium-dependent coordination positions. This is a carbon-containing comparator illustrating altered coordination, not a verified noncarbon complement. Its conditions are not transferred to compound15.
