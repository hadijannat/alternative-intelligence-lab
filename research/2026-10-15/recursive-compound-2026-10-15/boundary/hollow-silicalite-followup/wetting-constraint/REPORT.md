# Wetting and defect constraints on the B1 boundary proposal

Status: source-attributed refinement, ready for independent review. New assessed roles: 0. New verified integrated complements: 0. No target experiment, target parameter fit, held-out target validation, or native calculation was performed.

## Independently reopened primary evidence

[Huve et al., New Journal of Chemistry 2020, 44, 273–281](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/nj/c9nj01891c) studied outgassed powders, approximately 200 mg, at room temperature with water or 20 M LiCl. Their TPAOH dissolution–recrystallization preparation differs from Dai's HPW encapsulation preparation; it contains no tested POM or acidic target medium. Walls were approximately 15–20 nm. The precursor was calcined at 550 °C; this does not quantify residual carbon.

## Consequence for the proposed target boundary

The 96 MPa result cannot serve as a minimum pressure required for water to reach a hollow cavity. Conversely, hypothesized spontaneous cavity wetting cannot establish precursor passage or intact target retention. A nominal crystallographic aperture alone cannot distinguish a dry shell, an accessible cavity connected by defects, exterior adsorption, and active confinement. Each possibility requires different observables.

This assessment keeps B1 as an unresolved boundary proposal. None of the source observations is a measurement on `[MoVI34 WVI VV O112(H2O)16]9−`. The LiCl conditions cannot be inserted as parameters of a dilute acidic transport model. Source retention of an element cannot identify intact active catalyst retention. This is an identifiability constraint on future tests, not a validated chemistry–transport model.

## Smallest discriminating measurement set proposed here

All arms must use one documented shell preparation and matched particle-size distribution, loading history, thermal history, medium, temperature, and pressure. The target must first be independently identified and its persistence established in that medium. Run shell-only, free-target, exterior-loaded, and putatively cavity-loaded arms. Quantify the empty-shell analytical background. Include both never-dried and dried/rewetted states where preparation permits; do not treat wetting history as an unrecorded nuisance.

1. **Accessible water volume and exchange.** Measure time-resolved water uptake/exchange at the intended operating pressure, alongside cavity-sensitive localization and an independently measured accessible volume. A bulk uptake curve alone cannot separate interparticle water, wall uptake, and cavity filling. Isotopic water is a possible tracer only after checking isotope exchange and matrix effects; isotope appearance alone is not a route marker. Use matched nonhollow particles as a structural control, acknowledging that their surface chemistry may differ.
2. **Nonreacting solute passage and shell sorption.** For each chemically characterized precursor, measure inlet, outlet, bulk liquid, and shell-associated inventories after a concentration step and a washout step. Include adsorption-only controls and a second mixing/flow condition to reveal external film limitation. Vary concentration in a range independently shown not to alter speciation. These experiments can constrain an effective transport conductance and reversible sorption only when compartments, volume, and analytical recovery are independently resolved.
3. **Intact target and function.** During the same exchange sequence, measure intact target in all recoverable compartments with an identity-sensitive assay, elemental mass balance, and catalytic competence separately. Validate fractionation against known free and exterior-bound controls so separation itself does not create apparent retention or destroy target. Measure free-target degradation in matched medium. A fall in intact target inside the shell can otherwise be leakage, decomposition, inaccessible recovery, or adsorption; a low effluent metal signal does not choose among these.
4. **Route perturbation and local structure.** Compare independently characterized defect populations while preserving or measuring changes in shell chemistry, cavity volume, and wetting. A defect-blocking treatment is informative only if it does not independently change adsorption or precursor speciation. Electron tomography or another local measurement may bound detectable openings, but a nondetection must retain its spatial resolution and sampling limits. Leakage correlated with a controlled defect perturbation can support a route assignment; bulk pressure curves cannot count defects by themselves.

For a conditional two-compartment description, use independently observed volumes and write transport as `J_s = G_s (c_out,s - c_in,s)` only in a verified linear, nonreacting regime. For charged species with appreciable electrical partitioning, the driving force requires electrochemical activities and an independently constrained potential/partition term. Do not absorb those terms into an allegedly transferable diffusion coefficient. Add separately measured sorption and degradation balances when necessary. If cavity concentration cannot be resolved, report the effective external uptake response and its nonidentifiability rather than an internal permeability. Even with cavity concentration, an inferred `G_s` identifies an aggregate conductance; microscopic defect number, radius, and diffusivity remain confounded without structural and route-sensitive evidence.

## Prospective rejection and holdout rules

Freeze the model family and assay-specific thresholds after a calibration pilot establishes detection limits, recovery uncertainty, and time resolution. Reserve complete independently prepared batches and a predefined pressure or flow regime for validation. Keep late-time washout and no-seed/background arms in both sets; never split adjacent time points from one trajectory across training and validation. Publish all exclusions, censored values, failed recoveries, and negative outcomes. If the data cannot separate transport from degradation or adsorption, the outcome is insufficient evidence, not a successful fit.

Admit a boundary complement only after the identified target simultaneously exhibits precursor access, recoverable intact active retention, and performance above matched exterior/free/background controls in held-out batches under the intended medium. Thresholds must be tied to the intended maintenance/turnover demand, which is currently unmeasured; no threshold is invented here. A subsequent seed-transfer experiment must distinguish ongoing target-dependent function from adsorbed carryover and newly nucleated background activity. Boundary performance alone would still not establish inheritance or intelligence.
