Root owns independent source review, decisions and any aggregate update. No source download, simulation, target assay or registration is part of this dossier-writing step.

The shortest paired falsification uses hollow-loaded, exterior-loaded, free-cluster and empty-shell arms under identical medium, exposure and washing conditions. Prespecify detection limits, sampling and the required functional-retention performance before measurement. Follow intact molecular identity and catalytic competence independently of elemental inventory across shell, liquid and effluent; include a measured small-precursor passage control. Test washed-material transfer to fresh medium against no-seed and matched residual-carryover controls. Leakage is judged against the prespecified performance criterion; any detectable leakage does not automatically imply complete functional failure. Loss of molecular identity with persistent reporter activity rejects attributing that activity to intact target, even when elemental retention looks favorable.
