# Receiver-function source audit

Question: does an inorganic material formed on a clean receiver retain independently measured product-forming activity after transfer, and what does that establish about renewal?

Two retrospective source families were selected for their explicit transfer controls: Stracke and Finke 2013 (10.1021/cs400141t), and 2011 (10.1021/ja205569j). The author's 2013 dissertation reproduces the corresponding manuscript chapters; it is not designated the publisher version. Publisher supporting information is acquired separately. Duplicate records from a study remain one evidence family.

Verify source identity, hashes, methods, product-assay location and the negative transfer result. Do not digitize a graph, infer individual atom activity from current, transfer oxygen measurements between separate experiments, or assign unreported rinse carryover as zero. No fitting, held-out target validation or physical experiment is performed. A fresh-feed catalyst control cannot establish founder replacement or inherited function.

This audit adds no candidate-role assessment. The existing 22 assessments and zero verified integrated complements remain unchanged unless genuinely new same-system functional evidence becomes available.
