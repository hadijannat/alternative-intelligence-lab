# Fresh-material function and its limits

**A washed, freshly prepared inorganic deposit has product-level activity evidence. Ongoing target renewal remains unverified.** Stracke and Finke's 2013 cobalt-oxide control deposits material from fresh cobalt nitrate on cleaned glassy carbon, rinses it, transfers it to precursor-free phosphate, and independently measures dissolved oxygen. The deposit has variable oxo/hydroxo composition. Rinse carryover is not quantitatively bounded, and cobalt is lost during the assay, so exclusively surface-bound activity and precise active-site counts are not established. Methods and conditions are in [constraints.json](constraints.json).

The same study's POM-transfer control produces approximately background current after rinsing. This is a current endpoint, not oxygen nondetection. Instability of authentic oxide prevents that negative rinse result from excluding transient heterogeneous catalysis. Its 2011 predecessor retains washed-deposit electrochemical activity, but its oxygen measurements belong to separate precursor-solution experiments. Those endpoints must remain separate. Primary records: [2013 SI](https://doi.org/10.1021/cs400141t.s001), [2011 SI](https://doi.org/10.1021/ja205569j.s001), and the [author's manuscript-format dissertation](https://mountainscholar.org/bitstreams/fb2173ae-02db-4bb6-8085-2687859c3d03/download), Chapters III/IV. The dissertation is not asserted to be the publisher version. Two studies, including their duplicates, constitute two evidence families.

## Activity-equivalent material is not measured conversion

The 2013 manuscript estimates that 0.45-0.58 nmol deposited cobalt at pH8 and 1.0-1.5 nmol at pH5.8 could account for the POM experiment's oxygen output. These are approximate activity-equivalent inventories based on deposition charge, not measured POM conversion or individual-atom competence. Its 2.5 micromolar POM in 1.50 mL contains nominally 15 nmol cobalt. Conditional endpoint arithmetic gives 3.000-3.8667% and 6.6667-10.000%, respectively. The manuscript separately says about4-8%; representative estimates and publisher metadata use slightly different rounded values. Preserve these distinctions rather than treating4-8% as an exact interval or declaring an established source error. Exact inputs, fractions and reported values are retained in the JSON.

This comparison matters because retained precursor and useful activity can coexist with a small active decomposition-product fraction. A target renewal experiment must identify the material responsible for the product, not merely show total-metal retention or current recovery.

## Next discriminating test

The fresh-feed control is a component-supported assay precedent. A renewal test would instead transfer quantified dissolution products to a clean receiver, bound particulate and soluble founder carryover, wash, and assay a chemically identified product in fresh medium. Matched fresh-feed, blank-receiver, no-drive and no-regeneration controls would separate reconstruction from passive transfer. Heredity additionally needs reproducible parent-associated functional variants in new material; none is supplied here. Phosphate conditions and the carbon electrode support are recorded, with no demonstrated compatibility with nominal Mo34WV.
