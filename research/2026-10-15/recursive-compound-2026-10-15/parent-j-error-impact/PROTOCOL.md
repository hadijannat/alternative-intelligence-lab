# Saved-array fixed-density J-error sensitivity

Purpose: assess how the six already reported RI-J differences affect the Coulomb energy evaluated at the same saved cycle-zero density P0. This is a sensitivity calculation on saved arrays, not a relaxed SCF calculation, a chemical-accuracy assessment, a new tolerance or a crash-cause diagnosis. The repeatability result retains five nonpassing comparisons and one passing comparison at absolute tolerance 1e-10, rtol=0.

Use only saved J1.npy, J2.npy and J3.npy from parent-j-repeatability/run-v1, Jold from parent-openmp-first-j-v2/run-v1/J.npy, and P0 from parent-pilot-memory/run-v1/partial.npz. Reuse the immutable first-J-v2 saved_density helper after inspecting its import chain: module initialization imports stdlib helper modules; saved_density loads NumPy arrays and validates them without building a molecule or invoking operators. No native integrals, SCF, PCM build, fitting, tolerance adjustment or new chemical data is allowed.

Orient J3 back to positive density by multiplying it by -1. Preserve all six original comparison names and pass flags. For sign-reversal comparisons the oriented difference is the negative of the original signed difference; its maximum absolute error, Frobenius norm and fixed-threshold exceedance count are unchanged, while the signed energy difference changes orientation. Report the oriented expression explicitly.

For each oriented difference deltaJ calculate deltaE_C = 0.5 sum_ij P0_ij deltaJ_ij directly with math.fsum across all binary64 products, avoiding subtraction of two large total energies. Record maximum absolute entry, first row-major argmax, entries above the unchanged tolerance, Frobenius norm and computed bounds 0.5 ||P0||_1,entry ||deltaJ||_max and 0.5 ||P0||_F ||deltaJ||_F. These are norm-inequality bounds evaluated in floating-point arithmetic, not certified outward-rounded intervals. The products and differences themselves are binary64 approximations. Energy units are Hartree under the saved PySCF AO density/Coulomb-matrix convention.

Read J arrays by read-only mmap, at most 16 rows per difference block. Retain only one full saved P0 and bounded blocks; close each mmap. Check finite float64 square shapes and nonfinite arithmetic. Recompute original maximum/argmax/exceedance/pass results and require exact agreement with the pinned original comparison report before interpreting sensitivity. Any disagreement or malformed/nonfinite input fails this analysis; it never changes a prior result.

AO/element localization is omitted: a full exact AO-to-atom map was not saved in the admitted artifacts. Four saved AO labels do not independently validate a reconstructed full map. No molecule is built to fill this gap.

Freeze run.py, test_run.py, this protocol and all input/helper-source hashes into preparation.json before generating real analysis outputs. Preparation hashes files but does not load or analyze their numerical arrays. Root review must supply independent-root-review.json with status APPROVED_SAVED_ARRAY_ERROR_IMPACT, independent_root_review=true, a nonempty reviewer and the exact preparation_sha256. Only after root review may --analyze run. The implementation task may run tiny synthetic tests and --prepare only. Use a fresh analysis-v1 output directory, preserve failures, and recheck all hashes after analysis. No overwrite or retry is implicit.

Tiny tests cover a known symmetric off-diagonal contraction, conservative norm bounds, negative-J orientation, exact cancellation with accurate summation, malformed shapes and nonfinite inputs. No real parent-array analysis is part of implementation validation.
