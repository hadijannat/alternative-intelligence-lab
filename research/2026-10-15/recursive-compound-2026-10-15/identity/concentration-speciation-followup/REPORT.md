# Concentration can change the identity being measured

Status: source-supported assay refinement; no target identity, fitted parameter or new complement admission.

Maksimovskaya, Bondareva and Aleshina (2008), [DOI 10.1002/ejic.200800500](https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ejic.200800500), reports multinuclear NMR of aqueous Mo/V/Te preparations. Its indexed publisher abstract describes a proposed TeMo3V5O27(5-) solution complex that was not isolated: concentration crystallized TeMo6O24(6-), shifted the remaining solution toward V9TeO28(5-), and redissolution regenerated the former solution complex. Distribution diagrams used Mo:V:Te 1:0.3:0.23, ammonium counterions and HClO4 acidification, pH7.25-1. The graphical abstract omits Te from another proposed formula where the main abstract includes it. Full spectra and numerical NMR assignments were unavailable.

These are Te-containing source systems, with no W or Mo36-bound V measurement. No rate, equilibrium constant, target chemical shift, lifetime or quantitative species fraction can be transferred. The abstract's interconversion shorthand is not a balanced reaction suitable for a kinetic model.

## Consequence for the target assay

This original inference concerns sampling, not a new compound: target identification and activity should be measured on the same operating solution before concentrating, drying or isolating it. Pair that measurement with the recovered solid and its redissolved solution. Preserve sample history, pH, concentration, counterion composition, temperature and elapsed time. Include matched concentration/dilution/reconstitution controls without claimed reproduction. Do not assign changes caused by sample processing to inherited chemical state.

For the proposed Mo34WV composition, a crystal or total-metal ratio could describe a selected precipitate while the functional solution contains different species. Conversely, recovering the same solution fingerprint after redissolution would not show continuous molecular survival. Discrimination requires species-resolved time courses together with calibrated element balances and activity attribution in each phase. This is a prospective measurement requirement; none of these target experiments was performed.

Decision: retain the existing identity gate as unmet and add processing history to its controls. Aggregate remains 24 assessed roles and zero verified integrated complements. All earlier failures and source discrepancies remain evidence.
