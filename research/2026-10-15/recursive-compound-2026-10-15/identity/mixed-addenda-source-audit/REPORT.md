# Mixed-addenda source audit: ordinary Mo36

Date: 2026-10-15. This is a source audit, with no new chemistry calculation or proposed synthesis. Existing identity, structure and site-chemistry ledgers were read first and left unchanged.

**Result:** a primary institutional thesis summary explicitly reports W substitution in ordinary Krebs-type Mo36 and preferential central seven-coordinate occupation. This strengthens the W substitution premise. The exact W loading, refined occupancy, substituted salt formula and solution stability could not be recovered from the accessible summary. No source inspected establishes the nominal `[MoVI34WVI VV O112(H2O)16]9−` compound, its V site, or its stability.

## Evidence that specifically names ordinary Mo36

**Mhamad Aly Moussawi, 2017 doctoral thesis, NNT 2017SACLV078, HAL tel-01719282v1.** [Official UVSQ summary](https://www.uvsq.fr/%C2%AB-assemblages-a-base-de-polyoxometallates-des-interactions-fondamentales-aux-materiaux-hybrides-supramoleculaires-%C2%BB-par-mhamad-aly-moussawi), published 13 October 2017; the HAL metadata gives defense date 25 October 2017. Locators: English abstract, first paragraph; locally retained three-page summary PDF, page 2 (French statement on page 1).

The summary first discusses mixed Mo/W Keplerates, then separately extends preferential W occupation to the Mo154 wheel and Krebs `[Mo36O112(H2O)16]8−`. It identifies the preferred position as the seven-coordinate center of a pentagon and names NMR, X-ray diffraction and EXAFS as methods. Thus this is a direct author report about the relevant Mo36 framework, beyond a Keplerate analogy. The French wording is clarified by the English coordination description; it does not establish W(VII).

The displayed Mo36 formula identifies the parent architecture, not the exact substituted product. The abstract supplies no W stoichiometry, occupancy table, crystallographic identifier, salt formula, protonation assignment, or quantified solution persistence. No V substitution is reported there.

Full-text identifiers recovered through the public [HAL metadata API](https://api.archives-ouvertes.fr/search/?q=halId_s%3Atel-01719282&wt=json&fl=%2A): [thesis landing record](https://theses.hal.science/tel-01719282v1), [deposited PDF URL](https://theses.hal.science/tel-01719282v1/file/74668_MOUSSAWI_2017_archivage.pdf), DOI `10.70675/0c7d0a8azeb8bz4b8fzb5c0zc6a013ac8438`. This DOI identifies the thesis, not a recovered peer-reviewed derivative paper. PDF requests returned anti-bot HTML; exact occupancies remain unread. The saved `uvsq2017.pdf` is only the three-page summary.

## Relation to existing site screening

The earlier fixed-parent bond-valence ranking places W best at Mo8@0/Mo8@2, which are seven-coordinate. Therefore the source's coordination preference is qualitatively compatible with that ranking. It does not identify the source's central pentagonal site with our Mo8 label: that requires topology mapping and the substituted crystal's atom/occupancy table. No new geometry, atom replacement, energy, or successful derivative model is implied. V and simultaneous W/V effects remain independently unresolved.

## Retained ambiguity and negative evidence

**Sanchez Sanchez et al., 2012, DOI [10.1002/anie.201200746](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.201200746).** The existing [primary manuscript and supporting information](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_1849723/component/file_1854326/content) were rechecked, not reclassified as a new dataset. SI §4.1, printed page 11, assigns a Raman band to `{Mo36−xNbxO112}` in a hydrothermal solution/solid mixture. SI §4.2, page 12, Figure S5, tentatively attributes shifts after V addition to partial Mo/V replacement in both nanocrystalline M5O14 and Nb-containing Mo36. No isolated V-substituted Mo36, exact formula or refined V occupancy follows. The paper's Mo72V30 species is a separate Keplerate.

**Martin Dieterle, 2001, _In Situ Resonance Raman Studies of Molybdenum Oxide Based Selective Oxidation Catalysts_, TU Berlin.** The [primary thesis PDF](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_741372/component/file_741371/content) was acquired and hash-checked (198 pages). Locator: §5.2.7.1, printed pp. 163–165, PDF pages 172–174, Figure 5.18. Mixed Mo/V/W solutions show largely separate polyoxomolybdates and polyoxovanadates, plus minor mixed species. A Mo36-related assignment near the section's end is tentative. The author explicitly cannot decide whether W enters Mo–O clusters because low W content and weaker Raman response make the spectra ambiguous. The inferred low V incorporation concerns the overall solution population, not a measured occupancy in one Mo36 molecule. This is evidence against assuming feed composition equals a single cluster composition; it is neither a failed synthesis of the exact target nor proof that such a compound cannot exist.

## Corroboration kept separate from exact-target evidence

Direct download returned 404, so it is an indexed abstract lead, not a locally verified structural dataset. Keplerate and Mo154 observations remain separate from ordinary Mo36 evidence.
