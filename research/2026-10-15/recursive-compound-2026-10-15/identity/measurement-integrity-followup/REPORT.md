## New discriminating measurement constraint

Before testing a boundary, validate that the identity measurement preserves or quantitatively accounts for its analyte. Compare a characterized parent reference before and after column passage with a matched column-free aging control. Measure molecular/speciation evidence, recovered inventory and the seed-response curve in the same collected fractions. Vary source energy to identify instrument-dependent signals, and check analytical blanks, washout, matrix-spike recovery and activity-disabled matched fractions. Different acidity or analytical additives must be treated as interventions with measured effects.

For a later pulse/chase boundary test, apply that qualified assay separately to donor, outflow and wall eluate. The desired observable is the recovery-corrected amount of a solution-resolved identity fraction that also retains seed competence over time. A mass peak or total-metal balance alone cannot supply it. Correct for destructive extraction, wall-dependent reporter chemistry and delayed release before interpreting active retention.

If oxygen labels are used, include a precursor-free founder-plus-labelled-water control matched for dilution, measured acidity, temperature, time, boundary exposure and analytical processing. Compare its isotope distribution, recoverable founder inventory and competence with the growth arm. Extra labelling in a precursor-fed arm is insufficient because precursor addition may alter exchange kinetics. A framework-sensitive ancestry discriminator and explicit founder accounting remain necessary; metal labels also need exchange/recycling controls. These are proposed measurements, with no invented thresholds or kinetic parameters.
