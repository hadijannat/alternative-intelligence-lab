# Qualification of a memory-bounded C-PCM implementation

The first parent pilot is retained as a resource-preflight rejection: 9,284 surface points and a 15,938,308,608-byte screening estimate exceeded its 10 GiB limit before SCF. This experiment changes the numerical implementation of the same solvent equations, not the geometry, solvent mesh, dielectric constant, functional or chemical acceptance threshold. No physical function is admitted by this test.

Reference: installed PySCF 2.14.0 `solvent/pcm.py`, SHA256 97bb24fb2e607745c444c36b6c3e649ffca66f808ec2965137a871061b3b9fed, and its documented SWIG equations (Lange and Herbert, DOI 10.1063/1.3511297). Restrict implementation to unfrozen C-PCM, ground-state RKS. For symmetric S and R=-f I, q=-f S^-1 v and q_sym=q; solvent energy is q.v/2 and the potential is the existing negative AO-charge contraction. Retain S and a reusable Cholesky factor. A failed factorization is a retained failure; no diagonal regularization or alternate solver is permitted in this qualification.

Construct S in row blocks without dense D, R, exponent meshgrids or displacement tensors. Use surface row blocks of 128 and AO-surface blocks of 8, releasing each integral tensor after contraction. The upstream minimum of 400 AO-surface points would allocate 30,120,396,800 bytes at 3,068 AOs before other work; replacing this block floor is required as well as reducing surface-matrix storage. Reset must invalidate all cached geometry/factors. Reject unsupported solvent methods and nuclear-gradient, Hessian, response or excited-state calls explicitly. No claims about those APIs follow from an SCF-only implementation.

Freeze tests before results: use the already-qualified hypothetical tetrahedral MoO4(2-) and planar MoO3 at Mo-O=1.75 A, with the same PBE/def2-SVP/Mo ECP, level-2 DFT grid, SWIG order17/radius scale1.2/probe0 and epsilon78.4 (plus epsilon1 control). Compare surface S, nuclear/electronic surface potentials, q, solvent energy and AO potential against the installed unmodified implementation on the same initial density and a symmetric perturbation. Require maximum absolute discrepancies below 1e-10 in atomic units and normalized solve backward error below 1e-12. Repeat small-reference contractions with block sizes 1, 7 and 8, and test reset after geometry change and explicit rejection of unsupported methods. A centered density derivative at h=1e-4 and 5e-5 must agree with Tr(V deltaP) within 1e-7 atomic units, with deltaP generated by NumPy default_rng(7392016), symmetrized and scaled to Frobenius norm 0.01. Repeat the operator comparison on each new converged density. These are implementation-equivalence tests, not held-out chemical observations.

Then run at most three small-reference SCFs: anion-water, neutral-water and anion-epsilon1. Use the original recorded reference energies, with absolute total-energy difference below 1e-8 Eh; require convergence, full solvent-aware orbital residual below 1e-5, electron-trace error below 1e-6 and absent cached four-center ERIs. Original SCF tolerances remain 1e-11 energy / 1e-7 orbital, maximum60 cycles. Each child has a 90-second wall cap and one requested thread; retain all failures and outputs with no automatic retry or tolerance relaxation. Root must review results before any new parent-scale numerical run. This qualification itself launches no parent SCF.

The same 10 GiB sampled RSS guard and explicit parent resource preflight remain necessary before a later parent attempt. Matrix and tensor sizes are allocation estimates, not certified peak memory. Full-parent timing, stability, gradient accuracy, aqueous identity, target incorporation and all integrated life functions remain unresolved.
