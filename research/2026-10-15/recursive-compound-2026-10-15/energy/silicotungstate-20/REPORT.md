# Candidate-role20: silicotungstic acid reservoir

**UNRESOLVED; not added to the verified assembly.** H4[alpha-SiW12O40] is proposed as an externally recharged electron/proton reservoir. This is a distinct role assessment following the earlier four energy candidates; it adds no verified complement to nominal Mo34WV.

[Rausch et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1257443), Figure2A, reports voltammetry of0.5M aqueous acid at pH0.5 under Ar at room temperature. These conditions are not assigned here to every cycling/storage assay. Waves at+0.01 and-0.22V versus NHE support one- and two-electron/proton states. Pt/C discharge gave68% hydrogen-equivalent charge recovery; adding titration of remaining reducing equivalents gave>98% accounting, not hydrogen yield. Nine one-electron cycles recovered98% charge. Four partial two-electron cycles and UV-visible similarity after20 cycles are separate observations. The reduced reservoir reacts with oxygen. Charging used carbon electrodes; the active solute's carbon-free formula does not describe the entire apparatus. The reported discharge-rate comparison excludes charging time. The author-hosted [full text](https://www.chem.gla.ac.uk/cronin/images/pubs/292.RauschScience.pdf) is copyrighted; it is a source, not an openly licensed raw dataset.

The [POM atlas](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adi0814) instead uses K4[alpha-SiW12O40]·8H2O. Its SI Fig.S30 concerns20mM oxidized potassium salt, including phosphate/citrate/HEPES media with0.2M buffers and approximately60-hour183W NMR acquisition. The source caption distinguishes intact and monolacunary signals; no fractions were integrated here. Those observations cannot establish persistence of the concentrated reduced acid.
