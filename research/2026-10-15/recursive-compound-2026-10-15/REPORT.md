# Recursive compound investigation: first integrated assessment

**The requested living, intelligent system has not been achieved.** The nominal `[MoVI34WVI VVO112(H2O)16]9-` remains an unverified molecular hypothesis. Seventeen candidate-role evaluations and two focused follow-ups now identify a better next persistence comparator, a failed medium assumption and specific structural uncertainties. No complement is verified to perform its function with the target. This is a progress checkpoint with the full goal still active.

The most useful next chemical comparator is **oxidized potassium alpha-Dawson tungstate, K6[alpha-P2W18O62]·19H2O**. Its measured persistence in selected phosphate preparations is stronger than PMo12's in the same starting buffers. This advances one component claim; it does not establish a common medium with Mo34WV, energy transfer, regeneration, inheritance or intelligence. It also does not replace the user's target with an easier material.

## What changed the next action

### 1. A real negative result selects a qualified alternative

The [primary POM speciation atlas](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adi0814), Tables S8/S10/S11/S17/S18/S19, supplies the following observations. POM concentration is 10 mM and starting sodium phosphate concentration is 0.1 M. Measurements were at 25°C; the later samples had been incubated for 24 hours at 37°C.

| Starting material | Nominal buffer pH | Measured initial pH | Measured later pH | Reported parent NMR signal fraction, initial → later |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| H3[alpha-PMo12O40] | 3 | 1.97±0.04 | 1.89±0.06 | 0% → 0% |
| H3[alpha-PMo12O40] | 4 | 2.10±0.06 | 2.03±0.09 | 0% → 0% |
| K6[alpha-P2W18O62] | 3 | 2.99±0.08 | 2.98±0.06 | 100% → 100% |
| K6[alpha-P2W18O62] | 4 | 4.00±0.06 | 3.97±0.06 | 100% → 100% |

The reported 0 and 100 values describe assigned POM phosphorus signals at the table's precision. Free phosphate is excluded; there is no independently calibrated total-metal recovery or zero-concentration detection limit. Each selected parent triplicate contains three matching integer entries. This is evidence of changed PMo12 speciation, not a measured zero molecular population. The different final pHs mean these are matched starting buffers, not identical final solutions or a joint mixture. Potassium oxidized Dawson cannot inherit the properties of the separately studied concentrated, reduced lithium salt.

**Recursive decision:** reject phosphate as a benign PMo12-preserving transfer under these tested conditions; advance potassium alpha-Dawson as a component-supported persistence comparator. Keep its energy-storage and target-coupling roles unresolved. The source contains neither Mo36 nor Mo34WV and supplies no rate law for them. Raw NMR FIDs remain unavailable; the published tables were acquired and inspected. The unbuffered D2O PMo12 control also contains a printed-mean discrepancy, retained in the [compatibility report](compatibility/REPORT.md) and [ten-row data file](compatibility/selected_rows.csv).

### 2. The parent structure is usable, but the proposed substitution needs testing

The complete connected parent heavy fragment was recovered from the potassium Mo36 crystallographic deposit: **36 Mo + 128 O**, with source-site/symmetry mapping, full selected occupancies and consistent periodic unwrapping. The same fragment survives 2.5–2.7 Å coordination cutoffs. The deliberately short 2.0 Å cutoff produces Mo4O11 and remains a rejected control. Hydrogens are absent; the sixteen inferred aqua sites and solution protonation remain unverified. [Structure and admission limits](identity/REPORT.md).

Using the [primary Gagné–Hawthorne oxygen bond-valence parameters](https://doi.org/10.1107/S2052520615016297), the fixed parent geometry gives:

| Test | Result | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Parent Mo(VI) | BVS 5.903477–6.312166; RMS mismatch 0.144677 valence units | Retain local parent disagreement; source calibration RMSD is not a target uncertainty |
| W(VI) replacement | Lowest mismatch at Mo8: BVS 6.004790 | A named local structural hypothesis for later comparison |
| V(V) replacement | Every site below 5; best at Mo7: BVS 4.719064 | Unrelaxed substitution does not satisfy nominal local valence accounting |
| Parent bias | Mo7 is also the worst parent Mo site, BVS 6.312166 | The apparent V preference partly exploits an already overbonded parent site |

All 36 positions, including inversion partners, were evaluated at three fixed cutoffs: 108 rows and 324 sums. Independent recomputation agrees. No bond strain, energy, coupled W/V pair interaction, substitution yield or aqueous lifetime was calculated. The result prioritizes hypotheses; it neither establishes nor disproves the derivative's existence. [Full calculation and retained limitations](site_chemistry/REPORT.md).

The full nominal target would have 196 atoms, 2590 total electrons and 1578 explicit electrons with the stated ECP treatment; a def2-SVP calculation has 3069 spherical basis functions. This is actual atomic-build bookkeeping, not a completed target calculation. Full stored integral strategies exceed the observed host RAM; direct or blocked methods remain possible but have unmeasured cost. The [next parent comparison](identity/next-step/REPORT.md) specifies alternative water/proton arrangements and a capped pilot rather than assuming a hydrated structure or launching an unbounded optimization.

### 3. The other apparent successes remain narrower than the missing functions

- **Energy:** hydrated WO3, Dawson, PMo12 and CeO2 each have measured energy-related behavior in their own apparatus. Their useful coupling to this target is unmeasured. Recalculated ceria aggregate output/input ratios differ from the source's sums of cycle percentages. PMo12's 86.73% half-cell benchmark is separate from the primary paper's reported 79% two-electrode relative-efficiency result. External energy is permitted; target maintenance still needs to be demonstrated. [Energy evidence](energy/REPORT.md).
- **Retention:** a ZrO2 isotherm's fitted 66.70 mg/g maximum cannot be assigned to a batch containing only 20 mg Mo per gram of sorbent. Retained Mo may be chemically transformed. SiO2, ZrO2, TiO2, goethite and FeS-containing membranes all lack a joint intact-active-target assay. [Boundary evidence](boundary/REPORT.md).
- **Inheritance:** CHA's accessible pore capacity rises from approximately 0.001 to 0.24 cm3/g. Unchanged founders can explain at most 1/240 of that capacity if their recovered mass does not exceed total mass. Changed old seeds remain an explanation; the calculation does not locate the new function in new atoms or prove serial transmission. Mo36/Mo6, PMo12, NaClO3 and CHA do not close functional inheritance for the target. [Heredity evidence](heredity/REPORT.md).
- **Adaptation:** WO3, VO2, NiO and Ag2S provide component-level memory/plasticity evidence. Ag2S source-fit arithmetic gives 3.40 versus 510.2125 seconds at 1 versus 3.5 mS, with strong model limitations. No source demonstrates a renewing Mo34WV system whose state controls resource acquisition and improves under held-out changes. [Adaptation evidence](adaptation/REPORT.md).

The candidate counts refer to roles, not seventeen unique compounds: WO3 and PMo12 recur across functions. Reusing one publication for two roles does not create independent evidence. A carbon-free active formula, carbon-independent preparation and carbon-free apparatus are different claims; organic fabrication/readout materials alone do not invalidate an inorganic active mechanism.

### 4. Small-reference calculations exposed and corrected two verification errors

The registered computational route now has a bounded test of direct PBE/def2-SVP with a Mo ECP and a water C-PCM model. The reference geometries are explicitly hypothetical tetrahedral MoO4(2-) and planar MoO3; these are runtime/derivative checks, not experimental structures or benchmarks of chemical accuracy. Four charge/solvation control cases converge, including vacuum equivalence at dielectric constant 1.

The default orbital-gradient check omitted the solvent part of the Fock matrix. Passing the complete solvent-aware Fock matrix makes that check consistent with the actual SCF calculation. A subsequent analytic nuclear-gradient comparison **failed** the fixed 1e-4 Eh/bohr criterion: discrepancies were 4.45900e-4 and 4.45890e-4 at displacement steps 1e-3 and 5e-4 bohr. Changing only the analytic gradient's `grid_response=True` setting reduced them to 7.94648e-6 and 7.95702e-6 against the same finite differences and unchanged criterion. The electronic checks, failed derivative test and corrected diagnostic are all retained in [method qualification](identity/next-step/method-qualification/REPORT.md).

This changes the acceptance code required for any later parent calculation. It does not establish a ground-state minimum, reliable solvation free energy, Mo34WV geometry, or parent-scale feasibility. No full parent or target SCF has run, and the parent hydrogen/protonation inputs remain unbuilt.

## Verification, negative results and next execution

Eight final source/geometry/accounting commands exited 0. Their complete outputs are in [the root receipt](../.omo/evidence/recursive-integration-verification.json); [checks.md](checks.md) separates these passes from unresolved chemical requirements. An independent reviewer recomputed the six initial numerical claims, then the NMR rows and all 324 bond-valence sums. The review found two ineffective heredity controls; actual changed-input and invalid-input tests replaced them, and the limitation remains documented. The root protocol was corrected to say bond-valence mismatch, because no strain was calculated. These checks support the reported arithmetic and provenance, not life.

Preserved negatives include primary-paper access failures, the incomplete extraction control, PMo12 medium failure and conflicting recipes, the printed NMR mean discrepancy, parent-site disagreement, V underbonding, unknown source detection limits, unknown target coupling, finite-reservoir exhaustion, blocked raw-data retrieval, and earlier failed RNA/transport/inheritance models. The previously published nine laboratories remain unchanged.

No target observations were available for held-out validation. Published tables and revisited source curves are retrospective evidence; source-fit evaluations are not unseen predictions. Future model fitting requires preparation-level train/test separation and a protocol frozen before viewing its tests.

Four explicitly hypothetical parent water/proton inputs now exist in [parent-inputs/](parent-inputs/REPORT.md): P0_A, P0_B, PT57_A and PT57_B. Each preserves all 164 heavy-atom coordinates, adds 32 model-built H and has nominal charge -8. All pass the frozen computational geometry checks; ten malformed-input controls reject. The PT57 H-Mo minimum is 1.713071746 A, so passing the gross 1.50 A cutoff does not establish chemical feasibility. Physical admission remains false.

The [parent pilot protocol](parent-pilot/PROTOCOL.md) and supervisor are prepared for the fixed P0_A input. No full-parent SCF, optimization or comparison between these arrangements has run. The prelaunch review identified a missing monitoring-failure receipt path; this is repaired and covered by regression tests before publication. Next computational action: evaluate the declared surface-memory preflight and, only within the frozen limits, run the single capped pilot. The small-reference result does not remove the need for basis, cavity, spin/orbital-stability and parent-scale checks. The next empirical action is species-resolved mediator/parent compatibility. Both must precede claiming that any complement closes a target function.

Skipped physical synthesis/operation, raw-FID reprocessing, full-target electronic optimization and functional-lineage/learning experiments. The main risk is that the target may not be a persistent solution species, and coupling useful components may erase the state they are meant to preserve. The full goal remains active and unmet.
