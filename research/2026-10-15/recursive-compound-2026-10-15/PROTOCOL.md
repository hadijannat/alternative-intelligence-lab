# Evidence contract for the full goal

The nominal starting anion is a hypothesis. The recovered Mo36 heavy-atom structure is a solid-state parent reference. Neither supplies a measured solution identity for Mo34WV. All additions below remain proposals unless their claimed function is demonstrated with the target under the same declared operating conditions.

## What would establish the missing functions?

| ID | Necessary function | Evidence that would test it | Controls and remaining inference |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | Exact material identity | Site/composition/charge and species-resolved persistence for the target in its actual medium, including counterions, protonation and redox states | Parent, precursor mixtures and decomposition products; a formula or stable computed geometry is insufficient |

| H | Functional inheritance into new material | Parent-associated functional state reproduced in new descendants after an independent quantitative founder bound | State-erased parents, equal composition/feed, unseeded formation, founder-only rivals and serial transfers |
| V | Heritable variation and selection | At least two inherited functional variants produce differential outcomes under controlled shared conditions | Drift, sorting, differential passive persistence and environmentally rewritten state; no invented copying rate |

| A | Causal action and adaptation | State causally changes resource/maintenance behavior and improves performance on held-out environmental changes | Memory-disrupted, sensor-disrupted, matched-resource and fixed-response controls; external software must not supply the claimed chemical update |

External energy, external training inputs and apparatus are allowed and accounted. They do not by themselves invalidate learning. Fresh replacement of the supposedly self-renewing component or software that computes its claimed adaptive update leaves that particular autonomy claim unproved. The active organization must be noncarbon-based; carbon in fabrication or measurement apparatus is recorded separately and is not automatically evidence that an inorganic active mechanism is carbon-based. A demonstrated reliance on an organic membrane or polymer inside the maintained organization needs explicit treatment.

## Recursive decisions

For every proposal record its role, exact material and conditions, source/evidence family, performed test, observed result and uncertainty, claim-specific disposition, unresolved dependencies and next test/alternative. Unperformed measurements remain null or explicitly unmeasured; missing uncertainty never becomes zero.

- **Proposed member:** a chemically motivated possibility. No function is credited.
- **Component-supported member:** a named function was observed under the source's conditions. The observation can guide the next experiment; it does not close the corresponding target-system requirement.
- **Verified integrated member:** the claimed function was observed with the target in the same characterized operating system and survived relevant dependency/control checks.
- **Rejected claim:** a specified claim or direct protocol transfer contradicts evidence. Preserve its scope; do not infer impossibility of every architecture using that material.
- **Unresolved:** evidence cannot discriminate the needed mechanism or transfer. Identify the next measurement instead of declaring failure or success of an unperformed experiment.

After changing medium, composition, drive or interface, reassess every affected accepted claim. In particular, sharing proton/electron reservoirs may erase memory; adsorption may retain the elements while destroying the active cluster; growth may overwrite state. Candidate lists from different papers are not an assembled system.

## Proposed sequence after the first evidence pass

1. Qualify the parent structural input and assess fixed-geometry bond-valence mismatch before constructing a target quantum hypothesis. Parent H arrangements and target site assignments remain explicit alternatives.
2. Use the NMR atlas to reject unsupported medium transfers. Retain an oxidized Dawson tungstate only for its measured component retention. Test target plus mediator before adding a boundary; the potassium oxidized salt cannot inherit concentrated reduced-lithium-salt properties.
3. Compare unmodified SiO2 and ZrO2 contact/exchange at a demonstrated shared medium, with species identity and active function on both sides. A successful boundary then needs renewal, not just retention.
4. Couple measured energy input to new active material and waste removal. Count complete regeneration cycles and finite capacities.
5. Test new-material inheritance and state-dependent action independently, then jointly under changed environments. Do not assume physical reproduction is necessary for all learning; functional inheritance is separately required by this research objective.

Thresholds and uncertainty for future physical acceptance must be registered before observing those experiments. No arbitrary success number is supplied by this document. No physical experiment or target-model holdout is claimed in this cycle.
