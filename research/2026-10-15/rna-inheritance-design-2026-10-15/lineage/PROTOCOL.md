# Registered conditional lineage experiment

Planning protocol v2, frozen after development and before official simulation, 2026-10-15. No fitted RNA chemistry rate is used. Scope is fixed-sequence, parent-dependent catalytic state and terminal-generation renewal, not genetic inheritance or evolution. Research basis: `../research/lineage/REPORT.md` and `../research/assays/REPORT.md`.

## Physical units and genealogy

Integer molecules (6.02214076e8 per fmol) distinguish active/inactive original founder, earlier newly synthesized F1, current-generation newly synthesized F1, and horizontal donor F1. Hyper-A, B, reporter precursor and inactive reporter product have separate inventories. Each ligation consumes one A or reporter precursor and one B. Finite external feed, measured-volume restoration, external loss and inactivation are explicit. Supplied renewal stoichiometry is a scenario, not calibrated kinetics.

Each independent root begins at 1 µM total F1: high history 0.4 µM active or low history 0.01 µM active, with inactive F1 matching the total. Root histories and intact/reset assignments are randomized within four batches. Reset is matched replacement with 0.01 µM active/0.99 µM inactive F1 in both histories; discarded stock and replacement feed are accounted. Intact sham processing retains the original inventory. In experiments these interventions require matrix-matched independent validation.

One cycle: reclassify prior new stock as old; physically divide each parent into two daughters with Beta(20,20) volume fractions and complementary binomial allocation of every molecular species; restore each daughter to its root volume using catalyst-free fluid; supply A=8, B=12 and reporter precursor=4 µM-equivalent amounts per restored volume; lose 10% of molecules to the exterior; inactivate 5% of active catalyst; ligate finite substrate; sample or continue. Every parent ceases at division. Six generations are explicit. At g5, randomly sacrifice one daughter from each sister pair (16 tips/root) and continue its sister; at g6 assay its 32 terminal descendants. A terminal pool contains only descendants of one root. Amounts are sums and volume is the sum of sampled volumes. The pool cannot establish every daughter's functionality.

Only selected g5 terminal tips receive a generation pulse; their sisters receive ordinary feed. All g6 terminal tips receive the pulse. Observer `founder` input conservatively includes all pre-existing non-donor F1; observer `new` is only current-generation F1. Original founder, ordinary feed and terminal pulse require three validated distinguishable provenance codes. Internal generation counters are simulator truth, not proof that analytical codes work. Current-generation and since-founder inventory remain separate in raw output.

## Frozen worlds and observation scenarios

Eight worlds: strong renewal (4 products/existing active molecule/cycle, 90% active product); weak renewal (0.5, 90%); founder-only (no ligation of new F1); inactive-new (4, 0% active); reporter-only (no active founder, no new active F1, precursor background); common-feed recreation (new active synthesis supplied at fixed 1 µM-equivalent independent of parent); horizontal (new F1 inactive, active donor input at 0.5 µM-equivalent/cycle); shared-history environment (parent-independent synthesis 2 versus 0.1 µM-equivalent for high/low history in BOTH intact and reset arms). The latter deliberately violates perfect common-garden execution and tests whether the reset comparison rejects it. All worlds retain precursor-assisted inactive reporter production. Reporter consumes R+B and never becomes active F1. Horizontal imports are recorded and subtracted by the shared observer; other worlds have zero import.

A ninth no-founder control has both founder states zero, plus complete precursor feed and reporter background. Reporter-only is the separate inactive-founder control (1 µM inactive founder, no active founder). These map distinct negative controls explicitly. Current-generation stocks are named `pulse_new` internally even in ordinary-fed continuing branches; they become older stock before any subsequent generation, while physical pulse labels are applied only to branches already assigned terminal sacrifice.

Three platforms: 0.524 pL/1 fmol instrumental LOQ; 1000 pL/1 fmol; 1000 pL/0.01 fmol. These are hypothetical LOQs and larger-compartment designs, not qualified instruments. Shared observation and inference come exclusively from `../assay/model.py`; no ideal activity observer is used. True native capacity is active F1 plus 0.05×Hyper-A and 0.005×reporter nuisance. Refolded capacity includes additionally 20% of inactive F1. Shared response/recovery errors per batch are normal SD0.03/0.05; residual assay response SD0.03 follows the assay API. Its default 25% calibration margin and all qualification assumptions apply. Family lineage uncertainty probability2%; activity-dependent dropout2%+8%exp(-active concentration) per terminal pool. Unqualified observations remain in all denominators and have unresolved intervals, never zero inferred activity.

## Estimands, sample sizes and decisions

128 registered independent Monte Carlo experiments per world/platform, seed namespace [20261015,5201,world,platform,replicate]. Development uses disjoint namespace [20261015,5199]. Generate 96 roots per history×arm (384 total), equally across four batches. N=24,48,96 comparisons use batch-balanced prefixes of the same roots, declared here before simulation. Each root contributes at most one pool per terminal generation; tips are never independent replicates.

For terminal-generation NEW native capacity per volume, the planning state threshold is 0.5 µM-equivalent, a deliberately stated minimum effect of interest rather than a source estimate. If the inferred lower bound exceeds it, state interval=[1,1]; if upper bound is below it, [0,0]; otherwise [0,1]. Missing/unqualified observations also receive [0,1]. New-generation detection is separately assessed using the assay's instrumental threshold. Require at least80% of all high-intact roots detected at BOTH g5 and g6.

Development v1 (two experiments per original world/platform; namespace5199) exposed low/reset interval overlap at g6 and zero joint power for the 0.5 µM threshold even in the strong scenario. Before official execution the registered threshold surface expands to [0.5,1,2] µM, retaining the failed original threshold rather than selecting a winning detector. These are alternative planning effect definitions, not three confirmatory tests permitting an any-threshold claim. Every threshold is reported separately. No observer error or physical rate is changed in response to development. A within-batch/arm shuffle of history assignment uses whole roots and identical labels at both generations; shuffled joint decisions are retained for every N and threshold as an analysis diagnostic, not a cure for unrandomized environments.

Ancestry contrast is mean(high intact)-mean(low intact)-mean(high reset)+mean(low reset), computed within each of four batches then averaged. Its conservative lower bound uses lower intervals for positive terms and upper intervals for negative terms, minus 3.3 times root-level standard error from interval midpoints. Variance sums within-group sample variances/n, then averages the four independent batch contributions (divide variance by16). Also subtract 3.3 times the standard error across the four batch contrasts; take the larger root or batch standard error. This is a prespecified conservative planning screen, not a proven finite-sample test. Joint inheritance decision requires lower contrast>0 and detection fraction≥0.8 at BOTH terminal generations. Report Monte Carlo false positives in every null world and power in strong/weak worlds with Wilson95% intervals. Target power≥80%, null acceptance≤5%; interval endpoints, not just estimates, determine whether the planning target is established.

Native activity-retention calibration is an independent no-feed cohort at each platform: before/after actual loss+inactivation with all emitted stocks accounted, using the shared observer and ratio intervals. Amount and native/refolded capacity retention are separate. No inference uses simulated truth, including measured-positive decisions; truth is used only for coverage and conservation checks.

Coverage uncertainty treats each Monte Carlo experiment as an independent cluster: calculate the fraction of qualified root/generation intervals covering true terminal-new native capacity within each experiment, then report mean and t interval across experiments. Also report the proportion of experiments with every qualified interval covering truth and its Wilson interval. Raw pooled hit counts are descriptive only. Shared errors and paired generations do not create independent observations. Retention simulations have one independent before/after pair per experiment, so their pair-level Wilson intervals are appropriate. Qualification failures remain visible; these estimates cannot quantify experimentally unidentified recovery or missing fractions.
