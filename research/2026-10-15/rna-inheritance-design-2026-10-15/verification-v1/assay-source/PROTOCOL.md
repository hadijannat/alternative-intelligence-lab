# Frozen assay design v1

Prospective synthetic planning only. Source identities and assay architecture come from ../research/assays/REPORT.md and ledger.json; no real recovery, LOQ, activity equivalence or capacity rate is known. Earlier RNA fits failed and supply no kinetic constants here. E810 motivates explicit nonidentifiability witnesses, not chemical calibration.

Scope: identity/provenance plus native-recovered and separately refolded capacity; nonreacting paired retention with inside/outside/surface fractions. Amounts are fmol; capacity is fmol of an authentic F1 reference giving the same initial reporter-production slope. No inference assumes mass equals activity or isotope fractions can be physically separated. Two worlds with founder=new=10 and total capacity=10, but (founder-active,new-active)=(10,0) or (0,10), have exactly equal marginal observations.

Calibration requirements: isotope label equivalence, G38/junction identity, full-length enrichment, matched process recovery, short linear/additive capacity assay or validated nuisance-only mixture upper envelope, imported-activity bound, native recovery qualification and matrix-specific LOQs. Explicit failed qualification returns unresolved even if the hidden synthetic truth is positive. Refolding-only activation cannot establish native capacity. Nonadditivity without an empirical envelope fails admission.

Planning assumptions: recovery .5; half material goes to identity and one quarter to each capacity assay. Recovered LOQ=1 fmol in every channel. Founder maximum specific capacity=1.1 equivalent/fmol; nuisance coefficients Hyper-A=.15 and reporter=.02; imported material maximum capacity=1.1 equivalent/fmol. Independent response error SD=.03, batch-shared response SD=.03, batch-shared recovery-calibration SD=.05. Raw recovered signals include calibrated bounded cross-talk .001 fmol. Simultaneous conservative intervals use relative margin .25 (over 3.8 combined SD); no exact frequentist guarantee is asserted for Gaussian tails, so coverage is simulated. Recovery estimates and response share errors across channels; paired fractions share batch calibration. Negative and sub-LOQ signals are censored, never treated as exact zero.

Inference uses observed intervals only: max(0, capacity lower − founder amount upper×1.1 − Hyper-A upper×.15 − reporter upper×.02 − imported amount upper×1.1). Report identity and capacity lower bounds jointly only when light wild-type F1 is above LOQ and the capacity bound exceeds capacity LOQ on the original-sample basis. Native and refolded conclusions are separate. New capacity upper is min(total capacity upper, new amount upper×1.1), conditional on the new-specific-activity upper calibration. This is a capacity bound, not a count of active molecules.

Official simulation: fixed seed namespace [20261015,5101], 256 independent batch replicates per scenario; seeds [17,19] reserved for development. Founder amounts 0,10,100 fmol; new amounts 0,10,100,1000; new active fractions 0,.25,1. Nulls include inactive new material plus Hyper-A/reporters and retained-founder-only. Additional gates: label failure, nonadditive mixture, unknown recovery, and refolding-only activation. No tuning after official scores. Report Wilson 95% binomial intervals; target power >=.8 and false positives <=.05, certify only if lower/upper confidence limits meet targets. Coverage evaluated against true native/refolded NEW capacity, separate from decision accuracy.

Retention scenarios before 1000 fmol/800 capacity: molecular retention .8 or .3, capacity surviving per retained molecule 1 or .4, unequal true extraction recovery inside .3/outside .8/surface .5, and unqualified unknown-recovery control. Outside receives escaped amounts/capacity; surface retains .1 of initial amount. Remaining inside fraction is prescribed, all physical amounts sum to initial. Inactivation reduces inside capacity; no regeneration occurs. Paired total RNA and capacity intervals distinguish compatible loss versus inactivation, while unresolved cases remain unresolved. New-age active retention is interval division (inside-new-capacity bounds/before-new-capacity bounds), unresolved if denominator lower=0; no exact allocation to isotope age is claimed.

Material feasibility: sphere volume pi*d^3/6/1000 pL, fmol=concentration_uM*volume_pL*.001. Scan diameter10,20,50 µm, pools1,100,10000,100000 and new fractions .1,.5,1. Report all three aliquot LOQ requirements; a10µm GUV at1µM contains .000523599 fmol. Terminal pools change the estimand to pooled branches and cannot establish individual-daughter success.

Retention material is a nonreacting standard mixture of100founder+900new-labelled fmol with total800capacity. In addition to marginal bounds, a paired inside/before capacity-per-total-F1 contrast cancels common multiplicative response and recovery-calibration errors. Its six independent native/founder/new response errors use±.10 bounds (>3.3SD), with explicit cross-talk subtraction. This requires same batch, matched recovery within each fraction, no censoring and qualified recovery; differential capacity recovery requires separate calibration. Gaussian tails and cancellation assumptions are checked by synthetic coverage, not guaranteed experimentally. Development-v1 showed marginal bounds too wide for60%inactivation; the paired contrast was added before the official run and retains the unsuccessful marginal analysis.

Pre-freeze review corrections: shared and independent response errors multiply as(1+shared)*(1+independent), ensuring exact common-factor cancellation for the paired contrast after bounded cross-talk correction. Coverage receives Wilson95%intervals; recovery-unqualified mass balance is null. Inconsistent capacity lower>identity-derived upper returns unresolved with a reason. These refinements were made before official seeds were used.
