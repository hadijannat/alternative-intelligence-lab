# Physical lineage and functional inheritance experiment design

15 October 2026. This is a proposed measurement design and specification for conditional simulations. No division device, recovered-F1 assay or physical inheritance experiment was performed here. The existing RNA mechanism model remains physically inadequate, so its fitted rates must not become experimental power assumptions.

## What the experiment can establish

Keep three claims separate. **Functional continuity** means physical descendants retain or regenerate the same catalytic capability, with newly generated active F1 demonstrated beyond founder carryover. **Heritable variation** additionally requires distinguishable functional states that persist because of parental state under common conditions. **Evolution** additionally requires heritable variation coupled to differential reproduction. The fixed F1 sequence supplied by the present fragment recipe offers no demonstrated alternative heritable functional variant. Starting with high and low catalyst amounts tests transient catalytic-state memory; a positive result alone does not establish multiple stable inherited types, sequence copying or evolution. Ameta's network experiments show why this distinction matters: background assembly can erase initial compositional differences even while catalysts reproduce [L4].

Use a tracked division event as the operational generation boundary. Continuous imaging must show one closed parent compartment producing identified, closed daughters. Record timestamp, parent ID, both daughter IDs, volume, retained lumen marker and membrane integrity. Mere constriction is insufficient; demonstrate separation and loss of lumen connectivity with an independently validated imaging test. Record fusion and exchange as additional edges, not parentage. Ambiguous edges remain missing or invalid; do not infer pedigrees from nearby fluorescent objects. Replacing half a population with fresh vesicles remains a serial-transfer experiment.

## Physical platform and sampling

A mechanical splitter is the more controllable first platform. Deshpande et al. directly observed liposomes dividing at a microfluidic Y junction, including leakage and geometric dependence [L1]. That demonstrates an engineering component, not compatibility with the Salibi sucrose/Mg buffer, a complete growth cycle or F1 inheritance. Zhu and Szostak provide a complementary fatty-acid growth/division example with redistribution of encapsulated RNA [L2]; their membrane and buffer cannot be substituted silently for POPC GUV conditions.

Before admitting multigeneration measurements, qualify matched buffer compatibility, delivery of fresh A/B, membrane growth or volume restoration, and division. Division alone makes smaller daughters; repeated equal division without growth reduces volume by half each generation. State explicitly whether volume is restored by membrane feeding, fluid injection or fusion with catalyst-free feed vesicles, and record each intervention. Feed-vesicle fusion is an externally supplied resource edge and possible contamination route. It is not an autonomous reproductive process. If only a first division works, the physical claim stops there even if the conditional multigeneration simulation runs.

Use independent root families randomized across device positions, days and treatments. At each verified division, randomly allocate one daughter to destructive assays and the other to continuation. Do not always continue the larger or brighter daughter. In dedicated calibration families, assay both daughters to estimate partition asymmetry and how well the sacrificed sister predicts the continuing branch. A sister assay is a noisy family proxy, never a measurement of the unassayed daughter's molecular state. MADS demonstrates destructive mass-spectrometry analysis on one droplet fraction while retaining its sister [L3]; adapting that architecture to intact F1 identity plus catalytic activity is proposed, not established.

**Sample-amount admission is essential.** A spherical 10 µm GUV holds approximately 0.524 pL, hence 0.524 amol at 1 µM before recovery or aliquot splitting. This is not a claimed LC-MS quantification limit. Let Q be the validated amount required by the least sensitive joint-assay channel, C the measured concentration, V the sampled compartment volume, e the recovery, and f its aliquot fraction. Require `sampled amount = sum(C_i V_i e_i f_i) >= Q`, with the same units and uncertainty allowance. Below LOQ is censored, never zero activity.

If single-GUV analysis fails admission, pool only identified descendants of one root at one defined generation, preserving all IDs and pool membership. Pooling across unrelated roots destroys the family estimand. A pool measures family-average functional continuity; it cannot show that every daughter is functional or recover daughter-level variance. More divisions alone do not increase total RNA amount. Pooling helps collect an already existing family inventory, and only growth/regeneration can increase it. A retained branch and sacrificial cousins can be pooled later, but their relation must remain explicit. A larger compartment is a separate platform needing buffer, transport and division requalification. The simulator should show single-GUV, same-family pooled and larger-volume observation scenarios, with unsupported assay feasibility marked unresolved.

## Measurements that separate origins, retention and function

Use a validated nonexchangeable stable-isotope distinction between original full-length founder F1 and supplied ligation fragments. Confirm full-length covalent F1 identity and label composition, including the ligation junction, rather than accepting any labelled nucleotide or fluorescent 66-nt-sized band. RNA pulse–chase MS methods support the general possibility of distinguishing old and new material [L5]; they do not supply an intact-F1 activity assay or a single-GUV LOQ. Isotope effects, natural-isotope overlap, incomplete enrichment, degradation and fragment reuse require standards and controls.

Founder/new distinction alone cannot locate synthesis. Newly made F1 in a child might have been made in its parent and then transferred. Add a separate terminal-generation pulse experiment in each generation cohort, with label-swapped controls and quantified residual labelled precursor. Either resolve generation-specific labels or use separate root cohorts pulsed only in the target generation. Do not reuse an indistinguishable label and call old product new again.

Collect matched observations immediately before division, immediately after division and after daughter incubation. Measure recovered full-length F1 amount inside daughters, outside in effluent, and on relevant device surfaces. Report total molecular retention separately from activity retention; both require absolute recovery and volume, not reporter yield ratios. Estimate retention during a short no-feed/no-regeneration transport interval, and separately test the complete growth medium. Where formation during division is possible, pulse timing and immediate sampling must distinguish formation from survival.

After recovery, remove A/B fragments and Cy5 reporter species with a validated separation, and test the purified F1 fraction against fresh reporter substrates under standardized initial-rate conditions. Include all processing blanks, purified inactive F1, precursor-only fractions, founder-only samples, known active standards, spike recovery, and carryover tests. Standardized refolding measures recoverable catalytic potential; it is not necessarily native activity inside the original compartment. A separate intact-compartment assay and matrix-matched recovery bridge are needed before claiming native activity retention.

Isotope-resolved amounts plus aggregate activity do not automatically identify new-molecule activity. Unless an isotope-selective native separation is validated, use an explicit conservative bound in the same recovered aliquot domain:

`new activity lower bound = max(0, total activity lower confidence bound - founder amount upper bound × maximum validated founder-specific activity - other-catalysis upper bound)`.

The founder-specific upper bound must include processing/reactivation, substrate conditions and calibration uncertainty. Validate additivity in mixtures; cooperative activity or matrix changes can invalidate subtraction. Isotope MS destroys material, so split matched recovered aliquots and propagate sampling uncertainty. A positive lower bound identifies some new-material-associated activity only under those validated observer assumptions. A zero bound means unresolved attribution, not proof of inactivity. Model an inactive-new-F1 adversarial world explicitly.

For an isolated, nonsynthesizing founder cohort on one chosen branch, the expected founder count is `N0 × product(retention_j × allocation_j)`. This expectation is not a conservative bound on a stochastic partition. Prefer measured founder amount at every endpoint; otherwise use a prespecified upper predictive quantile under the partition model and report model dependence. The hard conservation bound for the entire isolated family is N0 (less measured losses), with no factor 2^-g. Incoming founders from other families invalidate the isolated bound unless source-resolved and included. Full-length nonexchangeable labels distinguish intact founder molecules from labelled fragments; retained labels alone cannot.

## Common-garden and horizontal-transfer tests

Randomize parental catalyst state while holding total RNA, buffer, volume, fresh feed and handling matched. A practical first contrast is active seed versus inactive sequence-matched seed, with no-seed and founder-only/no-regeneration controls. Heat treatment alone changes other chemistry, so matrix-match processing and verify that the inactive preparation stays inactive after recovery. A substrate intervention suppressing regeneration must preserve reporter-assay conditions or be interpreted as a compound perturbation.

After division, place identified descendants into separately isolated, fresh, matched environments and randomize positions again. Test new-F1 activity at the endpoint, beyond founder contribution, rather than comparing fluorescent yields. A persistent active-parent effect under these conditions supports parent-dependent functional continuity. If high/low starting amounts converge, report loss of state memory while assessing continuity separately. A stable alternative state would require independent characterization and repeated common-garden transmission before calling it heritable variation.

Run separate contact-permitted and physically isolated arms. Add catalyst-free recipient sentinels, sham-fed sentinels and unrelated labelled donor families. Monitor donor-specific founder labels, intact F1, function and fluid contacts. Use no-donor controls to estimate background assembly. A signal in unrelated recipients is evidence for a possible horizontal route, not descendant inheritance. Contact isolation must retain matched feeding and physical conditions as far as possible; any remaining difference is reported.

Shuffle parent labels only within randomized batch, treatment and generation strata as an analysis negative control. A shuffle cannot remove a true shared environmental confound when treatment and environment were not independently randomized. Cluster all uncertainty calculations by independent root family. Report survival/track loss and assay censoring by arm; missing branches are not successful or nonfunctional descendants. Include both intention-to-observe and qualified-pedigree summaries to expose selective attrition.

## Minimal stochastic simulation specification

Reuse the existing `serial-renewal-lineage-identifiability-v1` distinction between founder/new and active/inactive inventories, and its observation hierarchy. Do not import its material units, generic deposition channels, exact q=1/2 transfer or ideal additive activity as measured RNA chemistry. Its exact certificates demonstrate an information requirement; physical assay calibration remains absent there.

Represent integer molecule inventories by origin (founder, generation-labelled new, horizontal donor), functional state (active/inactive), and molecular identity (F1, reporter, fragments). Store root/parent/child IDs, birth generation, volume and all losses. One externally controlled branch cycle contains finite feed, conditional reaction, deactivation, leakage, verified-or-failed division, stochastic volume-dependent molecular allocation and measurement. Use finite-substrate reaction counts; every ligated molecule consumes one A and one B. Reporter ligation consumes its own reporter fragment and B. Log external feed and sinks. Use molecule counts for partition noise, concentrations only after dividing by measured volumes.

Freeze hypothetical rate/noise/LOQ grids before evaluating worlds. No rate from the failed fit is a validated constant. At minimum simulate these distinct worlds with matched assay and device noise:

| World | Truth imposed | False interpretation it tests |
|---|---|---|
| Founder only | Original active F1 persists; no new active F1 | Carryover called regeneration |
| Reporter/precursor route | Reporter increases without any new active F1 | Fluorescence called active inheritance |
| Inactive new product | Full-length new-labelled F1 is inactive; founders remain active | Isotope amount plus bulk activity conflated |
| Shared environment | Batch/position drives both parent and child measurements | Correlation called heredity |
| Horizontal transfer | Active catalyst arrives from unrelated donors or common bath | Dissemination called vertical transmission |
| Leakage/deactivation | Material can remain while function is lost, or vice versa through selective loss | Tracer retention called catalyst retention |
| Conditional regeneration/transmission | Active new F1 is supplied by explicitly assumed chemistry and passed through physical division | Positive sensitivity control; not a discovery of that chemistry |

Cross these worlds with contaminated purification, isotope misclassification, asymmetric partition, family-dependent failure and assay censoring. A composite adversarial world combining founder activity, new inactive F1 and precursor reporter production is particularly important. Global fluorescence can remain convincing in that world while the bounded activity decision should fail.

Primary estimands are new-active evidence probability per qualified family/generation; intact-F1 amount retention and recoverable activity retention; randomized parent-state effect on daughter new-active outcome in isolated common garden; and horizontal acquisition rate in unrelated sentinels. Preserve per-generation results rather than averaging away extinction. Report coverage of the founder upper bound and new-activity lower bound against simulated truth. A positive-control success rate is conditional power; null-world acceptance is false-positive rate. Repeated descendants are not independent replicates.

The decision must require identity, activity attribution, verified pedigree and controls jointly. Use a prespecified family-level error allocation or simultaneous uncertainty bounds across generations and components. Calculate Monte Carlo confidence intervals for false-positive and sensitivity estimates; do not call a nominal rate controlled from a small sample. Size root-family counts only after validated LOQ, recovery, partition and leakage estimates exist. Report an N-by-noise sensitivity surface now, with no guaranteed experimental power. If reliable pedigree or usable assays are unavailable, the outcome is insufficient evidence.
