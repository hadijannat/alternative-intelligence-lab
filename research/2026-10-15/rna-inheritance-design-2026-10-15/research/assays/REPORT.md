# Assays for newly generated active F1

Research date: 2026-10-15. These are proposed measurement designs; none was performed experimentally here. Local Salibi source files were read directly, and supporting primary publications were checked online. The source and measurement ledger is `ledger.json`; `verify.py` checks the sequence transcription against the retained SI and captures file hashes.

## Chemical identities that the observer must preserve

Salibi SI Table 1 gives Hyper-A as 52 nt and the 5′-triphosphorylated B as 14 nt. Their ligation produces the 66-nt F1 sequence. Cy5-A is also 52 nt but has G38A and a 5′ Cy5 label; its 66-nt ligation product is the inactive reporter. The mutant and wild-type 52-nt sequences differ at exactly position 38. The complete strings are in `ledger.json`. The 66-nt rt-F and 19-nt FQ-B belong to a different reporter system and must not enter this assay. [Salibi article and SI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-36940-z).

A 66-nt band is insufficient evidence of newly generated active F1. Founder and newly ligated wild-type F1 have the same sequence; inactive but intact wild-type F1 can also have the same mass. Cy5-F1 shares their length. Mutation-specific analysis can identify a competent sequence, never its catalytic state. The SI marks F1's 5′ terminus with a dash despite IVT preparation; do not infer a stable founder/new terminal-phosphate distinction without direct characterization.

## Minimum staged measurement design

**Stage A — establish the observer in bulk.** Prepare independent identity standards for unlabelled wild-type F1, heavy-isotope founder F1, Hyper-A, B, Cy5-A, Cy5-F1 and unlabelled G38A full-length F1. Include an independently assayed inactive wild-type preparation if a reproducible stable inactive state can be produced; otherwise do not pretend that the mutant calibrates all wild-type inactivation. Characterize full length, G38 identity, isotope purity and terminal chemistry before mixing. Use synthetic or IVT isotope-labelled RNA standards, not metabolic growth in the experiment itself. Segmental isotope labelling is established as an RNA preparation strategy, but F1 performance and substrate activation remain unvalidated here. [Segmental labelling study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2978384/).

Use one permanently heavy founder code and light feedstock in the minimum design. A reciprocal label arm tests isotope effects. This identifies founder-derived versus new material; it does not date each new molecule. Generation-specific provenance would require additional separately resolvable isotope codes and residual-feedstock measurements. Alternating two codes alone cannot distinguish a new generation from material retained from an older generation carrying the same code. If coding B instead, verify its 5′ triphosphate and show that heavy B occurs in full-length wild-type F1 rather than unreacted B or mutant reporter. Stable-isotope pulse/chase RNA measurements have distinguished old/new nucleosides, but nucleotide digestion erases parent-molecule identity unless species are isolated first. [NAIL-MS study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7810713/).

Split each sample into three matched aliquots. (1) Analytical denaturing PAGE or validated capillary separation measures 14/52/66-nt material and Cy5 products. (2) Full-length enriched material is analysed by intact LC-HRMS and sequence-informative oligonucleotide mapping, including G38 and a fragment spanning the A–B junction. Establish recovery, isotope-envelope overlap, salt-adduct removal and analytical carryover using mixtures. RNase choice must actually produce an informative junction fragment; a generic complete digest is inadequate. Published LC-MS methods establish oligonucleotide identity feasibility, including mixtures up to 100 nt, not this assay's LOQ or recovery. [Primary LC-MS platform](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2790879/). (3) A separate recovered fraction enters a function assay. MS is destructive and does not provide physically isolated isotope fractions for this assay.

**Stage B — measure catalytic capacity independently.** Recover full-length material and challenge it with fresh Cy5-A and B, omitting fresh Hyper-A. The product of this challenge is inactive, limiting autocatalytic amplification during measurement. Remove or quantify pre-existing Cy5-F1 and use initial product increments above the sample's time-zero signal. Calibrate a dose/rate curve of authentic F1 processed identically. Include Hyper-A-only carryover, reporter-only, G38A-only, blank matrix, founder-only and deliberately contaminated mixtures. Validate a short linear range with negligible substrate depletion and no measurable full-length synthesis from contaminating Hyper-A. Product inhibition or precursor-assisted catalysis can make linear subtraction invalid.

Compare gentle recovery with the denaturing/refolding route. Salibi's analytical recovery uses EDTA, Triton, formamide and heating before urea-PAGE. Activity measured after that route is **refoldable catalytic capacity**, not proof of retained active conformation. Native purification can preserve folds that denaturation changes in other ribozymes, but the literature's engineered affinity constructs do not establish a native fractionation method for this untagged F1 mixture. [Native purification comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2944885/). If gentle separation cannot resolve 52-nt Hyper-A, carryover must remain in the nuisance bound; do not silently assign its activity to F1.

For an additive regime verified by mixture standards, a conservative new-capacity bound is:

`new_capacity_lower = max(0, recovered_capacity_lower − founder_amount_upper × founder_specific_activity_upper − precursor_and_reporter_background_upper)`.

All terms must describe the same processed aliquot and assay units. Recovery correction needs measured species-specific uncertainties. Outside an additive regime use an empirical **nuisance-only mixture upper envelope** at the observed founder/precursor/product concentrations. If no envelope covers the mixture, the result is indeterminate. This supports some newly generated functional catalyst when the bound exceeds the activity LOQ; it does not assign an active state to every newly ligated molecule. Do not transplant Robertson's rapid-quench F1 rate constants: those experiments used 25 mM Mg and different substrate formats, without the Salibi sucrose matrix. [F1 characterization](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3943892/).

A founder-specific affinity-depletion arm may tighten the bound if needed, but it is optional assay development. A founder tag must not be transferred to daughters; its activity, partition, loss, cleavage and capture efficiency need separate calibration. Isotope discrimination alone cannot accomplish this depletion. A no-founder Hyper-A+B arm demonstrates precursor-driven formation of functional F1 but cannot by itself establish seeded regeneration.

**Stage C — retention with generation chemistry stopped.** First load purified F1 without ligatable precursor feedstock and expose it to the intended compartment treatment. Sample inside, outside, every wash and apparatus-associated material before/after treatment. Measure both amount and capacity, including recovery controls added before versus after processing. Use molecule amounts with measured volumes; concentration changes may reflect vesicle volume changes. Report three quantities separately: total RNA retained, catalytic capacity retained, and capacity per recovered molecule. An external RNA digestion control is useful only after proving that the nuclease cannot enter intact compartments and is removed before lysis; freeze-thaw damage can invalidate that assumption.

Then run the complete reaction with the same paired measurements, sampling immediately before exchange/division, immediately after, and after the warm reaction interval. Keep the original POPC/R3C conditions as the primary comparison (50 mM EPPS pH 8.5, 20 mM MgCl2, 900 mM inner sucrose/outer glucose, 42°C), and record any trapping, dilution or matrix change as a distinct arm. Membrane wash recovery is itself an intervention: preferential loss of small or damaged vesicles can bias retention. Include all lost fractions and image compartment recovery.

**Stage D — function in descendants.** In the original protocol, serial feedstock transfer plus freeze-thaw content exchange is a population propagation assay, not a parent–daughter pedigree. To test vertical inheritance, track an isolated parent through an observed division, retain both daughters, record volumes and exclude fusion/contact with other lineages. Reserve independently randomized branches for destructive molecular/function measurements; a lysed daughter cannot then found the next generation. Use parallel pedigrees or a continuing sibling, without treating the sampled sibling as the same lineage member. Run a matched contact/exchange arm separately. A newly active descendant must pass identity/provenance, nuisance-bounded function and compartment-contamination gates. Preserved activity without new synthesis supports retention; generation-dependent regeneration requires new wild-type material and function above the founder/carryover bound.

## Admission gates and detection limits

The quantitative thresholds below are **proposed acceptance targets**, not measured assay performance or universal standards. Freeze them before blinded validation, revise only in a new protocol version, and report failure openly.

* Identity mixtures: correctly recover wild-type/G38A and heavy/light ratios over the actual anticipated range; off-target signal must be below 1% of the smallest biological effect being tested. A single percentage of the abundant founder pool is insufficient when new product is rare.
* Label equivalence: 90% confidence interval for labelled/unlabelled activity and retention ratios inside 0.8–1.25 in matched conditions. Failure requires separate label-response models or a different label; a nonsignificant difference is not equivalence.
* Quantification: matrix-matched dilution series, process blanks, independent spike recovery, both high-to-low carryover challenges and blinded mixtures. Define LOQ as the lowest amount with absolute relative bias and CV each ≤20%; use an empirical blank-based detection threshold. Do not import a nucleoside femtomole LOQ into intact 66-nt F1. Published nucleoside LC-MS illustrates calibrated sensitivity, with a different analyte. [Quantification study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4191383/).
* Function: positive, inactive, mixture and process controls must bound all known nuisance activities. Paired native/refolded capacity must be named separately. A fluorescent label can lower activity in another ligase system, demonstrating why equivalence is required. [DasGupta et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/chem.202301376).
* Mass balance: target recovery of 80–120% for spiked species across collected fractions; unresolved missing mass invalidates the proposed retention estimate. Corrected uncertainty must include extraction and wash losses, not only instrument repeats.
* Pedigree: every counted transition has a recorded parent, both daughter identities, relevant volume measurements and contamination control. Unresolved identity is missing/censored, never successful inheritance. Minimum sample size follows simulation of actual LOQs, capacity variability and batch effects, not image count.

A 10-µm-diameter spherical vesicle has a volume of about 0.524 pL and, at 1 µM, contains only about **0.524 amol = 0.000524 fmol** (approximately 315,000 molecules), before lysis, fractionation and splitting. A 1 µM nominal concentration is therefore not evidence that single-descendant LC-MS is feasible. Even a hypothetical 1-fmol assay LOQ needs at least 1,910 such vesicles with perfect recovery, or about 15,279 with 50% recovery, half the sample assigned to identity analysis, and new material representing 50% of F1. These are arithmetic examples, not a claimed intact-F1 LOQ. Start with ensemble chemistry validation and then ancestry-preserving terminal pools; pooling improves sensitivity but changes the estimand to a pool. The simulation should vary recovered amount and LOQ explicitly and return indeterminate when the measurement budget cannot resolve new capacity.

Skipped wet assays, commercial instrument qualification and founder-tag engineering. Risk: the full identity-plus-function observer, native-state recovery and single-descendant sensitivity remain experimentally unvalidated for this system.
