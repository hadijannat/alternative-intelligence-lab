# How to read the evidence

The question spans several levels. The strength of one level does not settle the next.

| Label | Meaning | Example in this archive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Literature observation | A published physical experiment, within its reported preparation and controls | Mo36 seed-dependent assembly |
| Project calculation | Numerical output from a saved model and declared inputs | 480 electrical encounter cases |
| Conditional support | The specified model behaves as predicted under assumptions | An assigned positive interface attracts the modeled negative solute |
| Engineered demonstration | A supplied design executes a mechanism | Prepared digital learning/copying programs |
| Negative within protocol | A registered criterion was not met in the tested scope | CCO pilot advantage |
| Unresolved | Missing or incompatible evidence prevents a conclusion | Actual Mo36 preservation on FeIII versus silica |

A numerical validation can catch implementation errors without validating chemistry. Independent replay of code is distinct from independent experimental replication. A simulation can reveal requirements, counterexamples and sensitivity without estimating a real evolutionary probability.

## Three questions to ask of every result

1. What was supplied: feedstocks, energy, geometry, program, environment or copying machinery?
2. What was measured or calculated, and what matched control attributes the effect?
3. Which conclusion follows within that scope, and which transition remains untested?

## The registry is an inventory, not a leaderboard of organisms

The 58 groups include noncarbon candidates, carbon-containing alternatives, hybrid systems, conventional references and fabricated devices. Preparation variants share sources and mechanisms. Counting them does not measure the number of possible life forms or a probability of intelligence.

## Read historical artifacts with the status overlay

Archived reports preserve contemporary wording and model versions. Later reports can correct counts, controls or conclusions. The snapshot's [final report](FINAL_RESULTS.md) is the status overlay; original artifacts and hashes remain available through the release manifests.

## Publication boundaries

The archive contains original analysis and project-generated outputs. Acquired literature payloads, private tool/session records and installed dependencies are excluded or separately identified. Paths in publication copies may be sanitized. Compare published hashes with the publication manifest, not an original hash for transformed bytes.
