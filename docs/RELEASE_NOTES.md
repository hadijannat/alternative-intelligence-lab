# Final research snapshot — 13 October 2026

This release publishes the final saved research record for Alternative Intelligence Lab: a scientific synthesis, accessible figures, key machine-readable results, and indexed bulk archives of authored reports, model code, configurations, numerical outputs, negative findings and historical studies.

The curated record contains **23,926 files in 14 archive parts**, totalling 3.04 GB compressed and 6.91 GB reconstructed. The manifest records declared exclusions and every published file's checksum. All 15 recent standalone scientific outputs, 11 key reports/registry files and 1,009 protocol paths were retained. The published CCO group was reconstructed and verified without executing its models.

**Scientific outcome: the full alternative-life and cognition goal remains unmet.** The final chemical programme is blocked at a qualified-input dependency. All integrated G0–G6 acceptance gates remain unresolved.

The canonical evidence registry contains 329 explicit preparations across 58 source-architecture groups. These include carbon controls, hybrids and fabricated devices; they are not 58 demonstrated alternative organisms.

The final Mo36 calculation contains 480 conditional electrostatic encounter cases. Unknown assigned fields can reverse the preference, so neither preformed FeIII nor iron-free silica is established as superior for intact-cluster preservation. Precipitation and clogging were not validated.

The CCO pilot completed 24 trained runs. Its 128-cell architecture achieved 49.67% in-distribution correctness, while a conventional recurrent GNN achieved 100%. The GNN fell to 51.39% on longer-path out-of-distribution queries. The registered CCO advantage was unsupported within this protocol.

Archives retain engineered digital constructions, failed criteria and historical versions. The current status overlay supersedes older “active” wording. Packaging and publication checks establish membership, transcription and byte integrity; no scientific simulations or historical validators were rerun for this release.
