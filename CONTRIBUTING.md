# Contributing

Contributions should make a named scientific question easier to decide. Provide the source or dataset, exact preparation/conditions, provenance and license, a matched baseline, and an explicit scope of inference.

- Label measured, transcribed, inferred, assumed and simulated quantities.
- Preserve negative findings and versioned protocols.
- Supply raw outputs and meaningful error/convergence checks for numerical changes.
- Do not silently change a preregistered criterion after seeing results.
- Keep carbon references, hybrids and inorganic candidates distinct.
- Do not promote autocatalysis, storage or seeded growth into evidence of autonomous life without the necessary causal controls.

Open an issue or pull request with the affected record, the new evidence and the conclusion it could change. A proposed new model should explain why its inputs and domain are qualified and why it is distinct from the completed calculations.

Scientific disagreement is welcome. Describe uncertainty precisely, attribute others' work and communicate respectfully.
